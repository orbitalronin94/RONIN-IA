Over the next few years many companies will attempt to enter the
manned space business
Most of these will include an abort system to escape a launch
failure
Much work has been done on demonstrating abort capability
– Flight dynamics
– Stability
– Aerodynamics
– Acceleration
During aerodynamic flight, especially maximum dynamic pressure,
the the abort will be initiated under preloaded strain
Lateral dynamics of separation during max-q have normally been
neglected as a point of interest
This presentation examines the lateral separation dynamics for
various configurations

The Vehicles
LaRC Structural and Thermal Systems Branch

•
•
•

•

This study looked at three vehicle concepts
– 50 ft, 30 ft, and 20 ft axial length
The models are all beam models with cylindrical cross sections
The dimensions of the beams mimic the basic concepts being
worked within the Commercial Crew Program at NASA
– 50 ft approximates a puller system
– 30 ft approximates lifting body
– 20 ft approximates a capsule
These models do not represent exact systems, but are used to
show the potential responses of such systems

Methodology
•
•
•
•
•
•

•

LaRC Structural and Thermal Systems Branch

Multi-body approach (Henkel-Mar)
Abort vehicle attached to “stay behind vehicle” rigidly in 6-dof
Launch vehicle engines accelerating stack
Aerodynamic forces applied axially and laterally
Control force applied to aft end of LV to stabilize system
At abort:
– LV engines keep firing
– Aerodynamic forces remain constant
– Connections allowed to separate (don’t actually separate until abort
vehicle T/W>1)
– Abort motor starts and rises in about 200 ms
– No abort vehicle control forces are applied
Nominal Case (from which sensitivity is established):
– Q-alpha = 2000 psf, thrust angle = 0, no shear pins

Conclusions
LaRC Structural and Thermal Systems Branch

•

With a built-in c.g. offset, motor angle is the most important
parameter for controlling abort accelerations
– Consistent with Armand presentation SCLV 2011

•

Separation twang will occur and cannot be neglected
– Twang responses are minimized by placing the crew near the c.g.
– Twang responses are reduced by reducing q-alpha
