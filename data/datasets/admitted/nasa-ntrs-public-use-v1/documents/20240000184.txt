<p>SLS FM Risk Assessment 
</p>
<p>Background
</p>
<p>SLS Fault Management (FM) function set assesses and responds 
</p>
<p>to off-nominal conditions
</p>
<p>❑ Objectives - Crew Safety and Mission Success
</p>
<p>◼ Annunciating Conditions to flight crew and mission support 
</p>
<p>personnel (Cautions and Warnings)
</p>
<p>◼ Performing predetermined operational responses (Aborts, 
</p>
<p>Redundancy Management and Safing Responses)
</p>
<p>FM Risk Assessment
</p>
<p>❑ False Positive (FP) – FM initiating an unwarranted response
</p>
<p>❑ False Negative (FN) – FM not responding when needed
</p>

<p></p>

<p>Background (continued 2/3)
</p>
<p>FM function
</p>
<p>❑ Detection – monitor for specific  off-nominal conditions
</p>
<p>❑ Response - transmit and perform the response actions
</p>
<p>FM Detection Function
</p>
<p>❑ Threshold-Based Assessment
</p>
<p>◼ Applicable mainly to functions that monitor continuous state 
</p>
<p>variables.
</p>
<p>◼ Limits, Persistence, Timing
</p>
<p>◼ Requires physics-based modeling and simulations 
</p>
<p>❑ Non-Threshold-Based Assessment
</p>
<p>◼ Detection system reliability
</p>

<p>Analysis based on 
</p>
<p>❑ SLS Block 1 crewed-vehicle design – includes the Solid Rocket 
</p>

</p>
<p>II mission.

</p>
<p>❑ External failures from the Orion, Interim Cryogenic Propulsion 
</p>

</p>
<p>Flight Rules)

</p>
<p>mechanism.

<p>Firmware Modeling
</p>
<p>❑ Firmware is embedded code within a hardware component that is 
</p>
<p>executed.
</p>
<p>❑ The firmware risk estimation is modeled the same as software.
</p>
<p>❑ Distinct from Field-Programable Gate Arrays (FPGAs) which are 
</p>
<p>treated as hardware.
</p>
<p>❑ Only three (3) avionics boxes identified containing firmware in 
</p>
<p>the vehicle

<p>Summary &amp; Conclusion
</p>
<p>❑ Implementation of the analysis refinements 
</p>
<p>◼ Provided more reasonable comparison of software and hardware risks.
</p>
<p>◼ Enabled risk assessments of the individual detection functions, while 
</p>
<p>allowing assessment of the entire detection suite minimizing over-
</p>
<p>estimation.
</p>
<p>❑ For Artemis II risk assessment, completed
</p>
<p>◼ All Abort Detection Functions FP/FN
</p>
<p>◼ Most Safing Detection Functions FP/FN
</p>
<p>◼ Warning Detection Functions FP only
</p>
<p>❑ Results supported
</p>
<p>◼ Artemis II SLS Design Verification Objectives (DVOs)
</p>
<p>◼ Estimation of Artemis II overall LOC and LOM risks</p>
