•Ytterbium was chosen due its higher atomic mass and lower valence in comparison to Zr4+


•Produces lower thermal conductivities


•Higher sintering resistance at high temperatures than YSZ 


•Stabilization of tetragonal zirconia formation

Summary





Aluminosilicate aerogels


•Aluminosilicate aerogels can be used up to 1100oC. 


•Their composites are not flakey and can be flexible. 


•The aerogel and their composites can be modified to be hydrophobic.





Yttria and/or Ytterbia stabilized Zirconia aerogels


•The surface areas of the as dried aerogels depend on the total dopant level and water ratio, but no relationship with Y to Yb ratio.


•With increase dopant level, the pore size increases up to 20% dopant, and surface area decreases with the increased dopant.


•Dopant level and the choice of dopant can affect the phase change and crystalline size growth


•Increasing dopant above 20% can avoid the phase transformation and suppress the crystalline growth at 1100oC


•SiO2 coating can effectively suppress the crystalline growth and phase change.
