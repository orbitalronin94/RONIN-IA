<p>C2Po1D-06:  Modeling of Condensation in 
the Presence of Noncondensables in GFSSP
</p>
<p>Michael Baldwin1,2, S. Mostafa Ghiaasiaan2, Alok Majumdar1, and Andre LeClair1
</p>
<p>1: NASA Marshall Space Flight Center, Huntsville, AL  2: Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA
</p>
<p>A reliable condensation model accurately predicts condensation rates.
</p>
<p>When a noncondensable gas (often He) is present, modeling of
temperature and concentration gradients is more difficult, and
uncertainty in condensation rates is increased.
</p>
<p>Aerospace applications of interest include:
1. Tank pressurization processes using He
</p>
<p>(Ullage collapse considerations)
2. In-situ resource utilization (ISRU)
3. Moisture removal (on board ISS, non-cryogenic)
</p>
<p>MOTIVATION
</p>
<p>1) The Couette flow film (stagnant film) model has been developed and coded
for GFSSP
</p>
<p>2) The GFSSP model performs well when compared to the two air-steam
experimental data sets analyzed
</p>
