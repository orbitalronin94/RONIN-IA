• HyperSolve is a three-dimensional finite-volume solver being used to investigate 
the efficacy of hyperbolic methods on unstructured meshes



• HyperSolve CFD Solver Capabilities
• 2nd/3rd-order edge-based, finite-volume discretizations
• In-core mesh adaptation for multiblock structured grids and unstructured meshes
• Thermally perfect gas and reacting flow in thermal nonequilibrium
• Spalart-Allmaras RANS turbulence model variants
• Jacobian-Free Newton-Krylov nonlinear solver2 with nonlinear preconditioning3



• Adjoint solver capability



• Core mission of HyperSolve to is to provide accurate surface heat transfer 
predictions for NASA Entry vehicles, with a focus on unstructured mesh 
adaptation

HyperSolve-LAURA Functional Equivalence



• Reacting flow capability in HyperSolve ported from LAURA
• Chemical kinetic models, transport property models, and gas thermodynamic models implemented in 



HyperSolve have been verified to match models in the LAURA code



• Functional Equivalence Tests (FETs) provide assessment on spatial accuracy
• LAURA results used as reference (truth) solutions
• HyperSolve should match LAURA solution heat flux and pressure within 4% tolerance, when both 



solvers are run on the same structured mesh



Functional Equivalence: To be useful as an aerothermal 
analysis tool, HyperSolve should have similar accuracy to 
LAURA on an identical structured mesh

Summary & Future Work



• Using an identical structured mesh, HyperSolve surface heat flux 
and pressure predictions match LAURA to within a 4% difference



• HyperSolve reacting flow gas capabilities demonstrate comparable 
shock capturing and stagnation line species composition to LAURA



• Mars 2020 validation: further investigation needed to understand differences.



• Future work will include extending the Functional Equivalence 
Tests to include cases with significant thermal nonequilibrium and 
ionization reactions



• Verification and validation of the vibrational energy relaxation capability in 
HyperSolve is needed
