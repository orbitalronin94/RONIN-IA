Today’s Semiconductor Bandgap Engineering

• Currently, major semiconductor alloy epitaxial growth is divided into two 
material groups.

• Cubic: 
• Diamond structures:   Group IV semiconductors (Si, Ge, C)

• Cubic zinc-blende structures:   

- Group III-V semiconductors (GaAs, InP) 

- Group II-VI semiconductors (ZnSe, CdTe) 

• Hexagonal:
• Wurtzite structures: III-Nitride semiconductors (GaN, AlN, InN)

• II-VI semiconductor:  ZnO 

• Hexagonal SiC (2H, 4H)

• The mixture of different crystal structures was thought to be very 
difficult. We developed a new growth technology of “Super-Hetero 
Epitaxy” with SiGeC alloy in which each layer can have different 
materials and different crystal lattice structures.

Characteristics of Semiconductor Materials
as They Affect Device Speed

• Silicon: 
 Short gate length with very stable oxide (SiO2), [shortest gate-length = 5nm]
 Reliable nano-fabrication to fabricate VLSI* chips (CPU, memory, logic controller, graphics chips 

with large scale integration)
– Charge carrier mobility: 1400 cm2/V·s at Ndonor = 1016 cm-3

• III-V Compounds: GaAs, InP, InSb, and more
 High electron mobility, GaAs: 4000 cm2/V·s at Ndonor = 1016 cm-3
– Lack of stable oxide insulator: longer gate length
– Low density integration for discrete microwave devices and laser diodes
– Not compatible with Silicon CMOS technology

• SiGe: 
 Charge mobility is greater than 3000 cm2/V·s between Si and GaAs
 Stable gate-oxide with SiO2 : short gate length is possible
 Compatible with Silicon CMOS microelectronics: VLSI*

+To date, Lattice-matched SiGe condition difficult to attain, but NASA Langley achieved it!!
+No high frequency parasitic capacitance

Advantages of Rhombohedral SiGe PV Cell

Based on our own estimation, the efficiency of the proposed PV cells could reach 30~40% level 
which is far exceeds the best currently available PV cells.  This estimation is based on the following 
categories: 

• High charge mobility (2000~4000 cm2/V·sec) of rhombohedral single crystal SiGe that is at least two or 
four times higher than single crystal Si (1000 cm2/V·sec).

• Hole and electron quantum-well structures for super highway of charge motion (we already built) 
that is able to enhance carrier transport at least 10 times as compared to bulk Si material and 
drastically reduces the internal losses due to resistivity.

• No shadow loss by the occupation of cell-matrix circuits on the cell plane. The conventional PV cells 
suffers 10~15% loss of exposed area owing to the cell-matrix circuits on top surface.

• The combined bandgap structures of Si and Ge cover the spectral range of sun light broader than that of 
single crystal Si.

• A compact cell-matrix circuit can be built to reduce the electron migration gap between electrodes, thus 
reducing the resistivity attributed from electron scattering.

• Sapphire substrate which faces outside is 10 times stronger and harder material than Si. Life of QW SiGe 
PV cell at least 5 times longer than Si-based PV cell.

• No protective coatings or special frames needed.

Summary

• Rhombohedral super-hetero crystal epitaxy technology was invented. The world’s first 
triangular crystal-plane epitaxy technology can combine cubic semiconductors with 
trigonal crystals.

• Germanium-rich single crystal SiGe layers on c-Sapphire can be fabricated with high 
reliability (>99.9% single-crystal).

• Technology has been patented:  US Patents: 8,257,491;  8,826,767;  7,906,358;  
7,769,135;  7,558,371; 7,514,726; 7,341,883; 8,044,294; 9,449,818; 9,455,374,  
Pending: SN: 14/484,517; SN: 15/386,592.

• Super growth chamber was designed and built to fabricate highly sophisticated 
quantum well solar cells and devices.

• Characterization shows single-crystalline SiGe layers on c-Sapphire have some 
residual defects. Surface morphologies are being improved with the reduction of RMS 
roughness. Quality is being improved with the reduction of residual defects.

• Technology expansion to III-V, III-Nitride, and II-VI is underway. 37
