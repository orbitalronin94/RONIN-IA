<p>Basic Conops
</p>
<p>• When an Astrobee is idle, it charges in its dock
• Astrobees can execute complex plans with full autonomy 
</p>
<p>and no astronauts present
• Including undock, traverse multiple modules, return to dock
</p>
<p>• However, Astrobees run with ground operator oversight
• When an anomaly occurs, an Astrobee generally stops and 
</p>
<p>waits for operator intervention
• It can continue operating during communication outages until 
</p>
<p>it encounters an anomaly
• The operator can always take over and teleoperate
• Astronauts can also be operators, but this is will likely be a 
</p>
<p>rare occurance (minimize crew time)
</p>
