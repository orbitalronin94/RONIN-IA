</div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Background</p>
<p></p>
<p>Beginning in 2032, SLS Boosters will make the transition from PBAN to HTPB</p>
<p></p>
<p>This change could have a large impact on the HTPB polymer market both in price and specifications</p>
<p></p>
<p>First flight of BOLE is scheduled for 2032, however between 2023 and 2029 seven motor loadings are planned </p>
<p></p>
<p>NASA also utilizes HTPB motors for Booster Separation and Launch Abort for each SLS flight</p>
<p>Commercial Crew also uses HTPB boosters (AJ60 and GEM 63) and will transition to GEM63XL</p>
<p></p>
<p>HTPB usage across the large solid rocket motor industry is projected to significantly grow during this time period due to new programs – Vulcan(GEM63XL), Sentinel, GBI, BOLE</p>
</div>

<div class="slide-content"><p>HTPB Polymer Usage in NASA and Space Force Flights</p>
<p></p>
<p>BOLE igniter will use BSM HTPB propellant</p>
<p>Quantities insignificant for both systems at two igniters (~50 lbs) and ten BSM’s (~150 lbs) per flight set</p>
<p></p>
<p>Launch Abort System for Orion utilized HTPB in the Jettison Motor and the Abort Motor</p>
<p>Estimate 100 lbs of polymer per Jettison Motor</p>
<p>Estimate 560 lbs of polymer per Abort Motor</p>
<p></p>
<p>Commercial Crew will be using GEM63</p>
<p>Estimate up to 19,500 lbs of polymer per launch (based on two boosters)</p>
<p>	</p>
<p>Space Force will be using Vulcan (GEM63XL)</p>
<p>Estimate up to 60,000 lbs of polymer per launch (based on six boosters)</p>
<p></p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Summary</p>
<p>SLS Booster will purchase R45M  per commercial specification at 150klbs per year beginning in 2022, increasing to 300 klbs per year in 2028</p>
<p></p>
<p>NG is carrying a risk for R45M lot variability</p>
<p>Will be assessed as we move forward</p>
<p></p>
<p>Total SLS, Commercial Crew and Space Force motors using HTPB is significant and a driving case for production</p>
<p>One Flight of SLS uses 300,643 lbs of HTPB</p>
<p>One Flight of Commercial Crew uses 19,500 lbs of HTPB</p>
<p>One Flight of Vulcan can use up to 60,000 pbs of HTPB</p>
<p></p>
