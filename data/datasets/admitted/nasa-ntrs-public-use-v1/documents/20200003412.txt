Summary
• The decline in aerosol backscatter (2006-2008 vs. 2014-2016) as observed by CALIPSO is not
clearly present in the MERRA2-GMI simulations.
• Nitrate is a dominant component of ATAL in both MERRA2-GMI and GEOS-Chem.
• Lightning NOx emissions significantly contribute to ATAL AOD (~10% on average).
• Higher model resolution could be important for better simulation of vertical transport associated
with ATAL.

Future work
• Perturbation simulations to determine the relative contributions of China/India emissions.
• Analyze transport tracers (CO, Rn-222, Pb-210) in the model to examine the role of ENSO in
2015 vs 2014.
• Conduct GEOS-5 simulations in both 2x2.5 and 0.5x0.667 resolutions to study the impacts of
model resolutions on vertical transport in ATAL.
