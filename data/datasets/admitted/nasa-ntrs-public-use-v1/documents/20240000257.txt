<div class="slide-content"><p>Nascent in-space servicing technology in 2014 inspired NASA to add rendezvous aides to the Magnetospheric Multiscale Mission (MMS)</p>
<p>Basic open questions: </p>
<p>What should be printed on the sticker?</p>
<p>How many stickers are needed?</p>
<p>Where should they be applied?</p>
<p>What information would a prospective servicer need?</p>
<p>Subsequent efforts to add rendezvous and capture aides have been similarly ad hoc </p>
<p>New question:</p>
<p>Are aides worth the current investment for a potential future savings?</p>
<p>How much is servicer performance improved?</p>
<p>Two recent missions have afforded a more systematic approach</p>
<p>James Webb Space Telescope and Roman Space Telescope</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Generated OpenCV images along an approach corridor with 6DOF dispersions (ideal lighting and camera)</p>
<p>Range from 30m to 2m, 5 deg about boresight, 4 deg about anti-boresight</p>
<p>Seeking the fiducial size and placement option that results in the largest range of servicer camera FOV while meeting pose performance metric</p>
<p>Passing performance requirements are from the unprepared OSAM-1 servicing mission</p>
<p>Assumed a single servicer camera for simplicity and conservatism</p>

<p>Rendezvous Fiducials – February 7, 2024</p>
<div class="slide-content"><div class="embedded" id="slide11_rId2"></div>
<p>Trends revealed</p>
<p>Larger fiducial size increases detectability and accuracy </p>
<p>Narrower camera FOV increases accuracy and long-range detectability</p>
<p>Narrow camera FOV at close range reduces detectability since the fiducials can be out of the camera view</p>
<p>Study recommendation: 64mm (100mm outer dimension) fiducial with the largest spatial distribution (scenario 4)</p>
<p>Allows greatest flexibility for the future servicer since the camera FOV can range from ~5-15 deg</p>
<p>Intuitive results indicate a simple geometric analysis would be adequate</p>

<p>Rendezvous Fiducials – February 7, 2024</p>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Pragmatic process has been defined for vehicle designers to incorporate low complexity/effort visual fiducials</p>
<p>Applicable to early and late adopters</p>
<p>Early adopters have greater flexibility which benefits the future servicer without additional effort compared to the late adopters</p>
<p>Photogrammetry approach to determine as-installed fiducial locations was determined to be feasible; more work to go to fully validate the process </p>
<p>Updated photo documentation instructions are available from NEXiS</p>
<p>Roman study demonstrated that as few as 5 fiducials can be used to meet comparable unprepared rendezvous system performance requirements</p>
<p>Future iterations can employ a simple geometric analysis in CAD rather than a machine vision simulation</p>
