Rendezvous Fiducials – February 7, 2024






Trends revealed


Larger fiducial size increases detectability and accuracy 


Narrower camera FOV increases accuracy and long-range detectability


Narrow camera FOV at close range reduces detectability since the fiducials can be out of the camera view


Study recommendation: 64mm (100mm outer dimension) fiducial with the largest spatial distribution (scenario 4)


Allows greatest flexibility for the future servicer since the camera FOV can range from ~5-15 deg


Intuitive results indicate a simple geometric analysis would be adequate

Rendezvous Fiducials – February 7, 2024



Pragmatic process has been defined for vehicle designers to incorporate low complexity/effort visual fiducials


Applicable to early and late adopters


Early adopters have greater flexibility which benefits the future servicer without additional effort compared to the late adopters


Photogrammetry approach to determine as-installed fiducial locations was determined to be feasible; more work to go to fully validate the process 


Updated photo documentation instructions are available from NEXiS


Roman study demonstrated that as few as 5 fiducials can be used to meet comparable unprepared rendezvous system performance requirements


Future iterations can employ a simple geometric analysis in CAD rather than a machine vision simulation
