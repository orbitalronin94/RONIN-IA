<p>Orion TPS DFI (Developmental Flight 
Instrumentation)  
</p>
<p>• Objective - deliver Artemis 1, 2, 3 + instrumentation hardware (heatshield and 
backshell) for measuring TPS performance, aerothermal environments, vehicle 
wind-relative orientation, and atmospheric density during the atmospheric entry of 
the Crew Module (CM). 
</p>
<p>• Both heatshield as well as the backshell have numerous embedded sensors that 
include – Thermal plugs, Flush Air Data System (FADS), shoulder pressure 
transducers,  Radiometer, Spectrometer and Optical recession sensors
</p>

<p>Orion Aerosciences
• CFD is used to develop environments for Aerodynamics and Aerothermodynamics for all phases of flight
</p>
<p>• CFD tools are validated utilizing ground and flight test data before applying it in design analyses
</p>
<p>• Key challenges for CFD in Orion Aerosciences
</p>
<p>– Aero: Complex geometries, turbulence, wake flows, plume flows, fluid-structure interaction (parachutes)
</p>
<p>– Aerothermal: Complex geometries, turbulence, wake flows, plume flows, gas-surface chemical interaction, radiation
</p>
<p>Reference : https://www.nas.nasa.gov/pubs/ams/2022/04-07-22.html
Orion Aerosciences: Aerothermal Database Development, Ryan McDaniel</p>
