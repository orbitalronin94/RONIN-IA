<p>Summary</p>
<p>Mars missions will require significantly more energy and much longer system service life, with more constrained departure windows, than current and lunar missions</p>
<p>Shorter total roundtrip duration missions could reduce crew health and performance concerns, but pushes the challenge to the transportation system</p>
<p>Four In-Space Transportation propulsion technologies are currently under consideration </p>
<p>Each can be optimized for their portion of the trade space</p>
<p>“Best” Transportation option will be dependent on many factors that are still being considered</p>
