<p>Navigation Doppler Lidar Enables Moon to Mars
</p>
<p>Navigation Doppler Lidar (NDL) 
• Frequency-modulated continuous wave (FMCW) 
</p>
<p>lidar system
• 1550 nm, fiber-optic system
</p>
<p>NDL measures spacecraft attitude, velocity, 
and altitude during descent
</p>
<p>• &lt;1 cm/s velocity resolution, ~10 cm range 
resolution
</p>
<p>• &gt;5 km operational range
Precise measurements enable reduced 
</p>
<p>landing uncertainty
Small size allows for redundancy, supports 
</p>
<p>small landers
2 NDL units flying to the moon in 2023
