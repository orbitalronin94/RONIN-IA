Navigation Doppler Lidar Enables Moon to Mars



Navigation Doppler Lidar (NDL) 
• Frequency-modulated continuous wave (FMCW) 



lidar system
• 1550 nm, fiber-optic system



NDL measures spacecraft attitude, velocity, 
and altitude during descent



• <1 cm/s velocity resolution, ~10 cm range 
resolution



• >5 km operational range
Precise measurements enable reduced 



landing uncertainty
Small size allows for redundancy, supports 



small landers
2 NDL units flying to the moon in 2023
