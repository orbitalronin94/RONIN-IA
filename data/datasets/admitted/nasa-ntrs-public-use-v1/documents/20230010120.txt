<p>Motivation
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- In supersonic flow, the presence of water vapor can impact data quality 
(if not characterized)
- “Condensation shocks”
- Mach number &amp; total pressure decrease
- Static pressure &amp; total temperature increase
</p>
<p>- Mike Mills (AEDC) and Max Amaya (NASA ARC) have presented on this 
topic multiple times 
</p>
<p>- As humidity-assessment instruments and methods for accounting for 
humidity in our tunnels were compared amongst several WTCWG 
members, it’s become apparent that there are subtle differences to 
discuss. </p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>9x15 LSWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Three (3) Vaisala (DMT143) Miniature Dewpoint Transmitters
- 10%-90% RH recommended for acoustic research objectives 
- Dewpoint requirements are different for each engine test entry 
</p>
<p>Test Section 
Humidity Rake
</p>
<p>Flow</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>8x6 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Dewpoint sample acquired in 
settling chamber
</p>
<p>- Total pressure in tunnel drives 
sample flow 
</p>
<p>DP Sample Location</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>8x6 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Approximately &lt; 50 feet of ¼” tubing between sample and sensor
</p>
<p>- Two (2) Vaisala DMT143 
- Supposed to be insensitive to flow rate
- Anecdotally, dew point values decrease rapidly when the compressor 
</p>
<p>starts up, even at relatively low Mach numbers 
</p>
<p>- No pressure or flow rate measurements made on sample line @ 
hygrometer 
- Plan to recommend both measurements to be added as potential 
</p>
<p>improvements, but not necessary.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>8x6 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Historical rule of thumb: dew point &lt; -15oF
- Acceptable at all Mach numbers
</p>
<p>- 55oF of supercooling 
- Depending on the situation, low dew points 
</p>
<p>may not be achievable for long at highest 
speeds, default to -15oF rule.
</p>
<p>- Dry air typically turned “on” at Mach 1.4, 
but this can be delayed until data needs to 
be acquired or model visibility diminishes</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>10x10 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- In 10x10 SWT, dewpoint 
measurement critical to 
calibration model.
</p>
<p>- Sample acquired near tunnel 
air heater in settling chamber. </p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>10x10 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Larger diameter pipe (~2”) brings 
air sample from tunnel air heater 
station in settling chamber to 
reservoir tank (“air chamber”) 
- Air re-enters tunnel near end of 
</p>
<p>contraction, upstream of flexwall.
- Approx. 50-75 feet of pipe/tubing 
</p>
<p>between sample and hygrometers
- Small sample lines (~1/8”) 
</p>
<p>transport sample from reservoir 
- Sample either pushed via tunnel 
</p>
<p>pressure or pulled w/ vacuum pumps
- 0.5 L/min flow rate set</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>10x10 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Two (2) Stephens Analytical 
MicroView On-Line Hygrometers 
- Calibrated -60 to 0oC 
</p>
<p>- GE Optica Hygrometer used as a 
check on the other two units.
</p>
<p>- Dewpoint pressure-corrected to 
barometric pressure measurement
- Local total pressure measured at 
</p>
<p>each hygrometer 
</p>
<p>- Rule of thumb for testing: 
pressure-corrected dewpt &lt; -15oF  </p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>10x10 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Question: How accurate do we need our hygrometer measurement to be?
- 8x6 and 9x15 are using Vaisala’s and are happy with their simplicity, 
</p>
<p>reliability, and ease of use. 
- Would it make sense for 10x10 to adopt these units, as well?
</p>
<p>- We can assess what an error in dewpoint measurement would do to the 
calibrated Mach number in 10x10 using existing calibration relationships.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>10x10 SWT
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>At -15oF &amp; Mach 3 in 10x10 SWT:
- Stephens Analytical 
</p>
<p>Hygrometer 
- 2σ ~= 1.2oC = 2.16oF 
- ~0.0012 Mach error
</p>
<p>- A Vaisala dewpoint sensor 
- +/- 2.0oC = 3.6oF 
- ~0.0020 Mach error
</p>
<p>The difference in calculated 
Mach number seems small but 
will need to be compared to 
other contribution sources.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Effect of SVP Model Choice
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- There are several choices of Saturation Vapor Pressure (SVP) model 
which we can use when computing the humidity levels.
- Goff-Gratch (1945)
- Hyland &amp; Wexler (1983)
- Buck (1996)
- Enhanced Buck (1981), etc.
</p>
<p>- Does it matter which one we choose?
</p>
<p>- Using simplified 1-D analysis1 with released latent heat of condensation2
</p>
<p>under expected 10x10 SWT conditions:
</p>
<p>1 Effects of Air Humidity in Supersonic Wind Tunnels, J. Lukasiewicz &amp; J. K. Royle - ARC 2563 (1953)
2 Estimating Water Vapor Condensation Effects for Transonic and Supersonic Flow Fields, Robinson C. E. &amp; Bauer R. C. (1985)</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Effect of SVP Model Choice
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>At -15oF
- Mach number delta if using Buck 
</p>
<p>Enhanced vs. Goff-Gratch: 
- ~0.0003 Mach (Re = 0.5e6/ft)</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Effect of SVP Model Choice
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>At -15oF
- Mach number delta if using Buck 
</p>
<p>Enhanced vs. Goff-Gratch: 
- ~0.0003 Mach (Re = 0.5e6/ft)
</p>
<p>- Freestream total pressure delta:
- ~0.004 psi (Re = 2.5e6/ft)  
</p>
<p>Seemingly insignificant difference in 
Mach number and total pressure 
(depends on the user/facility), would 
suggest using Buck Enhanced (1981):
- Relatively modern
- Enhancement factors to account for 
</p>
<p>pressure levels 
- Definitely debatable, open to suggestions!</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Conclusions &amp; Future Work
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Choice of SVP Model doesn’t make a huge difference if properly used 
(i.e., frost-point vs. dewpoint, enhancement factors used properly, etc.)
</p>
<p>- Future work:
- Add PT &amp; flow rate sensors to 8x6 SWT Vaisala lines
</p>
<p>- Add calculations for pressure-corrected dewpoint in 8x6 &amp; 10x10 SWT 
(corrected to tunnel total pressure &amp; corrected to atmosphere)
</p>
<p>- Perform MUA following 10x10 full characterization (2025) to 
determine whether complex dewpoint system could be simplified.</p>
