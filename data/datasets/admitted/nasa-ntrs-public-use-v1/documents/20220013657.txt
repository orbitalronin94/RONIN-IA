Motivation
• Designing high-pressure turbines (HPTs) for peak temperatures at the 

combustor exit è More cooling air èLess cycle efficiency 
• Designing HPTs for the mean exit-temperature at the combustor exit è More 

local hot spots (hot streaks) è Less gas turbine durability
• Component interactions (such as combustor-turbine) more important in 

compact high-OPR future engines 
• Advance CFD tools to enable coupled analysis and provide design guidelines 

1. Roux et al. [1] showed that the inclusion of the high-pressure turbine strongly affected the amplitude and 
frequency of the dynamics (i.e., the strongest mode) of the combustor. 

2. Griffini et al. [2]used RANS to investigate the clocking effect and showed that clocking significantly influenced 
coolant mass flow rate. 

3. Hilgert et al. [3] numerically investigated the Large Scale Turbine Rig (LSTR) and showed the heat transfer 
distribution at hub end wall is greatly influenced by the clocking effect. 

4. Insinna et al. [4] investigated the conjugate heat transfer of the MT1 research high-pressure stage, imposing 
the realistic turbine combustor exit using RANS.

5. The EU's Full Aerothermal Combustor-Turbine interactions Research project built a large-scale rig (FACTOR) 
for a better understanding of aero-thermal fields between the combustor and the HPT(e.g., [5]-[9])

profile migration in the HPT use a simplified combustor assumption.

Features of Open National Combustion Code 
(OpenNCC)

• OpenNCC is the releasable version of the 
National Combustion Code (NCC), which has 
been continuously updated for more than two 
decades at NASA Glenn Research Center (GRC)

• Main Features
ü Numerics:  Jameson-Schmidt-Turkel (JST) 

scheme, Roe's upwind scheme, and AUSM [11]

ü Turbulence: Cubic non-linear k-ε model with the 
wall function, Low-Re model, local dynamic            
k-model (LDKM) [12]

ü Combustion: Finite Rate Chemistry, EBU [12] , PDF 
[13] , Linear Eddy Model (LEM) [14], Dynamic
Thickened Flame Model (DTF) [15]

ü Spray:  Lagrangian liquid phase model [16]

ü Other features: Low-Mach preconditioning, 
transition model, unstructured mesh, adaptive 
mesh refinement (AMR), moving mesh, massively 
parallel computing

We proposed the sequential approach to investigated the Combustor-Turbine 
Interactions using the E3 geometries and tested two different operating conditions..  

Main Funding in This Study

ü It is shown that inclusion of HPT as the combustor exit boundary alters the migration 
of hot-streaks.

ü Hot-streak distributions among three Steps differ from each other. However, The 
results at both the low- and high-pressure conditions look similar. 

ü We demonstrated that Step 3 was capable of capturing the dynamic behavior and 
confirmed that modeling both the combustor and HPT may enhance the oscillation 
through the temperature fluctuations.

Conclusion and Future Work

Future Work

HPT and to use the “source term approach”, in
which we specify the source term (velocity,
mass flow rate, injection angle, etc.) at the grid
surface of HPT without “meshing” of hundreds
of holes.
