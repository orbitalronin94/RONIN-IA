Xray Density Gradient 



August 11, 2023 12



• Need cube method to progress to 
Xray method



• More continuous density profile 
than cube method



• Fraction of the time it takes to 
machine cubes



• Easier to view boundary defects











AETB Billet
• Untrimmed– 705 g 
• Untrimmed to Trimmed Billet – 488 g, 31% initial weight loss
• Trimmed to Cubed Billet- 315 g, 35% weight loss
• 55% total weight loss, majority from denser, external skin region



• Excessive material waste
• Reduces test volume



• More discrete density gradient characterization
• Removes skin region information



• Skin Region
• Majority of Billet Defects
• Densest region
• Must be removed for billet usage
• X-ray method allows us to characterize it
