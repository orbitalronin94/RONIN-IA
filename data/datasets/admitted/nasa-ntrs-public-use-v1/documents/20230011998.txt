<p>Alumina Enhanced Thermal Barrier (AETB)
</p>
<p>August 11, 2023 3
</p>
<p>•AETB-8, -12, -17, -20 (lbs/ft^3)
•Low-density, ceramic tile
•Fibrous thermal insulator
•Composition
</p>
<p>•Silica fibers
•Aluminumborosilicate fibers
•Alumina fibers
•Silicon Carbide 
</p>
<p>•Best dimensional stability of the 
HTRM tile materials
•The golden standard
</p>
<p>•Arcjet testing
•Companies interested 
•Best mechanical strength to thermal 
insulation tradeoffs
</p>
<p>•Use up to ~2800 ℉ (single use)</p>
<p></p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Process
</p>
<p>August 11, 2023 5
</p>
<p>1. New batch of ingredients 2. Mix into wet slurry 3. Chop in V-blender
</p>
<p>4. Pour into casting tower 5. Compress and vacuum out 
water into wet billet
</p>
<p>6. Dry, then fire, sintering the 
fibers</p>

<p>Xray Density Gradient 
</p>
<p>August 11, 2023 12

<p>• Need cube method to progress to 
Xray method

than cube method

machine cubes
</p>

<p>AETB Billet
• Untrimmed– 705 g 
• Untrimmed to Trimmed Billet – 488 g, 31% initial weight loss
• Trimmed to Cubed Billet- 315 g, 35% weight loss
• 55% total weight loss, majority from denser, external skin region
</p>
<p>• Excessive material waste
• Reduces test volume
</p>
<p>• More discrete density gradient characterization
• Removes skin region information
</p>
<p>• Skin Region
• Majority of Billet Defects
• Densest region
• Must be removed for billet usage
• X-ray method allows us to characterize it
</p>
