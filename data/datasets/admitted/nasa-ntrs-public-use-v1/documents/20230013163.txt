organic liquid electrolytes
</p>

temperature range

non-flammable components Tesla Li-Ion Battery Fire

 “The primary barrier to electric aviation is battery 
</p>
<p>performance”
 Aerospace batteries require:  safety, high energy density, 
</p>
<p>fast discharge rate, light weight packaging, and scalability

</p>

extracted from fossil fuels

converts to sulfur dioxide, the

</p>
<p>tonnes)

<p>Components of A Solid-State Lithium Sulfur Battery
</p>
<p>“Fuel” - Electrochemical energy source:
 Cathode – Sulfur
</p>
<p> Insulating to both Li and electrons
 A sustainable choice
 Need additives (“composite cathode”)
</p>
<p> Carbon: electron conductor
 Solid electrolyte: Li ion conductor
 Binder: mechanical strength
</p>
<p> Anode – Lithium
 Conductive to both Li and electrons
 Could be directly used but may need 
</p>
<p>engineering to perform better
</p>
<p>Solid electrolyte: 
 Nonflammable
 Li ion conducting (but electron insulator)
 Uses: (1) separates cathode and anode 
</p>
<p>(2) Li ion conductive additive for cathode
</p>
<p>Other components:
 Current collectors
 Bipolar plates (separate solid-state unit cells)

<p>Bi-Polar Stack Solid-State Battery
</p>
<p>SSE-enabled bi-polar stack design minimizes safety containment in packaging
</p>
<p>Bi-Polar Stack Packaging Enabled by SSE
 Contains no flammable liquids
 Enables a shared current collector (bi-polar)
 Reduces safety containment weight
 Minimal/passive cooling system possible
 Potential for higher power density and C-rates
 90% of cell specific energy can be retained in pack
</p>
<p>Lithium-Ion Battery (SOA) Packaging
 Contains flammable electrolytes
 Requires heavy housing and cooling system
 The added pack weight reduces energy density
</p>
