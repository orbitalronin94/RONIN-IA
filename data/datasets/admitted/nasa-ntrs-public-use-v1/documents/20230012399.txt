<p>NDAS </p>
<p>NASA’s Data Acquisition Suite (NDAS) is a data collection and analysis software suite designed to support rocket propulsion testing.</p>
<p>Project Goals </p>
<p>Provide a NASA-owned and maintained software platform. </p>
<p>Provide a modular and customizable software suite that supports multiple hardware platforms.</p>
<p>Provide a common user experience to drive consistency across test complexes and centers.</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide3_rId4"></div>
<p>Project History</p>
<p>2011 : NDAS Project began as an RPT-level effort</p>
<p>2014 : Deployment to SSC A2 for J2X</p>
<p>2015 : Deployment to SSC A1 for RS-25 </p>
<p>2016 : Deployment to SSC B2 for SLS Green Run</p>
<p>2017 : Deployment to SSC E2 for EUS</p>
<p>2018 : Deployment to WSTF Test Labs</p>
<p>2019 : Deployment to MSFC East 115 &amp; 116</p>
<p>2020 : Deployment to SSC E1 and E3</p>
<p>2021 : Deployment to MSFC 400 Area &amp; SSC HPGF</p>
<p>2022 : Deployment to MSFC 40k Area</p>
<p>2023 : Established Core Team</p>
<p>Software Engineering Team </p>
<p>Combined Civil Servant &amp; Contractor Team </p>
<p></p>
<p>Expertise in software engineering, data acquisition instrumentation, embedded systems, electrical systems, and controls engineering.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Hold multiple NI LabVIEW certifications including Developer, Embedded Developer, Architect, and Champion.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Software Capabilities </p>
<p>Data acquisition, scaling, visualization, and recording of sensor data. </p>
<p>Software acquisition rates up to 400k sps provide flexibility for multiple project requirements.</p>
<p>Capable of communicating and display/recording external systems data.</p>
<p>Post processing into multiple file formats</p>
<p>Software Lifecycle Process</p>
<p>DevOps (Development and Operations) aims to integrate the work of development teams and operations teams by facilitating a culture of collaboration and shared responsibility.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Agile Scrum is an agile project management framework that helps teams structure and manage their work through a set of values, principles, and practices.</p>
<p>NDAS Core Platform</p>
<p>Core software platform allows for software solutions within the NDAS ecosystem</p>
<p>Thrust Vector Control System (TVCS) and Liquid Level Gauge (LLG) utilize streaming, display, and recording capabilities from core platform</p>
<p>Potential to provide other solutions</p>

<p>Suggested System Requirements</p>

<p>Windows 10 fully supported including LTSC</p>

<p>Windows 7 can be supported but not recommended.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.atlassian.com/devops/what-is-devops/devops-best-practices">DevOps</a><a href="https://www.atlassian.com/devops/what-is-devops/devops-best-practices"> </a></p>
<p>(Development and Operations) aims to integrate the work of development teams and operations teams by facilitating a culture of collaboration and shared responsibility.</p>
<p></p>
<p>DevOps <a href="https://www.atlassian.com/devops/what-is-devops/devops-best-practices">best practices</a> include Agile project management, shifting left with CI/CD, automation, monitoring, observability, and continuous feedback.</p>
<p><a href="https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html">Agile Scrum </a></p>
<p>Scrum is a lightweight framework that helps people, teams and organizations generate value through adaptive solutions for complex problems.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Scrum is founded on empiricism and lean thinking. Empiricism asserts that knowledge comes from experience and making decisions based on what is observed. Lean thinking reduces waste and focuses on the essentials.</p>
