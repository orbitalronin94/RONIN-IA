<div class="slide-content"><p>2023 Architecture Concept Review</p>
<p>11/17/23</p>
<p>7</p>
<p>The agency’s annual Architecture Concept Review process enabled stakeholders across NASA and among its partners to provide feedback on our Moon to Mars exploration plans.   ​</p>
<p>Products that come out of the Architecture Concept Review include updates to the agency’s Architecture Definition Document and White Papers highlighting key results from that year’s Strategic Analysis Cycle.</p>
<p>ACR23 focused on refining and adding subarchitectures, adding mature elements, and architectural strategies for the first crewed missions to Mars. </p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Architecture Definition Document (ADD)</p>
<p>11/17/23</p>
<p>8</p>
<p>The Architecture Definition Document is a NASA-published reference document that presents the current state of the human spaceflight architecture and exploration strategy.</p>
<p>The document decomposes Moon to Mars objectives into functions and use cases for allocation to implementable programs and projects.</p>
<p>It includes current partnerships presence in the architecture, identifies architectural gaps, and presents opportunities for further collaboration.</p>
<p>It is NOT a requirements document, a mission definition document, a planning manifest, or a procurement strategy.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230002706/downloads/M2MADD_ESDMD-001(TP-20230002706).pdf">current version</a> was published April 1, 2023. </p>
<p>NASA plans to publish yearly updates to the Architecture Definition Document, incorporating the results of the prior year’s Architecture Concept Review.</p>
<p></p>

<p>2024 Strategic Analysis Cycle</p>
<p>11/17/23</p>
<p>15</p>
<p>The 2024 Fiscal Year will be first full, annual cadence for the new Architecture Concept Review. </p>
<p>The architecture team endeavors to ensure repeatability and identify lessons learned for subsequent cycles. </p>
<p>The volume of needed analysis, integration, and workload will be matched to available agency resources.</p>
<p>Our priority is producing quality assessments that can be supported programmatically in implementation while ensuring communication with and feedback from the NASA community and government, industry, academic, and international stakeholders. </p>

<p>Current Process Limits </p>
<p>11/17/23</p>
<p>17</p>
<p>Historically, human spaceflight formulation have often been transient teams for single efforts. As a result, repeatable processes were not been established in many cases. </p>
<p>NASA Policy Directive 1000.5
Acquisition strategy placement occurs after mission concept review. </p>
<p>NASA Procedural Requirement 7120.5
Program project process begins at formulation milestone. </p>
<p>NASA Systems Engineering Handbook
Describes formulation functions but does not articulate ownership or process preceding a program.</p>
<p>Moon to Mars efforts are multi-decadal, necessitating consistent and repeatable processes. </p>
</div>

<p>18</p>
<p>Element Initiation
A New Pre-formulation Process</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Meeting a Need</p>
<p>Historically, exploration programs and projects have been developed through a variety of teams, processed, and functions.</p>
<p>Inconsistency in technical maturity and mission expectations at formulation has led to issues for implementing programs including cost, schedule, and risk.</p>
<p>Clarity and consistency in formulation and definition is necessary for NASA to execute the Moon to Mars exploration effort.</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p>Element Initiation</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Element Initiation Purpose</p>
<p>11/17/23</p>
<p>19</p>
<p>An overt integration point initiated by the Strategy and Architecture Office where the mission directorate commits to formulate element based on an identified architecture gap. </p>
<p>The element initiation afirms a proposed element aligns to an architectural need and implies intent to apply necessary resources to formulate that element. </p>
<p>Includes initial use case and functions within the architecture, as well as a preliminary concept.</p>
<p>Highlights potential scope of international contributions to further bilateral study commitments.</p>
<p>Identifies a team to develop products needed for a successful mission concept review. </p>
