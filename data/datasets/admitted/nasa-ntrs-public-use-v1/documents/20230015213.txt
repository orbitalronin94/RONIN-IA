• Liquid remains cohesive

• Bare tank (i.e., no baffles)

fill ratio

Apollo Era Heritage



Residual propellant + RCS thruster profile 
= anomalous retrograde motion [1].

Docked Control



• Flying the tank using 6-DoF LEO trajectory inputs



• No settling thrust



• Control inputs applied to maintain constant vehicle 



orientation (Sun pointing)



Key Observations:



• Baffles can keep the fluid aft somewhat, 



but can introduce some unexpected effects



• Tendency is for fluid to wet as much of the 



wall as possible and to rotate around the 



tank long axis
• Not ideal for transfer 



• Significant more boiloff



• Detrimental for vehicle control



• Use of tangential thrusters tends to unsettle 



the propellant



Credit: John Mishic, GRC



Generic tank configuration



Possible Solution: use axial thrusters for 



simultaneous settling and vehicle control
