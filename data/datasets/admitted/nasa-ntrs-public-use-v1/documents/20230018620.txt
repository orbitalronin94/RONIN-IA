<p>2</p>
<p>Background</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Self-pressurization</p>
<p>Liquid propellant and/or ullage is heated due to heat leakage to the propellant tank which gradually increases tank pressure</p>
<p>Must be understood and controlled for safety and to minimize propellant venting</p>
<p>Key dynamics</p>
<p>Low Reynolds number turbulent flow and natural convection, phase change</p>
<p>Nodal tools are unable to model and 3-D CFD analysis is required</p>
<p>Goals of validation</p>
<p>Primary: Time-accurate prediction of tank pressure for given heat leakage rate to the tank with reasonable margin of error</p>
<p>Secondary: Time-accurate temperature prediction across the tank</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Loci-Stream</p>
<p>Pressure-based solver</p>
<p>Solid heat module for conjugate heat analysis</p>
<p>Two phase sharp interface method or VOF method for two phase flows</p>
<p>Two-phase Sharp Interface Method</p>
<p>VOF advection using geometric reconstruction is expensive and slow</p>
<p>Developed sharp interface method for quiescent interface</p>
<p>10-100x speed up  Allows testing for model sensitivities</p>
<p>Avoid stationary interface instabilities of VOF method</p>
<p>Improvement on “lumped gas liquid CFD” method</p>
<p>Allows simulation of ullage dynamics – Lump gas node replaced with 3-D gas CFD</p>

<p>CFD Modeling</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>

<div class="slide-content"><p>6</p>
<p>CFD Model</p>
<p>Geometric simplifications</p>
<p>Internals removed</p>
<p>Axisymmetric</p>
<p>Turbulent flow (u-RANS)</p>
<p>Ideal gas, Boussinesq liquid</p>
<p>Temperature dependent gas properties</p>
<p>Constant solid properties</p>
<p>Kinetics method for mass transfer</p>
<p>Antoine equation for saturation condition</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>13</p>
<p>Flow Regime and Accommodation Factor Sensitivity</p>
<p>Laminar model lacks turbulence mixing and overpredicts interface temperature</p>
<p>SST model enhances mixing but still vastly underpredicts the mixing and consequently overpredicts the interface temperature and ullage pressure</p>
<p>The k-epsilon model performs best</p>

<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Determined and modeled the most important features for self-pressurization to successfully transfer the learnings to space flight application</p>
<p>Liquid simulant convective currents induced by localized heating, heat transfer within the fluid and with the tank walls, and the phase change mass transfer at the interface</p>
<p>Validated Loci-Stream CFD solver for self-pressurization of ZBOT tank in normal gravity</p>
<p>Tank pressure, pressurization rate as well as temperature across the tank simulant, ullage and walls are predicted with very good accuracy</p>
<p>Evaluated model sensitivities to ensure validation is independent of modeling parameters and can be applied to same class of problem</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>16</p>
<p>Two Phase Sharp Interface</p>
<p>Solve one set of conservation equations</p>
<p>Gas-liquid interface boundary treatment</p>
<p>Zero boundary normal velocities</p>
<p>Equal tangential velocities</p>
<p>Equal tangential stress components</p>
<p>Phase change mass transfer with source terms</p>
<p>Phase change</p>
<p>Balance Method</p>
<p>Equilibrium assumption – interfacial gas is always saturated</p>
<p></p>
<p>Kinetics-Balance Method (Iterative)</p>
<p>Interfacial gas pressure can deviate from saturation condition</p>
<p>Mass transfer using kinematic relationship by Hertz-Knudsen-Schrage (HKS)</p>
<p>Solved iteratively with interfacial heat balance</p>
<p>Interface Turbulence Treatment</p>
<p>Experimental evidence: turbulence is damped and behaves somewhat similar to wall-bounded flow</p>
<p>Also, some evidence of asymmetric behavior</p>
<p>Wall-like on gas side</p>
<p>Slip-wall-like on liquid side</p>
<p>Simplified Egorov’s damping approach</p>
<p>Gas interface is always wall-like</p>
<p>Liquid interface is either wall-like or slip-wall-like</p>
