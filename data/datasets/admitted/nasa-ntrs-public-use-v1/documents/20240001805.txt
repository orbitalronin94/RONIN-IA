<p>Additive Manufacturing (AM) is certainly changing the space industry and 
providing new opportunities to travel to low earth orbit and explore our 
universe
</p>
<p>New design opportunities – not previously possible – for new high 
performance metal alloys, light-weighting, managing thermal, structural, and 
dynamic loads are being enabled by AM.
</p>
<p>Additively manufactured parts are already being used for NASA programs in 
critical applications – “ready to fly”
  
Human exploration of space, especially deep space, requires extreme 
reliability</p>

<p>Advancing Metal AM – New AM-enabled Alloy
</p>
<p>12
</p>
<p>• Glenn Research Center eXtreme temperature alloy (GRX-810) was 
developed using ICME
</p>
<p>• Ni-Co-Cr medium entropy alloy with nano-scale Y2O3 coating.
• Significantly improved properties:
</p>
<p>• 2x strength at elevated temperatures (1100°C)
• 1,000x better creep rupture
• 2x better resistance to oxidation
</p>
<p>Motivation
</p>

</p>

</p>

engineers at Marshall Space Flight Center have 
helped Relativity Space Inc. of Long Beach, 
California, 3D print its Aeon rocket engines using a 
NASA-invented copper alloy, and the company 
leases space and facilities at Stennis Space Center 
to test the engines. These partnerships have saved 
the company untold time and money.”

<p>Ensuring Safe AM Insertion
</p>
<p>• AM technology is demonstrating incredible benefits in wide-
spread applications, design flexibility, affordability, schedule, 
performance, sustainability, and production on demand.
• Certification challenge: complexity, unknowns, and 
</p>
<p>delays are severely negating the benefits. 
• NASA continues to be a leader in the advancement of 
</p>
<p>policies for the Certification of AM processes and parts
• NASA-STD-6030 and 6033, Agency standard for additive 
</p>
<p>manufacturing; actively being used for critical NASA 
flight programs
</p>
<p>SpaceX Starship Lunar Lander Blue Origin Lunar Lander
</p>

</p>

</p>
<p>“Certification” is used broadly in this presentation to encompass all activities and data generation used to approve the advanced 
manufacturing process and the parts it produces for use in safety critical applications </p>
