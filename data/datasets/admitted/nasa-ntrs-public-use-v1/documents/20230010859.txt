<p>Roman Mission Overview
</p>
<p>• RST: Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope (Class A)
• Mission:  Wide-Field Infrared Survey
• Objectives:
</p>
<p>– Determine the nature of the dark energy that is driving the current accelerating expansion 
of the universe
</p>
<p>– Perform statistical census of planetary systems through microlensing survey
– Survey the NIR sky
– Provide the community with a wide field telescope for pointed observations
– Fly a technology demonstration of a high-contrast coronagraph instrument
</p>
<p>• Mission Duration: 5 years science 
• Orbit: Quasi-Halo Orbit about Sun-Earth L2
• Launch Vehicle: Falcon Heavy
• Launch Site: Eastern Range
• Mission Budget: $3.3 Billion through Phase E
• Mass: 10,750 kg (NTE)
• LRD: October 2026
</p>

<p>CGI Top Level Error Budget and Stability
</p>
<p>• CGI stability requirements flow from Flux Ratio Noise (FRN) error budget 
– Contrast sensitivities, derived from optical diffraction models set in PROPER tool, have been validated against the coronagraph testbed
– MUF = 2 is used on sensitivities
</p>
<p>• L4 Initial Static Raw Contrast: quasi-static conditions
• L4 External Contrast Stability: Effects due to Observatory stability during an observing scenario, which CGI must suppress using internal 
</p>
<p>control loops and/or accommodate
• L4 Internal Contrast Stability: Effects due to CGI internal stability, such as DMs, optical bench, etc
</p>
<p>Focus of 
Presentation</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>808/10/2023
</p>
<p>• CGI relies on observatory stability to meet technology demonstration goals
– Observatory is designed to meet Wide Field science requirements
</p>
<p>• CGI stability requirements are met by using CGI internal controllers and observatory operational 
capabilities
</p>
<p>– LOS jitter rejection achieved by fast steering mirror (FSM) and low-order-wavefront-sensor (LOWFS)
– WFE drift rejection through focus mechanism (FM) and deformable mirrors (DMs) 
– Constrain wheel speeds
– Avoid moving HGA during exposures
– CGI stability requirements only need to be met for ≥70% of images
</p>

<p>Optical Telescope Assembly: Thermal Management System
</p>
<p>• TMS is cold-biased design supplemented with heater 
control to provide a stable OTA in all specified 
environments
</p>
<p>– Each heater zone has independent PI thermal control 
during all phases of mission
</p>
<p>– IOA has multiple temperature-controlled zones
• Outer Barrel Assembly (OBA) provides FOA with a 
</p>
<p>stable, 230 K thermal environment
– MLI Closeout between AMS and OBA limits FOA’s 
</p>
<p>radiative exposure to uncontrolled environments
• Observatory’s most thermally sensitive components 
</p>
<p>include the PM, SM, and SMSTs
– STOP analysis has shown that these components 
</p>
<p>require ~10 mK level stability to meet optical 
requirements
</p>
<p>– Sub-milli-Kelvin temperature stability capability was 
demonstrated with a flight-like integrated thermal control 
system (Roman DITS)
</p>
<p>• AOM, POMA, and TOMA are separate optical 
assemblies located on the aft side of the AMS with 
their own operating temperatures / thermal designs.
</p>
<p>– TOMA includes a tip/tilt fold mirror to correct pupil shear
– Each rely on their MLI-blanketed enclosures as radiators 
</p>
<p>to provide sufficient cold-biasing for positive heater 
control
</p>
<p>Heater Set Points
AOM Heaters @ 218K Set Point
AMS, FMS, FOA Strut, SMA &amp; POMA Heaters @ 266.5K Set Point
SMST Heaters @ 269K Set Point
TOMA Heaters @ 293K Set Point
</p>

<p>• Analysis is a key verification approach for 
stability requirements
</p>
<p>• For requirements verified by analysis, all 
performance predictions include model 
uncertainty 
</p>
<p>• Model uncertainty can be incorporated by 
using worst case assumptions, model 
uncertainty factor (MUF), or Monte Carlo 
analysis
– Optical analysis uses Monte Carlo approach to 
</p>
<p>capture reasonable fabrication and alignment 
tolerances
</p>
<p>– Thermal analysis uses worst case assumptions
– Distortion and dynamic analyses use MUF to 
</p>
<p>capture reasonable parameter variations (CTE, 
moduli, etc.)
</p>
<p>• Roman uses structural Monte Carlo analysis 
to determine appropriate MUFs for analysis 
predictions
</p>

<p>Major Dynamics Error Sources
</p>
<p>WFI Element Wheel

<p>RWAs

<p>HGAS
(two gimbal actuators)
</p>
<p>CGI Fast 
Steering 
Mirror
</p>
<p>• Reaction Wheel Assemblies
– Six Honeywell HR18-250 RWAs
– Fine balance option for reduced static/dynamic 
</p>
<p>disturbance
</p>
<p>• High Gain Antenna System (HGAS)
– Two axis gimbal using low-detent stepper 
</p>
<p>motors to provide gimbal pointing
– The antenna rarely would need to be moved 
</p>
<p>during imaging
</p>
<p>• WFI Element Wheel (EW)
– Stepper motor used to place the desired optic 
</p>
<p>into the light path
– Will not operate during imaging
</p>
<p>• CGI Fast Steering Mirror (FSM)
– Reaction compensated tip/tilt mirror
– Self-induced disturbance, managed by CGI 
– Note: much less contribution from other, smaller 
</p>
<p>mechanisms (Focus and DMs)</p>
<p></p>

<p>1708/10/2023
</p>
<p>Dynamics Mitigations
</p>
<p>• Launch Loads and Vibration Isolation System (LLVIS)
– Between the SC and IC, Honeywell D-strut heritage
– ~20 Hz first mode, attenuates RWA and HGS disturbances
</p>
<p>• Reaction Wheel Jitter Mitigation Implementations 
– Each RWA is individually isolated, Moog CSA SoftRide heritage
– RWA speeds are limited to 5 rev/sec (30 RPM) during CGI operation to 
</p>
<p>avoid exciting resonant modes above this frequency
– ACS is using an L-infinity wheel distribution algorithm that drives four 
</p>
<p>wheels to the same speed
– ACS is enforcing 1 Hz (60 RPM) separation between the four wheels
</p>
<p>• HGAS Jitter Mitigation Implementations 
– HGAS Jitter Damper (HJD) developed by Moog CSA
</p>
<p>• Damps out HGAS boom modes excited during HGAS operation
– Actuator microstepping; 16 micro-steps per every detent step
– HGAS step avoidance during inertial hold
</p>
<p>• ACS is designing their HGAS pointing algorithm and slew profile to minimize the 
need to step during imaging
</p>
<p>– HGAS step rate keep out zone 
• Accelerate through problematic mode frequencies to avoid ringing up the modes
</p>
<p>• Solar Array Sun Shield (SASS) Tuned Mass Dampers (TMDs)
– Under development by Moog CSA
– Damps out SASS modes excited during wheel and HGAS operations
</p>
