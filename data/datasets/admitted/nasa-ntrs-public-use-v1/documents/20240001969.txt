▪ STMD-GRC – Refractory Alloy Additive Manufacturing Build Optimization (RAAMBO)



▪ Hypersonic Technology (HTP) Project.
• Advance material characterization and prototypic testing allowing for critical data needed to demonstrate TRL

• Enable national supply chain for refractory alloy feedstock essential for production of historical to custom alloys

Uniaxial Tension Testing of Niobium Alloy C103



12



Elevated Temperature Tension Testing



−Wrought barstock in the as-worked 
(extruded) and recrystallized condition 
was tested to compare to historical data 
and was found to be similar.



− Printed materials exhibited dramatic 
differences in elevated temperature 
strength depending on post processing



− As-printed condition has 2X yield strength 
of HIP’d condition



− HIP’d material shows similar high-
temperature performance to 
recrystallized barstock











Creep Testing of Niobium Alloy C103



13



Elevated Temperature Creep Testing



−Wrought barstock in the recrystallized 
condition tested to compare to 
additively manufactured materials.



− Creep testing is ongoing and needs a lot 
of time and specimens to determine 
material response over a wide range of 
temperatures and stresses.
