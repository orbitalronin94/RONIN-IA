<p>Crew Time Is Valuable – a Gateway Example
• Crew time is highly planned during a mission
</p>
<p>• The chart at right estimates time allocations based on historical 
ISS crew time usage and assumptions about Gateway 
operations
⎻ Crewmembers: 4
⎻ Mission duration: 30d
</p>
<p>• Utilization is a major objective for human spaceflight, but 
secondary to crew health and spacecraft caretaking
</p>
<p>• Robots have an opportunity to maximize crew time
⎻ Upkeep ops: housekeeping, work prep, 
</p>
<p>system/consumable management, organization, etc.
⎻ Utilization: scientific research tasks
</p>
<p>⎻ Caretaking and preparations for crew arrival
</p>
<p>On the Lunar Surface, crew time will be similarly precious, 
especially on EVAs
</p>
<p>Source: C. Stromgren, et al. “Predicting Crew Time Allocations on Lunar Orbital Missions Based on Historical ISS 
Operational Activities,” 2018 AIAA SPACE and Astronautics Forum and Exposition, Orlando, FL</p>
<p></p>
</div>

<p>How Can Continuum Manipulators Help? 
• Gateway is smaller than ISS but will 
</p>
<p>have many heritage features and 
influences
</p>
<p>• We can lean on ISS experience for 
ideas
</p>
<p>• Inspection and surveying of 
inaccessible or difficult to reach areas
⎻ A recent troubleshooting activity for 
</p>
<p>crew exercise equipment required 
rotating a rack.
</p>
<p>⎻ Excluding the work on the anomalous 
component, this took 6h 10m to 
prepare and reset the rack.  An 
additional crewmember was needed to 
assist in the physical rotation due to its 
mass and size.</p>

<p>Looking to Gateway Intravehicular Robotics
• Gateway System Managers are 
</p>
<p>interested in general purpose robots, 
but realize they don’t do it all
⎻ It might also be useful to have a limited 
</p>
<p>number of specialized robots, [Dr. Julia] 
Badger says. “For example, if there was 
a reason to get behind a rack, you may 
want a snake-type of robot for that.”
</p>
<p>• Other use case candidates include 
searching for leaks, assessing 
equipment, fire alarm response
</p>
<p>Note: Gateway has initial provisions for 
intravehicular robotics, but no systems are yet 
baselined.
</p>
<p>Quote Source: Ackerman, Evan. “Meet the Lunar Gateway’s Robot Caretakers” https://spectrum.ieee.org/lunar-gateway-robots. 2022-04-07.
Image source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/Gateway-Telephoto-IAC7-Set-4k-BG_5</p>

<p>Lunar Surface Opportunities
• Surface power distribution
</p>
<p>⎻ Power generation may be located at a distance 
from the loads, connected via cables
</p>
<p>⎻ Are crewmembers the wisest choice for making 
connections?
</p>
<p>⎻ A continuum robot integrated into a cable could 
make connections, inspect physical connectors for 
dust/debris
</p>
<p>⎻ Is a continuum robot part of a mobility system to 
unspool cables?
</p>
<p>• Habitats and other elements are likely to have 
external fluid connections, data cables, etc.
</p>
<p>• Inspection of landers prior to ascent
</p>
<p>• Intravehicular tasks onboard a habitat or 
pressurized rover may be suited for continuum 
robotics, like aboard Gateway
</p>
<p>• These architectures are in work and can be 
influenced with new capabilities
</p>
<p>Source: NASA STMD Envisioned Future Priority for Power and Energy Storage Systems
https://techport.nasa.gov/strategy</p>
<p></p>
<div class="annotation"><a href="https://techport.nasa.gov/strategy">https://techport.nasa.gov/strategy</a></div>

<p>Key Messages
</p>
<p>• Future human spacecraft have long uncrewed periods
⎻ Caretaking while crews are away will be greatly improved by the presence of 
</p>
<p>robots
⎻ Robotic inspections and maintenance can help ensure very costly and 
</p>
<p>irreplaceable mission elements are long-lasting
</p>
<p>• Crew time can be maximized for science and exploration objectives 
with robotic support before, during, and after a mission
</p>
<p>• Demonstrating and communicating what continuum robotics bring to 
the trade space allows for more informed architecture decisions</p>
