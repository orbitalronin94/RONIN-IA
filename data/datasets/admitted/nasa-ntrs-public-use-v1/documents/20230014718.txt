<p>External CAMMP Overview
eCAMMP Analyses - ISS Configuration
</p>
<p>▪ SSP 30219 (Space Station Reference Coordinate Systems)
</p>
<p>• Establishes a set of coordinate systems to be used when reporting data between the International Space Station (ISS) Program Participants
</p>
<p>▪ SSP 50949 (ISS Configuration Document)
</p>
<p>• Details the ISS baseline configuration (external platforms, element configuration, camera port plan, ISS port connectivity, VV support 
</p>
<p>hardware, robotic elements, retroreflector locations, and internal topologies)
</p>
<p>▪ ISS Reference Information
</p>
<p>• SharePoint site providing ISS overview data such as Russian Configuration Numbers, Camera Port Configuration, Current ISS Configuration 
</p>
<p>Images, External Carriers and Payload Site configuration (contained within ISS Reference Packet)
</p>
<p>o External Carrier Site configuration data feeds into IDRD Appendix E
</p>
<p>o Updated on an ongoing basis as planned on-orbit operations are confirmed</p>
<p></p>

<p>MAGIK Overview
ISS Robotics General Information
</p>
<p>▪ OM7/ISS Robotics Integration leads the coordination with various 
</p>
<p>stakeholders to ensure robotic readiness in support of ISS objectives
</p>
<p>▪ Works closely with FOD through the life cycle to prepare for 
</p>
<p>Payload Operations
</p>
<p>▪ MSS (Mobile Servicing System) and JEM RMS (Japanese Exploration 
</p>
<p>Module Remote Manipulator System) are the primary methods for 
</p>
<p>installation, operation, and disposal of external payloads on the 
</p>
<p>International Space Station
</p>
<p>• JEM RMS, operated by JAXA, is composed of Main Arm and Small 
</p>
<p>Fine Arm (SFA)
</p>
<p>• Used to install and service payloads on the JEM External Facility 
</p>
<p>and JEM Airlock
</p>
<p>▪ Canadian Space Agency (CSA) developed and jointly operated with 
</p>
<p>NASA, MSS is made up of:
</p>
<p>• SSRMS (Space Station Remote Manipulator System)
</p>
<p>• SPDM (Special Purpose Dexterous Manipulator)
</p>
<p>• Mobile Base System (MBS) installed on the Mobile Transporter 
</p>
<p>(MT)
</p>
<p>SSRMS
</p>
<p>SPDM</p>
<p></p>
</div>

<p>▪ iCAMMP/IVCWG Definitions
</p>
<p>• Maintains 3D CAD models of the interior configuration of ISS
</p>
<p>• Performs analyses on the planned and current configuration
</p>
<p>• Internal Volume &amp; Configuration Working Group (IVCWG) is the ISS 
</p>
<p>community working group that meets monthly to discuss internal
</p>
<p>configuration changes and requirements
</p>
<p>▪ Types of analyses performed by iCAMMP include:
</p>
<p>• I-4.5 mo. and I-1.5 mo. Increment Configuration Analysis
</p>
<p>• Placement Analysis for systems hardware
</p>
<p>• Cargo Translation Analysis
</p>
<p>• Stowage Capability Analysis
</p>
<p>▪ Additional iCAMMP Responsibilities
</p>
<p>• Document and maintain SSP 50261-01 GGR&amp;C Section 13 
</p>
<p>• Document and maintain USOS Rack Topology (baselined in IDRD)
</p>
<p>• Integration activities for internal volume configuration
</p>
<p>Services &amp; Deliverables
</p>
<p>Internal CAMMP Overview</p>
<p></p>
</div>

<p>| HUMAN SPACE FLIGHT TECHNICAL INTEGRATION CONTRACT (HSFTIC)|  17

<p>▪ Placement Analyses
</p>
<p>• Performed as requested to identify locations for systems hardware 
</p>
<p>installation or relocation
</p>
<p>• Requestor provides requirements/constraints and multiple locations are 
</p>
<p>assessed for GGR&amp;C Section 13 requirements
</p>
<p>▪ Increment Analyses
</p>
<p>• Performed at Increment minus 4.5 months (I-4.5m) and at I-1.5m
</p>
<p>• Verifies compliance of planned internal configuration of the US Lab, US 
</p>
<p>Airlock, PMAs, and Nodes 1, 2, and 3 to GGR&amp;C Section 13
</p>
<p>• Multiple organizations' data and previously completed analyses are used
</p>
<p>to build the Increment configuration models
</p>
<p>▪ Stowage Analyses
</p>
<p>• Performed to analyze stowage configurations supplementing hardware
</p>
<p>placement or protrusion assessments
</p>
<p>• Assessments can include:
</p>
<p>o Analyzing volumetric capability
</p>
<p>o Packing efficiency studies
</p>
<p>o Images for stowage visualization
</p>
