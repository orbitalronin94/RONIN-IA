Human Eye Response


Human Eye Characteristics:


Uses a combination of mechanisms to adapt


Light to Dark Adaptation


Pupil: few minutes


Cones: 5-10 minutes


Rods: 20-30 minutes


Dark to Light Adaptation - much faster





Simulated Eye Response:


Auto exposure algorithm with a center weighted pixel sampling


Weighting based on the cosine of the angle off center


Decreased adaptation time vs real world


Light to Dark: 10-20 seconds


Dark to Light: 1-2 seconds
