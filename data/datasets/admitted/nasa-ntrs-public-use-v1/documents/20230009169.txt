Conclusions



• Image segmentation applied to optical imagery of the borosilicate 
specimens provides a practical path to estimating porosity in this 
context.
• Initial results show agreement with porosity estimates obtained from 



X-ray CT data.
• A thorough analysis of the uncertainty in the porosity estimates 



obtained using the random forest classifier using the methods 
introduced by Wager, Hastie, and Efron2 will be an important next 
step.
