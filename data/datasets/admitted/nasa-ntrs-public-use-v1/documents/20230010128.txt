<div class="slide-content"><p>CERES Instrument Status Summary</p>
<p>All CERES instruments continue to demonstrate stable performance.</p>
<p>NOAA-20/FM6 instrument continues to perform nominally. </p>
<p>SNPP/FM5 is currently operating in full biaxial mode. </p>
<p>No evidence of deviation of instrument performance since transitioning to biaxial mode. </p>
<p>Terra and Aqua instruments are conducting Cross-track, biaxial, and GEOSAT scans.</p>
<p>Validations show that all instruments are performing consistently.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Level 1 Data products </p>
<p>NOAA-20/FM6 Edition 1 gains have been delivered through March 2023.</p>
<p>S-NPP/FM5 Edition 2 gains and SRFs have been delivered through January 2023. </p>
<p>Terra and Aqua instruments’ Edition 4 gains and SRFs have been delivered through February 2023. </p>

<p>GEOSAT : </p>
<p>For 5 days spaced evenly through the month, scan plane of CERES is aligned with a GEO imager for a few orbits. </p>
<p>Target a different GEO imager each day.</p>
<p>Instrument is operated in XTK for the rest of the day. </p>
<p>XTK: Cross-track</p>
<p>BIAX: Biaxial (RAP)</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Aqua/NOAA-20 Intercomparisons</p>
<p>The orbital geometries for Aqua and NOAA-20 are such that orbital overlaps occur every ~64 hours. </p>
<p>Obtain spatially and temporally matched observations during every crossover.</p>
<p>No special operations are conducted to match viewing geometries; Both instruments continue operating in cross-track mode.</p>
<p>Use matching criteria to subset the data:</p>
<p>SZA, VZA difference &lt; 2.00</p>
<p>RAZ difference &lt; 50</p>
<p>Distance between centroid of footprints &lt; 7 km</p>
<p>Obtain monthly all-sky SW reflectance and LW radiance differences using the matched footprints.</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>SUMMARY</p>
<p>CERES instruments on Terra and Aqua have started operations to support scientific studies as the orbit drifts.</p>
<p>All CERES instruments continue to perform nominally.</p>
<p>NOAA-20/FM6 instrument on-board calibrations continue to show the sensors’ stable performance after the initial response rise. </p>
<p>SNPP/FM5 is currently operating in full biaxial mode. </p>
<p>No evidence of deviation of instrument performance since transitioning to biaxial mode. </p>
<p>Terra and Aqua instruments’ performance are monitored through validation studies as well as inter-comparisons with other CERES instruments.</p>
<p>Level 1 Data products </p>
<p>NOAA-20/FM6 Edition 1 gains have been delivered through Mar 2023.</p>
<p>S-NPP/FM5 Edition 2 gains and SRFs have been delivered through Jan 2023. </p>
<p>Terra and Aqua instruments’ Edition 4 gains and SRFs have been delivered through Feb 2023. </p>
<p>Members of the IWG continue to engage with the Libera team through bi-weekly Cal/Val Working group meetings. </p>
