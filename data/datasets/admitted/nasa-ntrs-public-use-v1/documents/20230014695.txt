<div class="slide-content"><p>Conclusion</p>
<p>In general, all the coatings were insulative at 0% RH/ vacuum which resulted in varying levels of dust clearing in vacuum</p>
<p>Thickness between the electrodes and surface affected dust clearing</p>
<p>Solar Absorptance was restored within 10% of original value when EDS worked</p>
<p>Even partially it restored some solar absorptance back</p>
<p>Thermal vacuum revealed EDS has some thermal impact but this hasn’t been fully tested</p>
<p>Different adhesive will have to be used to prevent delamination (+100 C)</p>
<p>More investigation is warranted before TRL 7</p>
<p>4</p>
