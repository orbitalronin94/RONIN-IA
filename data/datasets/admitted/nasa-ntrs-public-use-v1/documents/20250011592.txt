Computational Model


Two phase model: sharp interface method 


Turbulence Model: IDDES (Hybrid RANS/LES closure) and RANS


Asymmetric interface turbulence transport modeling: Wall-like turbulence on gas side of the interface


Phase change: Gas kinetic theory-based Hertz-Knudsen-Schrage model


1-deg axisymmetric CFD Model with refined mesh

Conclusion


Validated multiphase-flow modeling methodology for CFM Modeling Portfolio for MHTB self-pressurization using 50% fill level, 51 W heat-leak test.


25% and 90% fill-level tests predictions were unsatisfactory, which prompted a detailed investigation


Flow physics insights from spatial heat leak distribution study


Strong sensitivity to spatial distribution of heat leak rate on pressurization rate


Phasic heat distribution is skewed towards liquid due to liquid phase’s high specific heat and effective thermal conductivity (laminar + eddy conductivity) and high conductivity of the aluminum tank wall


Despite most of the heat distributed to the liquid phase, the small amount of heat rate absorbed by the ullage vapor dictates the self-pressurization rate


Future work


Investigate effect of heat leak distribution with higher fidelity modeling of tank features


The study provides detailed insights into self-pressurization mechanisms and, together with the heat leak sensitivity analysis technique, enables improvements in self-pressurization predictions for NASA’s spaceflight missions
