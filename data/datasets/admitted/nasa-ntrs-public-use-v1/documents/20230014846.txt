<p>Background
</p>
<p>2
</p>
<p>▪ NASA’s long term vision includes crewed Mars missions
</p>
<p>▪ Current focus is on Artemis program
• Demonstrate key Mars enabling technologies 
</p>

</p>

highly available and reliable power
• Power needs to be as reliable/universal as terrestrial utility
</p>
<p>• Expansion necessitates planetary surface power grids
</p>
<p>▪ NASA’s STMD investing in R&amp;D work for an Artemis grid
• Microgrid Definition and Interface Converter for Planetary 
</p>

<p>Universal Modular Interface Converter
</p>
<p>4
</p>
<p>UMIC
</p>
<p>▪ Anticipate 120 VDC local lunar 
power systems
• Converter is necessary to 
</p>
<p>interface these to grid
</p>
<p>▪ MIPS set out to develop 
Universal Modular Interface 
Converter (UMIC)
• Conducted trade studies to 
</p>
<p>identify grid design parameters</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>5 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>UMIC Concept Design
</p>
<p>▪ Conceptual Implementation
• HV AC relay for isolation
</p>
<p>• Line frequency transformer(s)
</p>
<p>• Parallelable inverter modules
</p>
<p>• Current limiting solid-state DC 
Switch Module (DC switchgear)
</p>
<p>• Ethernet Controller for external 
comms interface
</p>
<p>5
</p>
<p>▪ Goals
• Standardized interface converter between 120 VDC users and 3 kV 3-phase AC 1 kHz grid
</p>
<p>• Consisting of field-replaceable, bidirectional, modular grid forming inverters
</p>
<p>• 1 kW per module, 10 kW total power or greater
</p>
<p>▪ Objectives
• Efficiency &gt;95%, specific power &gt;350 W/kg (estimated for flight)</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>6 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>UMIC 10 kW Breadboard Rack
</p>
<p>6
</p>
<p>▪ Breadboard Implementation
• 10 kW nominal power capability (12 kW peak)
</p>
<p>• 19” rack based breadboard form factor
</p>
<p>• Composed of one transformer chassis and multiple 
parallel inverter chassis
</p>
<p>• Parallelable - can provide more than 10 kW at 
single location
</p>
<p>• Design goals:
</p>
<p>• &gt;95% efficiency
</p>
<p>• 350 W/kg power density (est. for flight)
</p>
<p>• Capability:
</p>
<p>• Grid forming
</p>
<p>• Synchronization with existing grid power</p>
<p></p>
</div>

<p>9 | #ascendspace

<p>UMIC Control Algorithm
</p>
<p>▪ Independent FPGA control in each inverter
• Cascaded, closed-loop, dq-based droop control
</p>
<p>• 50 kHz PWM
</p>
<p>• 50 kHz Current loop
</p>
<p>• 10 kHz Voltage loop
</p>
<p>• 5 kHz Droop loop
</p>
<p>• Approach based on terrestrial grid forming 
inverter literature</p>
<p></p>

<p>UMIC Transformer Designs
</p>
<p>10
</p>
<p>NASA 1 kVA 3-phase design
</p>
<p>3.3 kVA single-phase design
(3x for 10 kW)
</p>
<p>▪ Transformer chassis to include all transformers 
needed at a given site (scales with power)
• Modular (1 kVA) transformer designs
</p>
<p>(both single and 3-phase types)
• 10x 1 kVA three phase transformers (10 kVA total)
</p>
<p>• Centralized transformer design
(single-phase type only)
• 3x 3.3 kVA single phase transformers (10 kVA total) </p>

</p>

<p>13 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>UMIC Module Test Progress
</p>
<p>13
</p>
<p>▪ Closed loop controllers validated in informal hardware bench testing
</p>
<p>▪ Instrument photos shown from initial test at rated load
• Shows ability of circuit to handle rated power and interface rated 3 kVAC to 120 VDC voltages
</p>
<p>• Efficiencies shown lower than expected: 94% for inverter, 95% for transformer
• Significant room to optimize both subsystems</p>
<p></p>

<p>Breadboard Expected Outcomes
</p>
<p>14
</p>
<p>▪ Validate concept with the following demo
• Bidirectional AC power transfer between two 10 kW UMICs via 1 km cable
</p>
<p>▪ Characterize UMIC to inform future development
• Identify challenges, find areas for improvement in HW design
</p>
<p>• Determine suitability of UMIC interface definition</p>

<p>Conclusions
</p>
<p>15
</p>
<p>▪ Future planetary surface missions will need universal access to reliable power
• Artemis
</p>
<p>• Commercial Lunar Development
</p>
<p>• Mars
</p>
<p>▪ The UMIC enables high voltage, bidirectional power transmission
• Necessary for universal, reliable planetary surface power over km distance
</p>
<p>▪ NASA is making investments to advance the UMIC technology and make it available 
for future government and industrial use</p>
<p></p>

<p>Future Work
</p>
<p>16
</p>
<p>▪ Seek opportunities to advance UMIC technology
• Increase TRL
</p>
<p>• Build for and demonstrate in environments beyond lab
</p>
<p>• Provide a solution for tech demos requiring power transmission
</p>
<p>• Push beyond breadboard
• Design for form and fit in addition to function
</p>
<p>• Demonstrate wide applicability, a truly universal solution
• Grid-to-120 VDC load or grid-to-120 VDC source interface converter
</p>

</p>
