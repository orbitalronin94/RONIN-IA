<p>3
</p>
<p>Theory of Metallized Plastic Current Collectors
</p>
<p>• Internal short defect is fed rapidly 
</p>
<p>through solid metal (Al, Cu) film current 
</p>
<p>collectors
</p>
<p>• Polymer separator thermally breakdowns 
</p>
<p>and shrinks away creating higher internal 
</p>
<p>short risk between anode and cathode
</p>
<p>• Plastic substrate of current collector 
</p>
<p>thermally breaks down and isolates the 
</p>
<p>electrochemically active materials from the 
</p>
<p>defect within milliseconds
</p>

<p>Potential Root Causes for Nail TR
</p>
<p>• Wh/kg, Wh/L limit reached for 
PCCs?
</p>
<p>• Need more thermally stable 
separator?
</p>
<p>• Need to add (-) Cu coated PCC?
</p>
<p>• Cathode active material adhesion 
needs to be poor?
</p>
<p>• Cathode active material porosity 
can’t be too low?
</p>
<p>• No: Lower SOC testing of 
&gt;260Wh/kg cells results in TR at 
90 &amp; 80%
</p>
<p>• No: Replacing sep with 300°C 
cellulose (Dreamweaver) results 
in TR at 100, 90, and 80% SoC
</p>
<p>• No: Replacing with Cu coated 
PCC results in TR at 100, 90, &amp; 
80% SoC
</p>
<p>• Possible: BAK cells have very 
poor adhesion
</p>
<p>• Possible: Higher porosity 4.5Ah 
cells by Coulometrics tolerate nail</p>
<p></p>

<p>Summary of our 2022-2023 Effort
</p>
<p>Soteria polyester PCC is reliable in tolerating nail penetration in 59 
out of 60 Li-ion cells with &lt;250 Wh/kg
</p>
<p>• 2.1Ah Coulometric 18650s (26 for 26)
– And 4 for 7 for Nomex PPCs
</p>
<p>• 2.9Ah BAK 18650s (11 for 11)
</p>
<p>• 4.6Ah BAK 21700s (10 for 11)
</p>
<p>• 10Ah SVolt Pouch Cells (12 for 12)
</p>
<p>Higher energy (&gt;260 Wh/kg) 21700 cell designs fail nail pen
</p>
<p>• 5.192Ah Coulometrics (0 for 21, even at 70% SoC)
– Al PCC reduces TR heat output vs Al foil cells
</p>
<p>• 4.97Ah Coulometrics with DW sep (0 for 9, even at 80% SoC)
</p>
<p>• 4.94Ah Coulometrics with DW sep &amp; Cu PCC (0 for 9, even at 
80% SoC
</p>
<p>Investigation into root causes continues with new cells builds
</p>
<p>• Higher porosity cathode active material
</p>
<p>• Lower adhesion of cathode active materials to collector
</p>
<p>• Compare safety with 20Ah pouch cell format
</p>
