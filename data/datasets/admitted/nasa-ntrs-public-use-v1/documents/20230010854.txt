<p>What is the FBCE?
• The Flow Boiling and Condensation 
</p>
<p>Experiment (FBCE) is an integrated two-
phase flow facility operating in the Fluids 
Integrated Rack (FIR) aboard ISS.
</p>
<p>• The FBCE serves as a primary platform for 
obtaining flow boiling and condensation 
heat transfer data in microgravity.
</p>
<p> 
</p>
<p>TFAWS 2023 – August 21-25, 2023 3
</p>
<p>FBCE in the FIR Ground Integration Unit (GIU) at GRC</p>
<p></p>

<p>Fluid Conditioning Capabilities
</p>
<p>• Test Fluid Flow: 2.5-40 g/s 
• Heater Power: 0-1500 W 
• Test Fluid Pressure: ~12.5-21 psia
</p>
<p>– Measured at accumulator outlet
– Test module inlet pressure is 
</p>
<p>dependent on flow rate and 
accumulator set pressure
</p>
<p>• ITCS Water Flow: 0 – 40 g/s
– Combined between both loops 
</p>

<p>Flow Boiling Test Module
</p>
<p>• The Flow Boiling Test Module is the first module to be integrated with the 
FBCE facility hardware
</p>
<p>• It is intended to gather flow boiling data in microgravity
</p>
<p>• Areas of Impact: Correlations made with this data will help to enable design 
of Rankine cycle power systems, and two-phase thermal control systems for 
spaceflight applications 
</p>

<p>Condensation Test Module Testing
</p>
<p>• 3 Teams of Principal Investigators
– Purdue
– University of South Carolina (USC)
– Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI)/New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT)

<p>Transfer Line Test Module
</p>
<p>TFAWS 2023 – August 21-25, 2023 16
</p>
<p>• Intended to investigate line chilldown in microgravity
– Relevant to cryogenic in-space cryogenic tank-to-tank transfer
</p>
<p>• Two teams of Researchers:
– University of Illinois Urbana Champagne (UIUC)
– Case Wester Reserve University (CWRU)
</p>
<p>• Requirements are currently being defined
• Two test sections:
</p>
<p>– Stainless Steel Heat Transfer
– Sapphire (TBD) Visualization </p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Transfer Line Test Module
• Initial conditions:
</p>
<p>– Test section under test
• Heated to desired temperature (entirely vapor)
</p>
<p>– Concurrently
• Inlet conditions are achieved
• Test section not under test acts as a bypass loop 
</p>
<p>TFAWS 2023 – August 21-25, 2023 17</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Transfer Line Test Module
</p>
<p>• Test:

</p>
