SAGE III Contamination Monitoring



• Why does SAGE III monitor contamination?



• The instrument optics are susceptible and sensitive to 
contamination.



• The goal of the Contamination Monitoring Packages (CMPs) is to aid 
in efforts to minimize contamination impacts to the payload and by 
extension the science data product.



• The instrument contamination window can be closed to protect the 
telescope.



• The flight computer has the ability to automatically close the 
contamination window if necessary.



• The window can be manually commanded to close.



• CMP measurements aid the SAGE III team in determining where to stow 
the contamination window for minimal exposure to contamination 
sources.

Summary
• SAGE datasets are notable for their inherent stability derived from the occultation 



technique.



• SAGE uniquely provides geophysical reference standards for vertically resolved 
stratospheric concentrations of ozone, water vapor, nitrogen dioxide, and multi-
wavelength aerosol extinction coefficient.



• SAGE III/ISS is in an excellent position to extend the uniquely valuable science 
records throughout this decade, improve their quality and bridge to future 
missions.



• SAGE III/ISS is manifested on ELC4 through 2030



• Over 60 published papers using SAGE III/ISS science data products



• Continued monitoring and sharing of the ISS external contamination data to the 
ISS community
