<p>Conclusions
</p>
<p>• Traditional population-based analyses show the presence of cognitive
</p>
<p>deficits as a trend at a group level
</p>
<p>• Percent impaired values suggest deleterious dose-effect of 56Fe in both SD
</p>
<p>and CD and 28Si in SD
</p>
<p>• Impairment due to 4He in CD at 10 cGy
</p>
<p>• Machine Learning models such as ANNs can pinpoint to the individuals at a
</p>
<p>higher risk of impairment
</p>
<p>• Predictions are based on expected dose and pre-exposure scores</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Limitations and Outlook
</p>
<p>• More behavioral tests and biomarkers should be investigated on the same 
</p>
<p>subjects
</p>
<p>• With the availability of multi-ion and/or multi-stressor data, ANNs can be 
</p>
<p>trained to attempt to predict the combined effect from single ions and single 
</p>
<p>stressors
</p>
<p>• Inter- and intra-variabilities in behavioral tests always present
</p>
<p>• More work needed for the translatability of results to human subjects</p>
