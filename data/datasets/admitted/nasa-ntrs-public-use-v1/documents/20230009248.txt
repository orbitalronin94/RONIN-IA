<p>The Bosch Process
</p>
<p>06/22/2023 2023 MSFC Jamboree 5
</p>
<p>Bosch carbon formed on metal foam pucks. Credit: 
M. B. Abney, et al., 2016.

<p>Carbon formed in pH Matter 
CFR. Credit: Z. Greenwood, 
</p>

<p>• CO2 + H2 ↔ H2O + C(s)
– Total O2 and H2 recovery
– Fe, Ni, Co catalyst
– 550-700 °C
</p>
<p>• Solid carbon product
– Fouls, degrades catalyst
– Clogs and contaminates
– Crew of 4 generates ~8 
</p>
<p>lbm of carbon per day!</p>
