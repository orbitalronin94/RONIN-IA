<p>Background
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- CE-12 is a free-jet probe calibration facility in the 
NASA Glenn Research Center Engine Research Building
- Nozzle diameter: 3.5 inches
- Mach: 0.05 – 0.95
- Total temperature: Ambient – 200oF
</p>
<p>- Typical customer types:
- 3- &amp; 5-hole flow angle probes
- Hot-wire &amp; hot-film probes
- Thermal recovery testing (RTD’s &amp; TC’s)
- Miscellaneous</p>
<p></p>

<p>Recent Thermal Recovery Tests
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Customer: 
- Type E thermocouple special-limit-of-error wire
- Direct termination to facility HyCal Reference Block 
- Accutech transmitter (AI-1500) &amp; RTD monitor junction 
</p>
<p>temperature
</p>
<p>- Facility:
- Total temperature measured in plenum upstream of nozzle
- 70:1 contraction ratio ensures near-stagnant flow in plenum
- Omega 1/10-DIN aspirated RTD probe 
</p>
<p>- 1/10-DIN ~= 0.1*(0.3 + 0.005*t) oC, where t is in oC
- Secondary Type K TC installed as sanity check
</p>
<p>- Probe tip inserted approx. 7 inches into plenum 
- Plenum I.D. is 29.2 inches
</p>
<p>Looking upstream into plenum</p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Additional Investigations
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>2. Are we transferring heat with the plenum walls, thus creating a thermal 
gradient with the plenum, which biases the plenum RTD low?
</p>
<p>- Ideas/Potential Fix: 
- Install surface-mounted TC’s to the outside of the plenum walls
</p>
<p>- Monitor wall temperatures relative to supplied air temperatures
</p>
<p>- Install 2nd Omega RTD at upstream end of plenum at same insertion depth 
- Monitor delta temperature across length of plenum to observe heat losses at 
</p>
<p>the radial location of the RTD probe tips.
</p>
<p>- Use tunnel air heater to force plenum walls to be approximately equal to incoming 
air temperatures</p>
<p></p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Additional Investigations
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>BINGO!
</p>
<p>- Incoming Air Temps to plenum: 85-88oF
- “Cold” Plenum wall temps: 80-82oF
- “Hot” Plenum walls temps: 94-100oF
</p>
<p>- Very different thermal recovery results if plenum 
walls are forced to a higher temperature.
</p>
<p>- Note: We typically thermally soak the facility for 60-90 
minutes before we take this type of data by blowing 
air at a moderately high Mach number, which is how 
we acquired the “cold” wall condition</p>
<p></p>

<p>Additional Investigations
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Trend observed in temperature 
difference between:
- Plenum wall and incoming air
- Upstream and downstream plenum RTDs 
</p>

<p>- Need to monitor plenum wall 
temperatures in additional locations 
along the plenum surface 
- OR monitor internal surface

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Recent Thermal Recovery Tests
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Successfully acquired a set of data for 
customer that follows theoretical, as-expected 
trends using plenum-wall-heating method.
</p>
<p>- Required:
- Initial heating of plenum walls to match 
</p>
<p>incoming air temperatures from central 
services (30-45 mins)
</p>
<p>- Monitor time-histories and trends in 
temperatures and delta temperatures
</p>
<p>- Reheat plenum walls, if necessary, as the 
day goes on and/or central services 
switches compressors, dryer beds, etc.</p>
<p></p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Improvement Plans
</p>
<p>National Aeronautics and Space Administration
</p>
<p>- Improvement #1: Circumferentially insulate plenum walls with 
~1.5” of insulation, not including end-caps of plenum. 
- The walls of the plenum are always trying to reject the heat to the room 
</p>
<p>(test cell room stays around 80F, typically).
</p>
<p>- Improvement #2: Use a Kaye 170 Ice-Point reference to improve 
quality of customer thermocouple measurements
- HyCal Reference Block temperature gradient/stability concerns
</p>
<p>- Improvement #3: Perform heater tuning runs during upcoming 
IST runs to improve the speed and accuracy of heater use.
- Current PID coefficients are [1,1,0]; it works but not efficiently
- Primary control valve just replaced to improve Mach number automated 
</p>
<p>control and stability, thus requiring IST for valve tuning, as well.</p>
