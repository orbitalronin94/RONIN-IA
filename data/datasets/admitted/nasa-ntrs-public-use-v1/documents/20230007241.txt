<p>Mission
Mature solar sail propulsion technology to enable near-term, 
compelling space missions for NASA and the nation.
</p>
<p>Mission Technology Goals
Demonstrate solar sail propulsion technology to enable near- and mid-
term missions in cis-lunar space, sun-L1 halo orbits, non-Keplerian and 
other planetary orbits
• Maneuver without regret capability in the Earth/Moon system
• Space Domain Awareness
• Increased Space Weather warning times 
• Sustained in-situ measurements within the Earth's magnetotail
• High inclination solar orbital observations
</p>

<p>Solar Cruiser Mission Overview
</p>
<p>2
</p>
<p>Solar Cruiser was to launch as a secondary payload on the NASA IMAP mission in 2025. 
Using a solar sail, it would have cruised toward the sun and demonstrate the ability of a 
solar sail to remain stationary along a line from the Sun to the Earth for long periods of 
time. 
</p>
<p>Due to a technical problem with the spacecraft and insufficient schedule margin 
to accommodate required changes, the mission was not confirmed for flight.</p>
<p></p>

<p>Near-Term Sail Application: Advanced Warning of Solar Storms
</p>
<p>(Space Weather)
</p>
<p>Cis-Lunar
</p>
<p>Station keeping sunward of Earth/Sun L1
</p>
<p>CME arrives at the sailcraft which 
</p>
<p>then sends an alert by radio
</p>
<p>Alerts can be 
</p>
<p>sent to cis-
</p>
<p>Lunar ahead 
</p>
<p>of and during 
</p>
<p>the storm
</p>
<p>An instrumented sailcraft would 
increase solar storm warning times by 
50% for both “typical” and “fast” 
Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) from ~55 
min. to 83 min., and 20 min. to 30 min.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Near-Term Solar Sails in Cis-Lunar Space
</p>
<p>Enhanced Communications, Position, Navigation, and Timing
</p>
<p>• Enable station keeping in orbits that allow 
</p>
<p>extended viewing of lunar hemispheric polar 
</p>
<p>regions to enhance communication (between 
</p>
<p>lunar surface assets and with Earth), position, 
</p>
<p>navigation, and timing as well as surface 
</p>
<p>observations and operations
</p>
<p>• Enable near-continuous observation and 
</p>
<p>communications from lunar farside
</p>
<p>• Can cycle between lunar Lagrange points 
</p>
<p>and the near-rectilinear halo orbit (Gateway) 
</p>
<p>without propellant limit
Can cycle between lunar Lagrange points 
and the near-rectilinear halo 
orbit (Gateway) with trip times of 4 – 6 
days.​</p>
