• Baseline models established for the B1900D, ATR 42-600, Saab 340B, 
and DHC-7-100
• Serve as benchmarks for future EAP technology sensitivity studies for EPFD



• Demonstration of Systems Analysis capabilities of coupled uncertainty 
propagation and M&S analysis
• Parametric reference models and methodology will allow for robust analysis 



of EPFD concepts with EAP



• Future studies will work on performance assessment of hybrid and 
fully-electrified aircraft concepts
• Systems Analysis tools such as GASP have been modified to conduct analysis 



of EAP-enabled configurations
