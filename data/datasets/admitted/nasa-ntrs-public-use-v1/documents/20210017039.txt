What is a Halbach Array?

• Arrangement of permanent magnets 
that results in a high –strength 
magnetic field on one side and a 
lower magnetic field strength on the 
other [4]

• Useful for maximizing field strength 
inside the nozzle, and reducing 
interaction with the interstellar 
medium

• Want to extend Halbach array from 
permanent magnets to wires
