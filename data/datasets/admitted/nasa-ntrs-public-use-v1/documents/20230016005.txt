POWER
The key commodity needed to utilize the Lunar surface



Illumination
The scarce resource needed to produce power



Polar Illumination Limits
• Intermittent with up to 3-day periods of continuous darkness
• Highly dependent on location/elevation



Equatorial Illumination Limits
• Cyclical periods of 14 days illuminated, 14 days (340 hours) dark
• Consistent
