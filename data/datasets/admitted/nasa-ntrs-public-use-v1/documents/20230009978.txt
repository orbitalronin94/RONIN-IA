<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>The Need for Universal Lunar Surface Power
</p>
<p>• Lunar surface operations will grow and expand
– Power demands will exceed elements 
</p>
<p>capability - Self sufficient power is not feasible
– Systems will have to be integrated together or 
</p>
<p>to a power grid (shared access)
</p>
<p>• Power over long-distance 
– Driven by minimum safe landing distance and 
</p>
<p>radiation stand off (1km+)
– Needed for power in permanently shadowed 
</p>
<p>regions (PSR)
</p>
<p>• Dissimilar power sources
– Increase power availability – provide power 
</p>
<p>during longer eclipse periods
– Contingency scenarios - Provide needed 
</p>
<p>redundancy and reliability</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Microgrid Definition and Interface Converter for Planetary Surfaces (MIPS)
</p>
<p>• Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD)
– Game Changing Development (GCD) Program
</p>
<p>• Project goal is to develop technology to provide 
universal access to power
</p>
<p>– Define Lunar Power System Architecture
• Power type and voltage level
• Power strategy / CONOPS
</p>
<p>– Develop and Demonstrate Converter
• Universal Modular Interface Converter 
</p>
<p>(UMIC)
• Define interface to help guide standards 
</p>
<p>and future technology development efforts</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Lunar Power Grid
</p>
<p>• Benefits of a power grid
– Flexibility, evolvability, and 
</p>
<p>reconfiguration
– Optimal dispatch of power sources and 
</p>
<p>energy storage to service loads &amp; 
enhance reliability
</p>
<p>– Systematic integration of new sources 
and loads
</p>
<p>– Common grid interface 
(commonality)
</p>
<p>– Allows for the deployment of future 
loads that do not need to carry their 
own power generation </p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Lunar Power System Architecture
</p>
<p>5</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Baseline Architecture Design Requirements 
</p>
<p>6
</p>
<p>Power Sources:
Solar Arrays 
• Vertical Solar Array Technologies (VSAT)
• 10 kW increments
• 120 VDC Source
Fission Surface Power (FSP)
• 40 kW Power
• Voltage TBD (AC)
• Must be located more than 1km from other 
</p>
<p>lunar elements
</p>
<p>Lunar Elements (Loads): 
Surface Habitat
• 20 kW during habitation (must be during insolation)
• 2 kW during  eclipse and non‐habitation
In‐Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)
• ISRU processing ‐ 46 kW insolation / 9 kW eclipse
</p>
<p>• Located on crater rim (in the sun)
• Excavation ‐ 22 kW insolation / 4 kW eclipse
</p>
<p>• Located in permanently shadowed regions (water‐ice)
• About 3km – 5km from processing facility</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Notional Reference Artemis Base Camp Microgrid
</p>
<p>• Trade studies focused on primary power 
distribution system (transmission)
</p>
<p>– Architecture (radial, ring, mesh)
– Power type (AC vs DC)
– Voltage: (600V – 6 kV)
– Data contains estimated mass of converters 
</p>
<p>+ cables
• Results
</p>
<p>– Increasing voltage decreases mass
– AC vs DC at a single voltage is marginal 
</p>
<p>• Especially higher voltages
– Technology limitations need to considered
</p>
<p>• Max DC: 1.5 kV
– Rad hard limitations
</p>
<p>• Max AC: none known
</p>
<p>7
</p>
<p>Radial Architecture
</p>
<p>Recommendation: 3kVAC, 3‐phase, 1000 Hz</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Universal Modular Interface Converter
</p>
<p>8</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Universal Modular Interface Converter for a Lunar Surface Grid
</p>
<p>• Lunar grid can provide key benefits to users of 
lunar surface power system
</p>
<p>– Power sharing for improved power 
reliability/availability
</p>
<p>• FSP power available after 1 year demo
• Reduce overall energy storage mass by 
</p>
<p>providing night-time power (FSP)
– Can connect new assets that do not carry 
</p>
<p>their own power
</p>
<p>• The UMIC can connect lunar assets to the grid
– Power sources (solar arrays, FSP)
– Energy storage (batteries, fuel cells)
– Loads
</p>
<p>• Can be standalone product or integrated into 
other source or load equipment 
</p>
<p>9
</p>
<p>UMIC</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Universal Modular Interface Converter Overview
</p>
<p>• Universal Modular Interface Converter
– Modular, bi-directional converter
</p>
<p>• Grid side: 3-phase, 3000 VAC, 1000 Hz
• Load/Source Side: 120 VDC
</p>
<p>– Full rack capable of 10 kW of power (nominal)
• 12 kW peak
</p>
<p>– Parallelable - can provide more than 10 kW at 
single location
</p>
<p>– Design goals:
• &gt;95% efficiency
• 350 W/kg power density (est. for flight)
</p>
<p>– Capability:
• Grid forming
• Grid syncing
</p>
<p>10</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>UMIC 1 kW Modular Power Electronics Details
</p>
<p>• 1 kW Inverter Module plus 1 kW DC Switch Module
– Inverter: Converts 120 VDC microgrid bus to
</p>
<p>UMIC internal low voltage 40 VAC bus
• Includes filters, power control logic, AC isolation
• Consists of 5 main subsystems in a stacked card form factor
</p>
<p>– DC Switch: Provides DC-side current limiting &amp; isolation capability
</p>

<p>Use cases of UMIC / potential applications
</p>
<p>UMIC can serve as interface converter between variety of space power assets
</p>
<p>17
</p>
<p>AC
• Point-to-point AC transmission system
• AC power grid
• AC loads
</p>
<p>– Motors
– Heaters
</p>
<p>DC
• Stationary DC loads
</p>
<p>– Habitat
– In Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)
</p>
<p>• Rover or rover charger
• Power plant
</p>
<p>– Solar array
– Fission Surface Power (rectified DC interface)
</p>
<p>• Energy storage element
– Battery
– Regenerative fuel cell (RFC)</p>
