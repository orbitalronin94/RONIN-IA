Cryogenic Propulsion Systems: A MSFC Strategic Imperative


The NASA strategic goals and initiatives rely heavily on cryogenic systems


Space Technology Mission Directorate (STMD) Strategic Framework – Go, Land, Live, Explore


Go – Cryogenic Fluid Management, Space Nuclear Propulsion, Advanced Propulsion


Land – Entry, Descent, and Landing; Large-Scale Lunar and Mars Global Access


Live – In-Situ Resource Utilization; Thermal Management Systems


Explore – Small Spacecraft Technologies


NASA Flagship Missions have historically been and continue to be dependent upon cryogenic system solutions


Human Lander System


Space Launch System


Space Nuclear Propulsion


MSFC is heavily committed to ensuring the success of these missions and the development of the associated mission-enabling technologies

MATCH Overview


Initially provides capability for liquid nitrogen and hydrogen, options for other cryogens in the future with minimal hardware change


All components LOX compatible, but lack appropriate cleanliness


Three dedicated flow legs


High Flow: Up to 250 GPM (LN2)


Medium Flow: Up to 125 GPM (LN2)


Low Flow: Up to 30 GPM (LN2)


Designed to interface with longstanding MSFC test facilities
