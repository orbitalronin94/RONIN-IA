<div class="slide-content"><p>Space Force Lightning Detection Capabilities</p>
<p>The New Mesoscale Eastern Range Lightning Information Network (MERLIN)</p>
<p>10 sensor locations + input from National Lightning Detection Network</p>
<p>45th Weather Squadron/Launch Weather Officer gives MERLIN data while on console</p>
<p>Gives coordinates and lightning magnitude</p>
<p>Studies have concluded that MERLIN has difficulty determining attachment location to tall structures</p>
