<p>Human-Autonomy Trust Scale Development
</p>
<p>4</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p> Google Forms to administer 125-item survey
</p>
<p> Included trust-related items applied to multiple autonomous systems (e.g., GPS 
when driving or AI tools) and demographics
</p>
<p> Amazon’s Mechanical Turk (MTurk) to recruit and pay participants 
</p>
<p> 400 participant goal (US adults, HIT (Human Intelligence Task) approval rate &gt;95%)
</p>
<p> Incorporated recommended practices to improve participant attentiveness 
</p>
<p> Randomly placed attention check items: “For this question select number seven.”
</p>
<p> Initial warning in instructions: “response patterns will be monitored, and any 
indications of random responding will result in no compensation”
</p>
<p> Limit length: time to complete around 15 minutes
</p>
<p> Progression indicator to improve attrition 
</p>
