Human-Autonomy Trust Scale Development



4











 Google Forms to administer 125-item survey



 Included trust-related items applied to multiple autonomous systems (e.g., GPS 
when driving or AI tools) and demographics



 Amazon’s Mechanical Turk (MTurk) to recruit and pay participants 



 400 participant goal (US adults, HIT (Human Intelligence Task) approval rate >95%)



 Incorporated recommended practices to improve participant attentiveness 



 Randomly placed attention check items: “For this question select number seven.”



 Initial warning in instructions: “response patterns will be monitored, and any 
indications of random responding will result in no compensation”



 Limit length: time to complete around 15 minutes



 Progression indicator to improve attrition
