● Pressure gain combustion can be used to realize a 
step-increase in combustor efficiency due to higher 
thermal power density 

o Detailed characterization of injector dynamics, which 
directly influences operability and performance, has 
seldom been reported, especially with liquid fueled RDEs

● Multiphase nature of liquid fueled RDEs introduces 
additional complexity to the RDE flow-field

o Injection, breakup, atomization, and vaporization

o Timescales for these processes impact net reaction 
rates, detonation peak pressure, and velocity

● Diagnostics applied to a relevant canonical liquid 
injector with appropriate (detonative) forcing can play 
an important role in the development of liquid-fueled 
RDEs

● Key objective of the proposed study is to investigate 
the injection, mixing, break-up of the liquid fuel jet in 
an optically accessible linear detonation combustor.

Experiment Overview

Summary of Operating Conditions

● Linear semi-bounded channel that supports self-
sustained self-excited detonation wave generation and 
propagation

• Operated with natural gas (~90% CH4) and 
gaseous O2

● Representative test sequence is depicted by dynamic 
pressure plot

o High-amplitude steep-fronted waves observed in 
~1-2 ms post-ignition

o ~8 kHz wave frequency comparable to typical 
rocket RDE operation

● Single point liquid diesel injection during steady portion 
of the test

• Tests performed with varying liquid fuel injection 
pressures

• Operating parameters for the NG-O2 combustor 
maintained between tests

12/12/2022 5

Results

● Liquid jet trajectory significantly modified by detonation wave 

● Deflection in the leeward direction upon wave impact followed by windward tilt after wave passage

● Fuel injection always present, albeit with a slower recovery at low injection pressures



12/12/2022 6

Results

● Liquid jet trajectory significantly modified from detonation wave 

● Jet remains fairly vertical after wave passage as injection pressure increases

● Faster jet recovery with increased liquid manifold pressure



12/12/2022 7

Results

● Jet deflection is minimal after wave passage, with jet remaining mostly vertical

● Liquid column appears to remain more intact immediately after wave impact

12/12/2022 9

Summary

● The impact of periodic detonation wave impact on a 
liquid fuel jet is investigated in a linear detonation 
combustor.

● Dynamic injection and break-up of a single diesel jet 
injected into the combustion chamber at varying 
injection pressures is evaluated with 
chemiluminescence, fuel-PLIF and Mie scattering 
measurements at 100 kHz

● Liquid jet recovery closely corresponds to detonation 
wave amplitude in the chamber and injection 
pressure
