For Public Release



Future Demand of SMD 
external payloads on CLDs
SMD does not guarantee use of CLDs for its missions. SMD 
competitively selects superlative science missions in a 
platform-agnostic manner. Thus, CLDs compete with other 
platforms such as airplanes, balloons, free-flying satellites, 
spacecraft-as-a-service, etc., in the pursuit of the best 
science. If CLD were the most conducive platform for the 
science mission, then, overall cost effectiveness, required 
spacecraft functions (power, communications, thermal 
management, etc.), ease of interface, etc., are important 
factors on the decision to select a specific platform over 
another.











14



For Public Release



Field of View



For SMD External Payloads which are competitively 
selected and PI-led, FOV requirements are dependent on 
science requirements of these selected competed missions. 
In general, there isn’t a firm requirement for unobstructed 
FOV. For all SMD missions, unobstructed nadir and zenith 
field of view that accommodates star trackers, GPS, and 
offset pointers are generally desired. 











15



For Public Release



Communications



SMD competitively selects PI-led missions including 
potential external CLD payloads. As such, for those 
payloads, specific data requirements for SMD external 
payloads have not been identified. The RFI has provided the 
data rate and data volume requirements. Higher data 
bandwidth and rates enable a broader range of science 
investigations.











16



For Public Release



Schedule for Utilization of Airlock 
and SmallSat Deployment



Many SMD Missions are competitively selected. We cannot 
forecast a utilization schedule for external CLD resources or 
SmallSat Deployment. For SmallSat deployments, a location 
that minimizes re-contact with the platform is desired. 
Utilization of deployment of SmallSats on CLDs will depend 
on cost effectiveness. 











17



For Public Release



Power



SMD does not require specific power or require any power 
conversions; however, consistent power and knowledge of 
requirements and capabilities for payloads is required. 
Keep Alive power requirements vary from mission to 
mission and in general, 300W is sufficient for most SMD 
missions now.
