Do we need to worry about microbes leaking from our suits and habitats?


BACKGROUND


Some organisms can survive exposure to space!





We also know that all crewed, pressurized volumes will leak or vent 


Cyano-bacteria, lichen and fungi survived up to 500+ days outside ISS 


Tardigrades survived extended ISS exposure…and then reproduced


ISSUE 


But we don’t know what’s actually leaking/venting from our current systems, how long those organisms could survive, or how far they may travel under destination conditions





Does proximity to a warm spacecraft matter?


The answers will drive element design (i.e. closed vs. open ECLS), where we place elements, and who/how we collect science samples


How close can crew get without compromising science? 





How far could our hitchhikers spread?
