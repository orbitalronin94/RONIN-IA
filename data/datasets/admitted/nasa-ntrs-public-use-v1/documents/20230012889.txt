<p>Liquid Hydrogen Challenges
</p>
<p>- Not possible to prevent air contamination or leakage (it will never be zero)
o Control the order of magnitude and the location
</p>
<p>- Define reasonable maintenance requirements and implementations
</p>
<p>- Aircraft cryogenic hydrogen Storage &amp; Distribution:
o Keep hydrogen from boiling-off in a wide variety of environments.
</p>
<p>o Get the hydrogen balance of plant to work in a wide variety of environments.
</p>
<p>- Cryogenic Hydrogen Production &amp; Distribution enable the aircraft
</p>
<p>- Safety:
o How can people interact with the hydrogen systems 
</p>
<p>(airport workers, passengers, maintenance, etc)?
</p>
<p>o How does the system respond in failure scenarios?</p>
<p></p>

o While spray foam is light and easy to install [incorrectly], it’s poor thermal performance drives

<p>Fuel Cell Technology Status
</p>
<p>- No current fuel cell technology meets all requirements
o No viable test data at scale
</p>
<p>o Low power density
</p>
<p>o Challenging thermal management
</p>
<p>- Multiple fuel cell technologies under development
o Low-Temperature PEM near-term potential option
</p>
<p>o High-Temperature PEM under development
</p>
<p>o Solid Oxide under development for co-generation with combustion systems
</p>
<p>- System integration development required</p>
<p></p>

</p>

<p>Hybrid Power System Analysis
</p>
<p>- Stand-alone power module contains 
battery subsystem, fuel cell stack, and 
fuel cell balance-of-plant (BoP)
</p>
<p>- Other systems manage aircraft power 
levels, hydrogen storage, management, 
and distribution, thermal management
</p>
<p>- Fuel cell stack assembly likely to include 
multiple sub-stacks to minimize power 
electronics
