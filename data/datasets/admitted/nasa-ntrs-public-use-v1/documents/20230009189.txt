<div class="slide-content"><p>Background</p>
<p>Energy Storage Challenges for Habitation</p>
<p>Initial allocations for shadow period survival challenges delivery capability</p>
<p>Mass: Habitable elements often struggle meeting vehicle delivery performance</p>
<p>Volume: Smaller habitable elements struggle to fit energy storage within volume envelopes</p>
<p>Potential architectural shifts to include multi-regional exploration </p>
<p>Driven by desire for significant surface exploration</p>
<p>Leverages relocatable/mobile habitable elements</p>
<p>NASA Lunar Power Sub-Architecture Trade requires habitation perspective</p>
<p>Emerging external power system investments (i.e., Vertical Solar Array Technology (VSAT)) </p>
<p>May change habitation power architecture</p>
<p>Fission Surface Power excluded in initial study</p>
<p>Differing element energy storage requirements necessitate closer investigation of energy storage assumptions</p>
<p>Surface Habitat (SH) concept protects 100 hr darkness, necessitating optimal placement in few locations</p>
<p>Multi-purpose Habitat (MPH) and mobile elements protect 150 hr darkness</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Goal and Objectives</p>
<p>Define scope of lunar surface habitation power needs</p>
<p>Summarize previous lunar power architecture studies &amp; assumptions </p>
<p>Assess current and near-future power generation &amp; energy storage technologies</p>
<p>Better quantify maximum expected terrain shadowing periods &amp; variability for lunar south pole </p>
<p>Determine the effects of mobility on power architectures (Element and/or external power system mobility)</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Assessment Processes</p>
<p>Power System Technology Assessment</p>
<p>Capture near-term power generation &amp; storage options</p>
<p>Establish Figures of Merit (FOMs) </p>
<p>Gather state-of-the art quantitative FOMs for each option</p>
<p>Quantify South Pole lighting availability</p>
<p>Capture &amp; discuss qualitative FOMs</p>
<p>Assess each option against incremental power generation &amp; energy storage levels </p>
<p>Power generation increments of 2 kW ranging 2-20 kW</p>
<p>Energy storage increments supporting 80-340 hrs of darkness and 2-20 kW power loads</p>
<p>Intent is to identify if preferred solution changes at given power or energy storage levels</p>
<p>Capture future power generation &amp; storage options</p>
<p></p>
<p>Power Integration Assessment</p>
<p>Identify MPH &amp; SH power loads for minimal crewed mission functionality</p>
<p>Identify MPH &amp; SH power loads for maximum crewed mission functionality</p>
<p>Identify potential external power solutions </p>
<p>Outline planned development timeframes &amp; highlight developmental risks</p>
<p>Identify possible mass/volume savings with external power</p>
<p>Assess opportunities for power sharing with other elements</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Assessment Ground Rules &amp; Assumptions</p>
<p>Assume lunar south pole region (~&gt;83°S) and associated illumination considerations</p>
<p>Region of focus for initial Artemis missions, with notional exploration regions aligning with Artemis III candidate landing sites</p>
<p>Power architecture will support habitat lifetime of 10 yrs </p>
<p>This may not be achievable with some systems, requiring logistical replacement/refurbishment which should be captured</p>
<p>Assume crewed operations do not nominally occur during darkness periods</p>
<p>May be considered as a future capability with augmentation</p>
<p>Power architecture will consider average/nominal power loads</p>
<p>Peak &amp; variable loading should be analyzed as a future assessment through modeling &amp; simulation</p>
<p>Energy storage will be sized for uncrewed operational power loads during darkness </p>
<p>Supplementary external power may be considered</p>
<p>Minimum standalone power generation and energy storage needs will be integrated with habitation element </p>
<p>Ability to scale the power architecture supporting habitation should be considered</p>
<p>Accommodates evolving mission needs (i.e, future inclusion of regenerative environmental control and life support systems (ECLSS))</p>
<p>Highlight technologies likely to support 2029-2030 delivery to lunar surface</p>
<p>Some technologies may carry higher development risk, which should be captured</p>
