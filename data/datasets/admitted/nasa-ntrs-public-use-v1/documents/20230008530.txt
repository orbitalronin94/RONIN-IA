<p>Support Vector Machine (SVM)
</p>
<p>• Identifies an optimal hyperplane that maximizes the 
</p>
<p>separation margin between the two classes
</p>
<p>• Uses kernel function for nonlinearly-separated
</p>
<p>classes
</p>
<p>• Training involves minimization of the 
</p>
<p>cost (error) function
</p>
<p>• Training involves adjusting/tuning hyperparameters
</p>
<p>- penalty parameter
</p>
<p>- parameter that controls the tradeoff between
</p>
<p>error and separation margin</p>
