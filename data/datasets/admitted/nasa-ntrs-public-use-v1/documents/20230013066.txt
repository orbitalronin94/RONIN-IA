ML for Automatic Flaw Detection in AFP
Goals:



• Automated highlighting of areas where 
further inspection is required. 



Challenges



• Other image analysis method performed 
poorly (e.g. edge detection, clustering).



• Thermal discontinuities are not uniform in 
size, shape, or any characteristic.



• Thermography is, by nature a diffusive 
process, meaning discontinuities lack 
defined edges.



• “Clean” data with clear flaws that can be 
used for training are difficult to come by. 



ML Approach:



• Manually segmented images to make 
a training dataset. 



• Trained a UNet to segment thermal 
images for manufacturing flaws.
