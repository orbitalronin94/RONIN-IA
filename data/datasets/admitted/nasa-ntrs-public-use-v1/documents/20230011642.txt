<div class="slide-content"><p>The flexible thermal protection system (FTPS) was developed to enable large inflatable heatshields, which can land higher mass than existing heatshield technologies</p>
<p>FPTS consists of 3 layers: external woven ceramic fabric, middle layer of insulators, internal gas barrier</p>
<p>In Nov. 2022, the Low-Earth Orbit Flight Test (LOFTID) project demonstrated re-entry of 6-meter (~20 ft.) inflatable aeroshell from LEO</p>
<p>FTPS and trajectory design utilized a probabilistic margin process to reduce over-margining and ensure significantly severe thermal response for model correlation and material proofing</p>
