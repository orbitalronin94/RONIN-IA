Conclusions and Future Work



• For this application and set of assumptions



1. Mechanically geared is shown to be lightest but benefit decays as torque is reduced



2. Direct drive is shown to be the most efficient due to low motor electrical frequencies



3. Magnetic gears require design improvement or reduced margin relative to nominal power to be 



competitive



• Future Work:



1. Incorporate reliability models in the design optimization 



2. Integration of explicit control response requirements to eliminate torque margin assumptions



3. Expand studies to different applications, technologies, and power levels



4. Explore other possible methods of improving magnetic gear performance beyond just reducing 



airgap
