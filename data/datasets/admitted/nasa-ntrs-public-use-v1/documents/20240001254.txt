<body><div class="slide-content"><p>Solid-state Architecture Batteries for Enhanced Rechargeability and Safety (SABERS)</p>
<p>Beyond Li-Ion: Technology to Enable Sustainable Electric Aviation</p>
<p></p>
<p>Challenge</p>
<p>Electric aviation will become a revolutionary leap in sustainable aviation</p>
<p>Current battery technology does not meet the strict aerospace performance and safety metrics</p>
<p>Can we exceed 400 Wh/kg in specific energy and operate safely at a rate of 1C and beyond?</p>
<p>A Combined Experimental and Computational Approach</p>
<p>Expected Impacts</p>
<p>Expand the missions served by electric aviation with a safe battery operating at twice specific energy of current SOA Li-ion batteries</p>
<p>Reduce waste in the environment through recyclability: recovering solid lithium and sulfur components, and reprocessing the electrolyte</p>
<p>Reduce waste in the environment by using a waste byproduct of oil refining, Sulfur - the active component in SABERS cells</p>
<p>Partners and/or  Participants</p>
<p>-    NASA Glenn Research Center (GRC) </p>
<p>NASA Langley Research Center (LaRC)</p>
<p>NASA Ames Research Center (ARC)</p>
<p> Argonne National Lab</p>
<p> Pacific Northwest National Lab</p>
<p> Georgia Tech University</p>
<p></p>
<p>Solutions</p>
<p>Develop a solid-state bipolar battery stack based on novel Li-S/Se chemistry and a non-flammable electrolyte to produce a battery that enables sustainable electric aviation</p>
<p>Design SABERS cells to exceed 500 Wh/kg, operate up to 150oC, are 1 to 4C capable, and inherently non-flammable</p>
<p>Satisfy system level needs such as non-flammability, energy density, discharge rate, packaging, and scalability</p>
<p>Results</p>
<p>Improved design optimization with computational modeling of the cathode microstructure based on experimental results</p>
<p>Achieved electrochemical-level specific energy of greater than 500 Wh/kg in coin cells</p>
<p>Achieved high-temperature (up to 150oC), high rate (up to 4C pulse; ultrahigh current density &gt;30 mA/cm2) all-solid-state cells</p>
<p>Demonstrated successful scale-up to small format pouch cells</p>
<p>Investigated various processing techniques for pouch cells</p>
<p>Demonstrated feasibility of fabricating bipolar stack coin cells</p>
<p></p>
<p>Next Steps</p>
<p>Employ ultra-thin reinforced sulfide and polymer solid electrolytes (SE)</p>
<p>Improve cycle life by Li-anode and solid electrolyte interface engineering</p>
<p>Improve reproducibility of pouch cells to increase production rate</p>
<p>Fabricate SABERS bipolar stack pouches</p>
<p>POC: Rocco Viggiano, GRC  Co-PI: Yi Lin, LaRC and Donald Dornbusch, GRC</p>
<p>Motivation and Solution</p>
<p>High Performance and Scale-Up</p>
