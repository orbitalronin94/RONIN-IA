<p>Digital Engineering
Definition
</p>
<p>“An integrated digital approach that uses authoritative sources of systems data and models as a continuum across 
</p>
<p>disciplines to support lifecycle activities from concept through disposal”. [1]
</p>
<p>Leverages model-based engineering (MBE), model-based systems engineering (MBSE), model-based mission 
</p>
<p>assurance (MBMA), MB Anything (MBx), digital thread, digital twin, Semantic Reasoning, etc.
</p>
<p>Model-Based Anything - Employ digital models across any/all functional domains to enable our people to address 
</p>
<p>increasing complexity, scope, speed, uncertainty &amp; changes. 
</p>
<p>Purpose
</p>
<p>To better manage the growing complexity of systems and of their development and operations by linking information 
</p>
<p>sources and analysis processes that were otherwise Stove-piped [2]
</p>
<p>Authoritative Source of Truth
</p>
<p>Document to Data and/or Model Centric 
Modeling</p>
<p></p>
