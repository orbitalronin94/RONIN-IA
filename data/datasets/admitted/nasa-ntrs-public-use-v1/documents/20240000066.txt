<p>LOFTID Concept of Operations
</p>
<p> RV Launches off on Atlas V 
with JPSS-2
</p>
<p> Centaur performs orbit 
adjustment burn

</p>

• Payload Adapter Canister deployed prior to 
</p>
<p>start of LOFTID mission

 6x Constant Force Springs to provide positive force 
</p>

</p>
<p>aeroshell

</p>
<p>PLA and stowed aeroshell
