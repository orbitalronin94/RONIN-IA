Twin sister of Apollo and goddess of the 

Moon in Greek mythology, Artemis is the 

torch-bringer personifying our path to the 

Moon. During the next era of human 

exploration, we will discover life-saving, 

Earth-changing science and technology 

along the way.

NASA’s goal is to land the first woman 

and first person of color on the Moon 

and return them safely to Earth. When 

the Artemis astronauts land on the lunar 

surface, they will step into the future, 

bringing all of humanity with them.

A SAFE RETURN

Survive Extreme Heating!
The capsule must survive 
external temperatures as high as 
5,000 oF! That’s half the surface 
temperature of the Sun!!!!

Our heat shield technology helps 
keep the inside of the capsule 
(where the humans will live!) in 
the mid-70 oF

This is the largest ablative 
heatshield in the world at 16.5 ft 
in diameter!



A SAFE RETURN
