<p>Introduction
</p>
<p>• Filling a cryogenic tank in deep space is challenging because  liquid propellant 
may not settle at the tank bottom causing loss of propellant due to venting
</p>
<p>• There are several No Vent Fill Methods to reduce or eliminate loss of propellant 
in space
</p>
<p>• Charge – Hold – Vent
</p>
<p>• Vented Chill &amp; No Vent Fill
</p>
<p>• No Vent Fill using TVS assisted injector
</p>
<p>• TVS assisted No Vent Fill is advantageous for its operational simplicity compared 
to other methods
</p>
<p>• This paper presents Numerical Simulation of Experimental Test of Chilling and 
Filling of CRYOTE tank with a TVS assisted Injector  with 2D axisymmetric GFSSP 
model  
</p>

<p>Concluding Remarks
</p>
<p>• Nodal modeling of no vent filling of a cryogenic tank using a TVS augmented injector has been 
presented.
</p>
<p>• Model accounts for different regimes of pool boiling heat transfer during tank chilldown.
</p>
<p>• Model also accounts for condensation at liquid-ullage interface, around spray droplet and liquid-
vapor interface.
</p>
<p>• Numerical model predictions were compared with measured data from a tank filling experiment 
conducted in a vacuum chamber for a small flight tank using liquid nitrogen.
</p>
<p>• Both 1D and 2D model predictions of the same experimental configuration were compared with 
the test data. 
</p>
<p>• The discrepancy in tank pressure and filling rate predictions are within 10% and 5% respectively.
</p>
<p>• 2D model predictions did not show any substantial improvement in accuracy over 1D model. 
However, 2D model allows use of more realistic droplet diameter and does not require any 
adjustment of heat transfer coefficient.
</p>
<p>• More validation is necessary to establish the applicability of this methodology by simulating the 
filling of larger tank with different fluid.  
</p>
