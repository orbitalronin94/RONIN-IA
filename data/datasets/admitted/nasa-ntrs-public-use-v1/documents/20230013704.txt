<p>2 - Introduction
Hardware-in-the-loop simulation: Objectives
</p>
<p>Hybrid simulation of a generic robotic space intervention system in 1-g environment with:
</p>
<p>Actual hardware for manipulation task with a suitable industrial robot
A real-time dynamic simulator for simulating the space manipulator kinematics and dynamics,
in its own space environment (on-orbit, zero-g, etc.)
</p>
<p>Possibility of choosing which capabilities of the space robotic system are to be simulated or
replicated in hardware
</p>
<p>Reduce cost of on-Earth test/validation/verification of a space robotic systems by pushing to
real-time dynamic simulations the aspects difficult to replicate on Earth (e.g. simulating the
effect of thrust on a long flexible manipulator)
</p>
<p>Perform a parameterized performance study of a space robotic mission
</p>
<p>Discover the benefits, limitations and sensitivities of the robotic system to known performance
contributors such as properties of the robot controller and sensors
</p>
<p>Verify the performance sensitivity to key hardware and software characteristics.
</p>
<p>Earlier steps: Use Monte Carlo simulation methods to predict robot arm performance against
selected hardware and software characteristics
</p>
<p>Later steps: validate these Monte Carlo predictions on robot hardware</p>
<p></p>

<p>4 - Real-Time Dynamic Simulator
Real-time simulation of flexibility
</p>
<p>Flexibility with augmented model (+ 1 DoF per joint)
</p>
<p>Elasticity is reduced at the joint level, along the torque trans-
mission path between the motor and the joint
</p>
<p>Dynamic model is augmented by adding extra branches
</p>
<p>This is equivalent to attaching each motor to the original joint
through a spring/mass/damper
Total degrees of freedom doubles
</p>
<p>The ”spring” acts only on the axis of the motor; every other
part of the structure is supposed rigid
</p>
<p>This model assumes the dominant action is the elasticity of the
torque transmission between the motor and the joint
</p>
<p>This model requires only 50µs to perform one update step on
a 7-DoF robot</p>
<p></p>

<p>5 - Contact Forces Extractor
Dynamic Parameters Estimation
</p>
<p>The Force/Torque sensor simulator needs to know the dynamic parameters of its load.
</p>
<p>Dynamic Parameters Estimator objectives:
</p>
<p>Identifies dynamic parameters of a robot end-effector
</p>
<p>Estimates velocities and accelerations of the end effector center of mass using an Extended
Kalman Filter (EKF)
</p>
<p>Estimates the externally applied forces and moments acting on the end effector using the
EKF
</p>
<p>Primary use: contact forces isolation in a 1-G environment for the HIL simulator
</p>
<p>How it works:
</p>
<p>Inputs: 12-DoF sensor data (linear/angular accelerations, forces and moments)
</p>
<p>Inputs: end effector pose w.r.t the robot base, which is provided by the robot controller
</p>
<p>A mathematical model of the end effector dynamics developed using Maple CAS provides
a solution for the F/T sensor inertial forces
</p>
<p>Simulated inertial forces are subtracted from the F/T readings to isolate the contact forces</p>
<p></p>

<p>8 - Conclusions
</p>
<p>Investigated feasibility of an hybrid hardware-in-the-loop simulation environment of a generic
robotic space intervention system in 1-g environment with:
</p>
<p>Actual hardware for manipulation task with a suitable industrial robot
A real-time dynamic simulator for simulating the space manipulator kinematics and dynamics,
in its own space environment (on-orbit, zero-g, etc.)
</p>
<p>Major challenge: implementation of real-time dynamics
</p>
<p>Approach seems promising; this solution allowed us assess several possible problems such as
tele-manipulation in zero-g in case of large and flexible manipulators
</p>
<p>Cost of the simulation environment was greatly reduced with respect the adoption of large
mechanical platforms
</p>
<p>The optimized implementation is suitable for advanced statistical analysis such as a parameter
sensitivity analysis that requires a very large number of simulation runs</p>
