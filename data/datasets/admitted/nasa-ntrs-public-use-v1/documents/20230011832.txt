• Windows Material Database
– A database that provides flight relevant data to designers, allowing for quick trade studies 



between transparent materials.
• The database contains 4 types of materials: Acrylics, Polycarbonates, Glasses, and Ceramics (various 



brands), and each is tested using the same method.
– Acrylic (AC) brands



» GKN Cast, GKN Stretched, Plexiglass, Poly II, SAR (coated)
– Polycarbonate (PC) brands



» Lexan 9034, Lexan MR10 (coated), Makrolon (coated, also now Tuffak), & Zelux
– Glasses and Ceramic Brands



» Corning – Fused Silica (FS), Tosoh – Fused Quartz (FQ), & Surmet - ALON
– Each material is taken through a suite of material and environment testing



• Environments: Flammability, Atomic Oxygen, Hypervelocity Impact, Thermal Vacuum, and Radiation 
& UV



• Testing: Optical, Creep, Strength, and Thermal Property
– Materials are tested optically before and after exposure to determine if there was a degradation in optical quality 



after exposure. Then, the materials were tested for strength after exposure.

22



• Test Setup
– Each material was exposed to a UV radiation environment and a proton and electron radiation 



environment. A single coupon was exposed to both environments to capture compounding effects.
– UV consisted of Near UV (NUV) and Vacuum UV (VUV), which was produced by a 1 kW Mercury-Xenon 



arc lamp and a Deuterium lamp respectively. 



Radiation & UV Exposure











23



• Severe Lichtenberg lines and yellowing occurred on the specimen.
– Comparisons to flight data show that this does not occur in flight.
– Strength testing shows that radiation embrittlement has taken place, but a more testing in a moreflight-



accurate environment is required.
