Objectives

➢ Previous NASA Juncture flow model experiments with DLR-F6 swept-wing model

• Assessment of CFD in prediction of onset and extent of 3D separated flow near
wing-juncture trailing edge region

➢ New experiment with NACA 0015-based swept wing in early 2022

• Assessment of CFD to capture incipient separation and transition

Use pretest CFD for selected flow conditions to characterize trends in boundary
layer transition, to reveal dominant transition mechanisms, and to assess the
influence of transition on the onset and size of the separated flow, so as to inform
the 2022 wind tunnel test

➢ Stability analysis using linear parabolized stability equations and RANS-based
transition models

➢ Blind assessment of both stability and RANS-based transition models and eventual
refinement of these models

Summary
NASA juncture flow model with NACA 0015-based wing  - Pretest computational assessment of BL transition was completed 

for a selected set of flow conditions via both Linear PSE and RANS-based transition models

o Upper surface of the wing: 

▪ Dominated by TS-instability; AFT2017b and LM2015 model predictions compare well with linear PSE 

results 

▪ Laminar flow extent decreases from 50% chord with increase in AoA

o Lower surface of the wing: 

▪ TS-instability most dominant mechanism on the outboard region

▪ Laminar flow extent increases with AoA

▪ CF effects begin to dominate within inboard region at higher AoA ( ≥ 5 deg.)

▪ Small subset of inboard region also displays mixed TS-CF instability.

▪ LM2015 predicts reasonably the overall trend seen from linear PSE stability results 

◻ Earlier transition predicted at AoA = 5.0 & 7.5 deg

▪ AFT2017b does not perform as well on pressure side, as it does not account for CF effects

o Valuable guidance for the planned experiment. In return, the measurements will help improve these models

Future work:

➢ Explore alternative crossflow models that don’t rely on input parameters related to surface roughness amplitude 

➢ Impact of assumed surface roughness levels on LM2015 predictions

➢ Influence of Re on predicted transition behavior and also, identify when laminar separation is likely to occur
