<p>Complexity</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide3_rId2"></div>
<div class="embedded" id="slide3_rId3"></div>
<div class="embedded" id="slide3_rId4"></div>
<p>Ɐ action preceded by solution of a decision-making problem </p>
<p>Reducing solvability uncertainty always done via bounding problem complexity; e.g., current airspace</p>
<p>This work:</p>
<p>Dynamic complexity bound for multiagent systems, both machine and HM</p>
<p>Additional challenge for machine component: must have explicit problem formulation</p>
<p>(Beneficial) side effect: an approach to informing decision allocation</p>
<p>Desiderata:</p>
<p>Represent complexity in computable, actionable form</p>
<p>Detect approaches to unacceptable complexity</p>
<p>Reconfigure system to forestall transition to unacceptable complexity &amp; back when resolved</p>

<p>t</p>

<p>Proposed Measure of Complexity</p>
<p>4</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<p>Here rely on formulating decision-making as optimization; other formulations possible</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Complexity</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Tractability, with a look ahead, of the problem solution on a time budget, as a function of external and internal parameters 	</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>			+										</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>The quality of solutions of the decision-making problem measured in terms of constraint satisfaction, optimality, and robustness </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>			+</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Other context-dependent criteria</p>

<p>Conceptual Reasoning Framework</p>
<p>5</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<p>Given:</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>An agent and a goal</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Initialize: </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Set time t=0</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Set initial sampling time interval Δt</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Set initial look-ahead time T</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Set initial look-ahead sampling time interval Δτ</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Select initial problem-solving algorithm P</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Select environmental complexity parameters C</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Select initial environmental complexity model M</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Select transition criteria</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Select stopping criteria </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<div class="slide-content"><p></p>
<p>Conceptual Framework, cont.</p>
<p>6</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<p>Do until (stopping criteria are satisfied)</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Acquire and assess complexity parameters C at time t</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Set τ = t </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Do while (τ ≤ t + T)</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Estimate look-ahead complexity parameters at time τ</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   τ = τ + Δτ</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   End do</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Input complexity parameters into complexity model M; assess approach to phase transition </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   If (approach to transition detected) then </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Reconfigure operations (“add structure”)</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Else</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Assess system’s performance slack</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   If (slack)</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>      Reconfigure operations (“relax structure”)	</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Else</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>      Continue present operations 	</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   End if</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   End if</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>   Update time</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>End do</p>
<p></p>

<p>Example: ab initio air traffic control</p>
<p>7</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<p>Problem:</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Compute trajectory of an aircraft from point A to point B</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>No assumption on structure</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Complexity parameters:</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>External:</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Internal, e.g.: physical properties of the aircraft and computational properties of decision-making algorithms 				</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Tractability: </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Solving trajectory optimization problem on a time budget</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Metrics for quality of solutions of the decision-making problem:</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Constraint satisfaction</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Optimality</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Robustness: robust solutions live in the regions of space that allow for many alternative solutions (e.g., flexibility preservation, Idris et al.). </p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Interaction activation</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Density and heterogeneity of the relevant airspace volume</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>9</p>
<p>Preliminary Computational Results</p>
<p>Operation setup in ATMLG (Air Traffic Monotonic Lagrangian Grid)</p>
<p>Initialize system</p>
<p>N = number of aircraft</p>
<p>System aircraft interaction distance (10 mi)</p>
<p>Set time ti = start timei (can be 0 or chosen randomly from a set) for i=1,…,N</p>
<p>Do Ɐ 10 sec until End</p>
<p>ID all aircraft in conflict (within interaction distance)</p>
<p>For Ɐ aircraft in conflict, select: {x1 = Δ heading±45°, x2= Δ speed±10 kt, x3= Δ altitude±1000 ft}</p>
<p>For Ɐ pair of aircraft in conflict, formulate a constraint:</p>
<p>Compute t of closest approach for 2 aircraft based on current position, heading and speed (regardless of altitude)</p>
<p>Compute separation distance between the 2 aircraft at t of closest approach</p>
<p>Compute a projected altitude factor</p>
<p> =                        =                          </p>
<p>Solve minimize, subject to g ≤ 0</p>
<p>End Do</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p></p>
<p>Concluding Remarks</p>
<p></p>
<p>The idea: reduce uncertainty in reaching successful decisions via “conditioning” the system to be simple enough to allow each agent to solve its problem in time.</p>
<p>Cautiously optimistic about proof of concept:</p>
<p>For a centralized or distributed solution, the onset of complexity is detectable in advance</p>
<p>Reconfigurability can be controlled automatically</p>
<p>For practical solution, need a minimum necessary set of information requirements for any viable architecture</p>
<p>In the case or air traffic, few problem formulations will be used</p>
<p>Caution: uncertain whether patterns will emerge for general NLP </p>
<p>Authority allocation aspect:</p>
<p>Reconfiguration may involve transfer of authority to alternative decision-maker</p>
<p>DOE of more realistic scenarios in development for data collection and complexity model construction.</p>
