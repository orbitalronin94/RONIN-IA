Conclusion


Validated CFD model for autogenous and helium pressurization operation


Careful interface turbulence transport modeling necessary for accurate predictions


Post-pressurization pressure drop is well predicted for autogenous pressurization but is unsatisfactory for helium pressurization





Further Work


Identify and address root causes for unsatisfactory post-pressurization pressure drop


Validate with moving and morphing interfaces – more complex CFM operations





Successful validation across propellant tanks of all sizes, different cryogens, and varying CFM operations using the same modeling approach demonstrate wide model applicability for supporting NASA’s future spaceflight missions
