Planetary Entry


4


Thermal Protection System (TPS) protects spacecraft from harsh heating 


TPS materials are typically thermally robust, low-density insulators that are inherently fragile


For the human-rated Orion vehicle, some crucial parts of the TPS need to be extremely strong as they are part of the vehicle structure

















Why is 3D-MAT Needed for Orion?


5


Compression Pads Have Severe Requirements


Must be extremely strong due to high mechanical loads


Must survive an explosive bolt pyroshock 


Must survive extremely hot re-entry temperatures (~3300 °F) 


3D-MAT was invented to enable Orion Artemis missions

Prior State of the Art Insufficient for Artemis


6


Orion’s Entry Flight Test-1 (Earth orbit) in 2014 verified that the best structural ablator we had would not work for future missions beyond low Earth orbit





Significantly higher loads for Artemis lunar missions meant that no known material would work

What is 3D-MAT?


7








Quartz Yarn


3D Woven Preform


3D Weaving





Resin Infusion


 


3D-MAT is a composite material


Made from 3D weaving quartz yarns & infusing with a cyanate ester resin


3D orthogonal weave with high fiber volume maximizes the material strength


Resin infusion to fully densify the material (no pores) locks in the strength & robustness





13 x 12 x 3 inches


3D Composite Billet














Automated Weaving Breakthrough


8


Developed new Jaquard loom harness design to individually control 5000 yarns





First achievement of continuous, automated 3D orthogonal weaving at large scale (12” x 3”) & high fiber volume (60%)





4 times larger cross section than prior state of the art was a major disruptive breakthrough

3D-MAT: Breakthrough Composite


11


The combination of materials and architecture result in an extremely high-performance composite unlike any other





Compared to 2D carbon phenolic used on Orion EFT-1, 3D-MAT is:


9 times stronger in tensile strength (z)


3 times stronger in shear strength


25% lower in thermal conductivity
