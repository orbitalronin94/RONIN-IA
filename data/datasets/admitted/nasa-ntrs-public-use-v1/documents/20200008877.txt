Defect-Engineered SiGe is beneficial for TE from high σ (high mobility) and low k (phonon
scattering)
The rhombohedral hetero structure epitaxy is established
A highly [111]- oriented SiGe layer of the c-plane sapphire with twinned phase is
fabricated
The more stacking fault is generated from E-beam irradiation on SiGe film
A thin film TE measurement system that measure Seebeck coefficient, electrical
conductivity, and thermal diffusivity at temperature up to 1000K has been demonstrated
High electrical conductivity and increased Seebeck coefficient from stacking fault
engineered SiGe film
Thermal diffusivity is challenge to measure because the SiGe layer is grown on the
sapphire which has very high thermal conductivity.
