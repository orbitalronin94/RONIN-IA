Conclusions

• The PGD method provides an efficient method for computing CP Decompositions
and compares well against ALS in terms of accuracy and efficiency.

• Tests on simulated data show that CP Thermography can in some circumstances
perform as well or better than Fixed Eigenvector Thermography. Comparing both

methods on experimental data with known defects is the logical next step.
