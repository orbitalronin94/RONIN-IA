The Geostationary Lightning Mapper
 An instrument aboard the GOES-R Series of Satellites 



that detects light that escapes cloud top from 
lightning events. 
 Operates in the near infrared at 777.4 nm
 Sampling rate is two milliseconds
 Unique measurements: spatial extent of lightning, 



optical brightness
 Rudlosky et al. 2019, GRL



 GLM Terminology
 Event – any pixel which is illuminated within a 2 ms



period
 Group – adjacent CCD pixels that illuminate in a 2 ms



period
 Flash – GLM groups that are found within 16.5 km of 



each other and 330 ms in time
 The 330 ms period is chosen to speed up processing, this 



could split flashes in approximately 5% of the population 
(see Peterson 2019, JGR).
