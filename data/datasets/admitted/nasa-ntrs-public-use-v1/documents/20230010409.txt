GSON Low Latency VIIRS for WRF-SFIRE Forecasting
• Conducted demo on Oct 1-8, 2021; GSON delivered low latency VIIRS Radiances and Active Fires 



products (<12 min)



• The Weather Research Forecasting – Spread FIRE (WRF-SFIRE, Disasters Program) ingested the low latency 
VIIRS radiance and fire radiative power data to initialize fire perimeter of the KNP Fire



• WRF-SFIRE produced 48-hour forecast of fuel moisture, fires spread, and smoke at 9 & 21z (with low 
latency) and at 12 and 0z (regular latency; operational forecast)



• Forecast initialed with low latency matches the observations best over the first 24 hours of the fire 
(Hilburn, et al., AMS CMM 2022) and is produced 3 hours sooner

Unique SatCORPS Data Products
Traditional Standard Cloud Products
• Mask, Thermodynamic phase
• Cloud Temperature and Height
• Optical Depth, Effective Radius, Water Path
• Droplet number concentration



Innovative SatCORPS Data Products
• Cloud optical properties at Night



- Physical retrieval for thin cloud optical 
properties



- Machine learning for thick clouds (diurnally 
consistent)



• Cloud vertical structure 
- Cloud layering, thickness, base heights
- Cloud water content profiles



• Radiative Fluxes (TOA and SFC)
• Surface Skin Temperature
• Aviation weather hazards & climate impacts



- Icing and convection 
- Contrail optical properties, radiative effects











Global Cloud Composites (GCC) from Satellites



New system is intended for the broader community
• Day & Night, 3-km grid, 30-60 minutes, cloud 



algorithm enhancements to improve consistency, all 
radiances, additional derived parameters



• First charter is to produce a multi-year, hourly dataset 
beginning in 2019 from the current constellation 
(ABI, AHI, SEVIRI, MODIS, VIIRS) targeted to serve 
modeling needs related to cloud parameterizations



• Real-time ingest of LEO satellites using SatCORPS 
Ground Station Observation Network (GSON) and 
operational LEO processing (in development)



System is being designed so that it can also run 
operationally to support NRT & other applications



Objective: Optimally combine radiances and derived 
products (cloud properties and radiative fluxes) from 
multiple GEO and LEO satellite imagers as seamlessly as 
possible

SatCORPS GIS | Discoverability & Capacity Building



User Engagement & Interoperability



• Establishing SatCORPS group on NASA ArcGIS 
Online (AGOL) and nomination to popular 
geospatial data catalogs such as Esri Living Atlas of 
the World (LAW) for discovery by internal and 
external users .



• Working with partners (e.g., NASA Prediction Of 
Worldwide Energy Resources Project, NASA Airborne 
Science Mission Tool Suite (MTS)) for direct 
integration into their web mapping applications.



• Future nomination to AWS Open Data Registry 
(ODR) as part of NASA Space Act agreement and 
ESDIS Earthdata Pub to encourage in-cloud 
analysis/use and discoverability.



• Positioning the future of SatCORPS data distribution 
and analysis for easier integration to NASA Earth 
Action (e.g., Earth Information Centers, Earthdata GIS 
Enterprise in the cloud, etc.)



• Development of interactive tutorials and resources 
for users (e.g., Jupiter Notebooks)
