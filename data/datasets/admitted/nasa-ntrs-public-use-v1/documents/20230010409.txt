<p>GSON Low Latency VIIRS for WRF-SFIRE Forecasting
• Conducted demo on Oct 1-8, 2021; GSON delivered low latency VIIRS Radiances and Active Fires 
</p>
<p>products (&lt;12 min)
</p>
<p>• The Weather Research Forecasting – Spread FIRE (WRF-SFIRE, Disasters Program) ingested the low latency 
VIIRS radiance and fire radiative power data to initialize fire perimeter of the KNP Fire
</p>
<p>• WRF-SFIRE produced 48-hour forecast of fuel moisture, fires spread, and smoke at 9 &amp; 21z (with low 
latency) and at 12 and 0z (regular latency; operational forecast)
</p>
<p>• Forecast initialed with low latency matches the observations best over the first 24 hours of the fire 
(Hilburn, et al., AMS CMM 2022) and is produced 3 hours sooner 
</p>

<p>The Satellite ClOud and Radiative Property retrieval System (SatCORPS)
</p>
<p>GOES-E (16)GOES-W (17) MET-11 MET-8 HIMAWARI
</p>
<p>CTH 
(km)
</p>
<p>NRT half-hourly GEO Global Cloud Composite (GCC), 3km subsampled data products
</p>
<p>Unique SatCORPS Data Products
Traditional Standard Cloud Products
• Mask, Thermodynamic phase
• Cloud Temperature and Height
• Optical Depth, Effective Radius, Water Path
• Droplet number concentration
</p>
<p>Innovative SatCORPS Data Products
• Cloud optical properties at Night
</p>
<p>- Physical retrieval for thin cloud optical 
properties
</p>
<p>- Machine learning for thick clouds (diurnally 
consistent)
</p>
<p>• Cloud vertical structure 
- Cloud layering, thickness, base heights
- Cloud water content profiles
</p>
<p>• Radiative Fluxes (TOA and SFC)
• Surface  Skin Temperature
• Aviation weather hazards &amp; climate impacts
</p>
<p>- Icing and convection 
- Contrail optical properties, radiative effects</p>
<p></p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Global Cloud Composites (GCC) from Satellites
</p>
<p>New system is intended for the broader community
• Day &amp; Night, 3-km grid, 30-60 minutes, cloud 
</p>
<p>algorithm enhancements to improve consistency, all 
radiances, additional derived parameters
</p>
<p>• First charter is to produce a multi-year, hourly dataset 
beginning in 2019 from the current constellation 
(ABI, AHI, SEVIRI, MODIS, VIIRS) targeted to serve 
modeling needs related to cloud parameterizations
</p>
<p>• Real-time ingest of LEO satellites using SatCORPS 
Ground Station Observation Network (GSON) and 
operational LEO processing (in development)
</p>
<p>System is being designed so that it can also run 
operationally to support NRT &amp; other applications
</p>
<p>Objective: Optimally combine radiances and derived 
products (cloud properties and radiative fluxes) from 
multiple GEO and LEO satellite imagers as seamlessly as 
possible
</p>

<p>SatCORPS GIS | Transformation &amp; Distribution

<p>Analysis Ready Data (ARD)
</p>
<p>• Rolling 2-week data parameters stored in 
cloud-optimized format and full archive access
</p>
<p>• Built with transpose to provide more-efficient 
time series retrieval
</p>
<p>• Global composite and regional mosaic 
availability
</p>

</p>

</p>
<p>• ~28 Multidimensional (time-enabled) parameter-level 
geospatial services as global and regional composites
</p>
<p>• Exposed as RESTFul APIs, ArcGIS Image Services, &amp; 
Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC) Web Mapping 
Services/Web Coverage Services
</p>

</p>
<p>• Establishing SatCORPS group on NASA ArcGIS 
Online (AGOL) and nomination to popular

the World (LAW) for discovery by internal and 
external users .
</p>

Science Mission Tool Suite (MTS)) for direct 
integration into their web mapping applications.

(ODR) as part of NASA Space Act agreement and 
ESDIS Earthdata Pub to encourage in-cloud 
analysis/use and discoverability.
