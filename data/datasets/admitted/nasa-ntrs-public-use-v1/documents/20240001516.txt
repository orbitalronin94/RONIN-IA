Microbial Isolate and Veggie Monitoring 



Microbial Database 
• Microbial Isolate Collections



• JSC - Microbial monitoring of the air, surfaces and 



water on the ISS since 2001 by the JSC 



Microbiology Laboratory. 



• KSC - Microbial analyses of the Veggie, APH and 



XROOTS hardware and the associated plant 



material at KSC Life Sciences Labs since 2014. 



• KSC - Space X monitoring since Crew 1 by 



Environmental Lab.



• JPL - Microbial Tracking 1 and 2.



• These collections are currently stored in separate 



locations.











5



Microbial Isolate Database 



Data will allow analysis of microbial 



community trends of the ISS environment.



• Acquire and organize ISS microbial 



isolate data since 2001
• ISS Air



• ISS Surface



• Veggie, APH, XROOTS



• Ground Surfaces including vehicles and 



payload hardware to be launched



• Developed a series of searchable fields



• Developing a tutorial for use



• Over 3200 entries of cultured isolates











6



Searchable Fields
• Location (Flight or Ground)



• Sample category (i.e. Surface, Air, Plant)



• Sample date



• ISS location



• ISS mission/increment



• Experiment/Tech Demo 



• Sample site (i.e. Bellows, Fans, Pillow#)



• Sample type (i.e. Root, Leaves, Substrate)



• Plant type (if applicable)



• Microbial category 



• Genus



• Species



• Method (of identification)



• Lab (source)



• APC



• Fungal CFU



• LSDA submission



• Publication Link
