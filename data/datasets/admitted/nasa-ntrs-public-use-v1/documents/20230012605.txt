<p>What is Planetary Protection?
</p>
<p>Protect current and future scientific investigations by limiting biological and relevant 
molecular contamination of other solar system bodies through exploration activities 
and protecting the Earth’s biosphere by avoiding harmful biological contamination
</p>
<p>carried on returning spacecraft, as described in the Outer Space Treaty. 
</p>
<p>Forward PP - Understand and control harmful 
contamination of other worlds by terrestrial organisms, 
</p>
<p>organic materials, and volatiles from spacecraft 
</p>
<p>Backward PP - prevent harmful biological contamination 
of the Earth-Moon system by potential extraterrestrial life 
</p>
<p>and bioactive molecules in returned samples</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>sma.nasa.govOffice of Planetary Protection
</p>
<p>Committee on Space Research (COSPAR)
• Panel on Planetary Protection forms international consensus 
</p>
<p>guidelines
• Defines PP Categories I – V based on target body and mission type
</p>
<p>NASA 
• Supports the science-based international consensus process
• Develops new guidelines and provides significant input to 
</p>
<p>COSPAR
</p>
<p>The Outer Space Treaty of 
1967
</p>
<p>International Responsibility Planetary Protection
</p>
<p>Article VI:
States Parties to the Treaty shall bear international 
responsibility for national activities in outer space, 
</p>
<p>including the moon and other celestial bodies, 
whether such activities are carried on by 
</p>
<p>governmental agencies or by non-governmental 
entities, and for assuring that national activities are 
</p>
<p>carried out in conformity with the provisions set 
forth in the present Treaty
</p>
<p>Article IX:
States Parties to the Treaty shall pursue studies of 
outer space, including the Moon and other celestial 
bodies, and conduct exploration of them so as to 
</p>
<p>avoid their harmful contamination and also adverse 
changes in the environment of the Earth resulting 
from the introduction of extraterrestrial matter and, 
</p>
<p>where necessary, shall adopt appropriate measures 
for this purpose
</p>
