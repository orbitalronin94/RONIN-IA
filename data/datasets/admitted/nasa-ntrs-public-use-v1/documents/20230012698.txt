3



Theory of Metallized Plastic Current Collectors



• Internal short defect is fed rapidly 



through solid metal (Al, Cu) film current 



collectors



• Polymer separator thermally breakdowns 



and shrinks away creating higher internal 



short risk between anode and cathode



• Plastic substrate of current collector 



thermally breaks down and isolates the 



electrochemically active materials from the 



defect within milliseconds

Potential Root Causes for Nail TR



• Wh/kg, Wh/L limit reached for 
PCCs?



• Need more thermally stable 
separator?



• Need to add (-) Cu coated PCC?



• Cathode active material adhesion 
needs to be poor?



• Cathode active material porosity
can’t be too low?



• No: Lower SOC testing of 
>260Wh/kg cells results in TR at 
90 & 80%



• No: Replacing sep with 300°C 
cellulose (Dreamweaver) results 
in TR at 100, 90, and 80% SoC



• No: Replacing with Cu coated 
PCC results in TR at 100, 90, & 
80% SoC



• Possible: BAK cells have very 
poor adhesion



• Possible: Higher porosity 4.5Ah 
cells by Coulometrics tolerate nail

Summary of our 2022-2023 Effort



Soteria polyester PCC is reliable in tolerating nail penetration in 59 
out of 60 Li-ion cells with <250 Wh/kg



• 2.1Ah Coulometric 18650s (26 for 26)
– And 4 for 7 for Nomex PPCs



• 2.9Ah BAK 18650s (11 for 11)



• 4.6Ah BAK 21700s (10 for 11)



• 10Ah SVolt Pouch Cells (12 for 12)



Higher energy (>260 Wh/kg) 21700 cell designs fail nail pen



• 5.192Ah Coulometrics (0 for 21, even at 70% SoC)
– Al PCC reduces TR heat output vs Al foil cells



• 4.97Ah Coulometrics with DW sep (0 for 9, even at 80% SoC)



• 4.94Ah Coulometrics with DW sep & Cu PCC (0 for 9, even at 
80% SoC



Investigation into root causes continues with new cells builds



• Higher porosity cathode active material



• Lower adhesion of cathode active materials to collector



• Compare safety with 20Ah pouch cell format
