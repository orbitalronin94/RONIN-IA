<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>The Need for Universal Lunar Surface Power
</p>
<p>• Lunar surface operations will grow and expand
– Power demands will exceed element’s 
</p>
<p>capability - Self sufficient power is not feasible
– Systems will have to be integrated together or 
</p>
<p>to a power grid (shared access)
• Power over long-distance 
</p>
<p>– Driven by minimum safe landing distance and 
radiation stand off (1km+)
</p>
<p>– Needed for power in permanently shadowed 
regions (PSR)
</p>
<p>• Dissimilar power sources
– Increase power availability – provide power 
</p>
<p>during longer eclipse periods
– Contingency scenarios - Provide needed 
</p>
<p>redundancy and reliability</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Lunar Power Grid
</p>
<p>• Benefits of a power grid
– Flexibility, evolvability, and 
</p>
<p>reconfiguration
– Optimal dispatch of power sources 
</p>
<p>and energy storage to service loads &amp; 
enhance reliability
</p>
<p>– Systematic integration of new sources 
and loads
</p>
<p>– Common grid interface (commonality)
– Allows for the deployment of future 
</p>
<p>loads that do not need to carry their 
own power generation
</p>
<p>• NASA STMD performing initial enabling 
R&amp;D work for an Artemis grid
</p>
<p>– Microgrid Definition and Interface 
Converter for Planetary Surface (MIPS)
</p>
<p>• Main product: UMIC</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Your Logo Here
</p>
<p>Power Sources:
Solar Arrays 
• Vertical Solar Array Technologies (VSAT)
• 10 kW increments
• 120 VDC Source
</p>
<p>Fission Surface Power (FSP)
• 40 kW Power
• Must be located more than 1km from 
</p>
<p>other lunar elements
</p>
<p>Lunar Elements (Loads): 
Surface Habitat
• 20 kW during habitation (must be during insolation)
• 2 kW during  eclipse and non-habitation
In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)
• ISRU processing - 46 kW insolation / 9 kW eclipse
</p>
<p>• Located on crater rim (in the sun)
• Excavation - 22 kW insolation / 4 kW eclipse
</p>
<p>• Permanently shadowed regions (water-ice)
• About 3km – 5km from processing facility
</p>
<p>Baseline Artemis Power System
</p>
<p>7</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Your Logo Here
</p>
<p>• Electrical Power System—Sizing and Analysis Tool (EPS-SAT) used for studies
• MATLAB-based tool for power system concept analysis, available for general govt purpose use
• https://software.nasa.gov/software/LEW-20017-1
• Planetary surface cable and converter models developed and used for this work 
</p>
<p>• Transmission bus voltage, power type, and frequency allowed to vary
• Voltage: 1.2 kV to 6 kV, DC and 3-Ph AC
• Frequency: 60 Hz to 1 kHz
</p>
<p>• Evaluate mass of radial, ring, and mesh architectures for overall grid design
</p>
<p>Trade Study Setup
</p>
<p>8</p>
<p></p>
<div class="annotation"><a href="https://software.nasa.gov/software/LEW-20017-1">https://software.nasa.gov/software/LEW-20017-1</a></div>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Your Logo Here
</p>
<p>• Cables
• Single or bundle of parallel 10-14 AWG wires, ETFE insulated Cu
</p>
<p>• Size for &gt;90% efficiency at 40 kW
• Skin/proximity effect, inductance, temperature modeled, others
</p>
<p>(e.g. coupling to regolith) ignored
</p>
<p>• Converters
• 95% efficient if DC-DC (bidirectional DC-DC)
• 96.5% efficient if DC-AC (bidirectional inverter)
• 98% efficient if AC-AC and no AC frequency changes (a 
</p>
<p>transformer)
</p>
<p>• Loads/Sources
• Habitat includes 20 kW source and 20 kW load
• ISRU includes 80 kW source and 68 kW load
• FSP is a 40 kW AC source
</p>
<p>Trade Study Modeling Assumptions
</p>
<p>9</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Your Logo Here
</p>
<p>• Trade studies focused on primary power 
distribution system
</p>
<p>• Data includes mass estimates of converters 
and cables
</p>
<p>• Results
• Increasing voltage decreases mass
</p>
<p>• Diminishing returns above 3-4 kV
• Increasing tie lines (radial  ring or mesh) 
</p>
<p>adds mass but does not change trends
• Easiest to start w/ radial, and add 
</p>
<p>additional tie lines later (add reliability)
• AC vs DC at a single voltage is marginal 
• Technology limitations need to considered
</p>
<p>• Max DC: 1.5 kV (Rad hard limitations)
• Max AC: none known
</p>
<p>Radial Architecture
</p>
<p>Recommendation: 3kVAC, 3-phase, 1000 Hz

• 10 kW nominal power capability (12 kW peak)
• 19” rack based breadboard form factor

multiple parallel inverter chassis

single location

• &gt;95% efficiency
• 350 W/kg power density (est. for flight)
</p>

• Grid forming
• Synchronization with
</p>
