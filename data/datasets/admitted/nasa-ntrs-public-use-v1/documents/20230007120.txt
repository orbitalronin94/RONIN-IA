<p>§ An idealized intercalibration setup has 
perfectly matched data 
in time, space, angles, and wavelengths
</p>
<p>§ Realistic intercalibration measurements 
have finite differences in sampling, thereby 
resulting in several sources of uncertainty
o Spatial mismatch
o Angular differences (SZA, VZA, and RAA)
o Spectral band differences
</p>
<p>§ CPF will demonstrate a state-of-the-art 
intercalibration methodology mitigating the 
uncertainties from imperfect data matching
o 2-axis pointing capability

<p>CPF-CERES Intercalibration Algorithms
§ CPF employs different methods to reduce uncertainty impact of finite differences in sampling
</p>
<p>o Spatial &amp; Temporal Differences
• CPF to JPSS-1 instrument boresight matching using 2-axis pointing
• Aggregating significant number of samples monthly to reduce random data matching noise
</p>
<p>o Angular differences (SZA, VZA, and RAA)
• For pixels close to boresight: 2-axis pointing
• For pixels off-boresight: Angular Adjustment Algorithm
</p>
<p>o Spectral band differences
• VIIRS: 3 nm Spectral sampling of CPF hyperspectral measurements for spectral convolution
• CERES: Leveraging spectral information to “predict” out of CPF band radiance</p>
<p></p>
</div>
