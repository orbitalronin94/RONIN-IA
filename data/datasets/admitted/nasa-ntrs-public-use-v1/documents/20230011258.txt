<p>Etymology

<p>6
</p>
<p>Osiris: Egyptian spirit of fertility, the afterlife, and life
Bennu: A crane, created by Osiris, symbolizing creation and rebirth</p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Why have an OSIRIS-
REx mission?
</p>
<p>• Astromaterial samples inform us on 
the origins of life, solar system, and 
even the
</p>
<p>• Understanding organic molecules (e.g. 
water) can lead to us understanding 
more about life on Earth 
</p>
<p>• Timing and location is everything 
when targeting a mission
</p>
<p>• Cost: $800 Million USD
</p>
<p>7</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>“Bennu may contain the molecular precursors to the 
origin of life and the Earth’s oceans. Bennu is also one of 
the most potentially hazardous asteroids. It has a 
relatively high probability of impacting the Earth late in 
the 22nd century. OSIRIS-REx will determine Bennu’s 
physical and chemical properties. This will be critical 
for future scientists to know when developing an impact 
mitigation mission.”
</p>

<p>TAGSAM works by using a “reverse vacuum”
</p>
<p>• Collecting sample with a rocky surface 
poses a problem – difficult to drill 
reliably on surface
</p>
<p>• N2 tanks pump gas into the local 
environment, stirring the regolith, 
which is then collected in a trap
</p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>My Role in OSIRIS-REx
sample return
• The goal of Astromaterial Curation 
</p>
<p>is to protect samples for 
preservation and future research
</p>
<p>• Earth is littered with fungi and 
bacteria that can degrade these 
invaluable samples
</p>
<p>• My team’s job is to survey 
cleanrooms to ensure the samples 
are safe
</p>
<p>Image of Chemotrophic 
bacteria degrading Iron in a 
rock sample
</p>
<p>19</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Experimentation with sample recovery
</p>
<p>• Tests that lie ahead for the recovered 
sample varies, but will likely include
</p>
<p>• Optical studies
• Mass spectrometry
• Raman spectroscopy
• X-Ray Diffraction
• Ion beam studies
• And more, curation will preserve the 
</p>
<p>samples indefinitely until the right 
experiments emerge
</p>

<p>Insight for future 
missions
</p>
<p>• OSIRIS-Rex mission planning, 
execution, recovery, and curation 
helps to train and prepare for 
future missions
</p>
<p>• Lessons will be learned for Mars 
sample return, and proposed 
missions such as astronaut led 
exploration of asteroids and Mars
</p>
