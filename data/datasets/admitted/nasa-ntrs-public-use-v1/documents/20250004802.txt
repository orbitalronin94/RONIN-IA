<p>What Is Differential Drag?
</p>
<p>•Predicted trajectory assumes 
a particular drag area.
</p>
<p>•Changing a satellite’s drag 
has a net propulsive effect</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Highsmith &amp; Clark | 3
</p>
<p>Motivation for Using Differential Drag
</p>
<p>• CARA missions are 
seeing more and more 
conjunctions
</p>
<p>• Operational missions 
without propulsion want 
to explore their options 
for collision avoidance
</p>
<p>• Missions in 
development are 
considering alternatives 
to propulsion</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Highsmith &amp; Clark | 4
</p>
<p>Analysis Approach
</p>
<p>•Analyze ~7,000 historical conjunctions with Pc &gt; 1E-4 at TCA-3 days
§Bin conjunctions by 1) perigee altitude and 2) initial energy dissipation rate (EDR)
</p>
<p>• EDR = rate of orbital energy lost due to atmospheric drag
</p>
<p>•Use Maneuver Trade Space (MTS) tool
§Propagate from ”maneuver start time” with different changes in ballistic coefficient  
</p>
<p>• Evaluate maneuver durations of 72 hr and 24 hr from TCA
§Define successful remediation to be post-maneuver Pc &lt; [remediation threshold]
</p>
<p>• Evaluate remediation thresholds of 3E-6 and 7E-5
</p>
<p>•Express efficacy results as:
§Percent of conjunctions within a given altitude or EDR bin that were successfully 
</p>
<p>remediated
</p>
<p>Pc:	Probability	of	collision								TCA:	Time	of	Closest	Approach</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>

</p>

</p>

</p>

• This analysis adds conjunction data from June 2022 to December 2024
</p>
<p>§Drew conclusions based on desired efficacy percentage &gt; 50%
</p>

§ Is post-maneuver Pc reduction requirement of 1.5 OOM appropriate for differential drag 
</p>
<p>missions?
§What efficacy is acceptable to declare a differential drag capability sufficient to baseline 
</p>
<p>for collision avoidance?
</p>

<p>Effective Delta-V from Delta-EDR
</p>
<p>• Example:
§ D-BC = 2, initial EDR = 0.0006–0.0015 W/kg 
</p>
<p>• Delta-EDR =&gt; 0.0012 to 0.0030 W/kg
§ 24 hr &amp; 72 hr maneuver durations
</p>
<p>• Differential drag efficacy:
§ 24 hr maneuver: ~1.5 – 3 cm/s @550 km
</p>
<p>• 3E-6 threshold efficacy:   9% (p. 17)
§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 10% (p. 11)
</p>
<p>• 7E-5 threshold efficacy: 28% (p. 18)
§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 32% (p. 12)
</p>
<p>§ 72 hr maneuver: ~4 – 10 cm/s @550 km
• 3E-6 threshold efficacy: 58% (p. 14)
</p>
<p>§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 64% (p. 8)
• 7E-5 threshold efficacy: 80% (p. 15)
</p>
<p>§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 77% (p. 9)
[Not equivalent to impulsive DV execution time from TCA]</p>

<p>Conclusion
</p>
<p>•Delta-EDR offers more direct 
correlation to effective delta-v
</p>
<p>•Post-maneuver remediation 
threshold must be set 
appropriately
</p>
<p>•Maneuver duration capability 
also critical
</p>
<p>•CARA still assessing policy 
implications
§Define sufficient efficacy (50%? 80%?)
§Define operationally relevant 
</p>
<p>remediation threshold (if different than 
NPR)
</p>
<p>•Differential drag can clearly be 
effective under proper 
conditions</p>
