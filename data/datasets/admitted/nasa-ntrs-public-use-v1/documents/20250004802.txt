Analysis Approach



•Analyze ~7,000 historical conjunctions with Pc > 1E-4 at TCA-3 days
§Bin conjunctions by 1) perigee altitude and 2) initial energy dissipation rate (EDR)



• EDR = rate of orbital energy lost due to atmospheric drag



•Use Maneuver Trade Space (MTS) tool
§Propagate from ”maneuver start time” with different changes in ballistic coefficient 



• Evaluate maneuver durations of 72 hr and 24 hr from TCA
§Define successful remediation to be post-maneuver Pc < [remediation threshold]



• Evaluate remediation thresholds of 3E-6 and 7E-5



•Express efficacy results as:
§Percent of conjunctions within a given altitude or EDR bin that were successfully 



remediated



Pc: Probability of collision TCA: Time of Closest Approach

Effective Delta-V from Delta-EDR



• Example:
§ D-BC = 2, initial EDR = 0.0006–0.0015 W/kg 



• Delta-EDR => 0.0012 to 0.0030 W/kg
§ 24 hr & 72 hr maneuver durations



• Differential drag efficacy:
§ 24 hr maneuver: ~1.5 – 3 cm/s @550 km



• 3E-6 threshold efficacy: 9% (p. 17)
§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 10% (p. 11)



• 7E-5 threshold efficacy: 28% (p. 18)
§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 32% (p. 12)



§ 72 hr maneuver: ~4 – 10 cm/s @550 km
• 3E-6 threshold efficacy: 58% (p. 14)



§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 64% (p. 8)
• 7E-5 threshold efficacy: 80% (p. 15)



§ 500-600 km, D-BC = 2 efficacy: 77% (p. 9)
