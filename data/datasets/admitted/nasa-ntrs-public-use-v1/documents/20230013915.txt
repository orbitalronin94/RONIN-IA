Conclusions & Outlook



- Entanglement witnesses can efficiently verify entanglement in a state



- Witnesses for QAOA-MaxCut require at most 3 sets of measurements 



(once each in the X, Y and Z bases)



- Such witnesses can also be used for other (e.g. GHZ) states



- Witnesses for other QAOA states?



- Is larger entanglement necessary for QAOA to solve combinatorial 



optimization problems?

Conclusions



• It is possible to approximate continuous group gauge theories via 



discrete subgroups



• For U(1) x Z2, DN is a fairly good approximation for N ~ O(10)



• Experimentally, we already have > 80% estimated fidelity for D4 



theory on a noisy QPU



• More complicated gauge theories have more complicated discrete 



subgroups, with more complicated gates to work out (future work)
