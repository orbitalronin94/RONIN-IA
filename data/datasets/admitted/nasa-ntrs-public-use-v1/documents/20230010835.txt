Due to the addition of a second body, conservation of mechanical energy cannot be used to establish critical values of post-collision angular velocities of the vehicle and pendulum that will result in toppling. 


Instead, a two-step procedure is adopted in this analysis :


1) Theory involving generalized momenta and impulses described in Dynamics: Theory and Application of Kane’s Method, is used to predict the post-collision angular velocities of the vehicle and pendulum


2) Solve the coupled 2 degree-of-freedom post-collision equations of motion numerically to determine if toppling occurs
