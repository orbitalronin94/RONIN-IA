Recent History for MBE & MBSE
2022
• It was always intended that MBSE be expanded to Include all domains and activities associated with the Engineering 

Domain, eg. the formulation of the NASA Model-Based Engineering (MBE) leadership team.
– Jan – created the integrated MBE team comprising: MBSE, Design to Manufacturing, Multi-Physics modeling.
– Jun – re-organized the MBE to reduce scope over-lap of teams and broaden coverage over entire life-cycle of projects wrt toolchains.
– Jun – Task 5: initiated a multi-center (JSC, KSC, GRC) AWS Gov. cloud-based PLM pilot project.
– Orion DT – Task 4: Worked with MB MA / OSMA to explore ideas of how to generate fault trees from SysML models.

• Developed MBSE 3-Year/Phase Implementation Plan based upon INCOSE Capabilities Assessment Maturation 
Model.
– Has been used to prioritize activities as funding was made available.

– Fall ‘21 Piloted new APPEL Tier 4 MBSE course, and approved for Agency use. Existing training changed to use MagicDraw.

– Task 1: Perform Agency survey of integrated toolchains and associated interoperability requirements/capabilities.

– Task 3: Modeling of Agency Main-Three NPRs: 7123 (SE&I), 7120.5/.8 (Project Management), 8705 (Mission Assurance) with the objective to:
• Worked with other DT MBx domains to assess processes via SysML modeling and integrate to determine needed dataflow.
• Model associated processes as is
• Assess processes from ‘data-centric’ viewpoint – more on this later
• Assess processes for efficiency opportunities
• From data centric approach determine needed data interoperability requirements to inform infrastructure changes/adds, policy changes, toolchain selection guidelines 

for Agency.

– Task 2: Continued baselining of external agencies and companies for MBSE, toolchains, and workforce training approaches.

– Released NASA MBSE Handbook for Agency review.

– Integrated MBE with NASA SBIR S17.02 Integrated Mission Modeling 8

Near-Term Forward Work
FY23
Close Partnership with the Center Engineering Directorates:
• Direct involvement with the Center’s Engineering directorates to better understand current capabilities, where 

they want to get to, and determine how MBE can help.
• Leverage current capabilities of Centers with other Centers in need of the capability.
• Formulate a plan for pilot projects which advance a Center’s needs and those of other Centers.

Complete Agency Process Mapping and perform Data-centric optimization, complete the NASA extension of BFO 
ontology, formulate interoperability requirements, and recommended toolchain with short list of tools per domain 
per project/program lifecycle.

Domestic Partners:
• Release a RFI for recommendation for successful digital engineering toolchains to solution providers, to inform 

NASA’s internal recommendations in the event there are unknown unknowns to NASA in this area.
• Release a RFI to domestic partners regarding how they would like to do business with NASA, collaborate, 

exchange data via contractual mechanisms to shape NASA’s near-term capabilities.
International Partners:
• Engage to develop capability to securely collaborate and exchange information seamlessly.

– Build on existing collaboration capabilities with JAXA and use as approach to implement with other space agencies.
– Understand how ESA has integrated across multiple companies and national space agencies within ESA.
