Japan-US Space Collaboration Agreement





On January 13, 2023, Japan and US signed: “Framework Agreement between the Government of Japan and the Government of The United States of America for Cooperation in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space, Including the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies, for Peaceful Purposes”


Signed by US Secretary of State Anthony Blinken and Japanese Foreign Minister Yoshimasa Hayashi at NASA HQ


NASA Press Release:
https://www.nasa.gov/press-release/us-japan-sign-space-collaboration-agreement-at-nasa-headquarters 


Full Text available at Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs website:
https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/ila/st/page24_002128.html 








4


Scopes of Japan-US Framework Agreement


Activities Areas:


Space science, including lunar science;


Earth science;


Space operations and exploration, including lunar operations and exploration;


Aeronautical science and technology;


Space technology;


Space transportation;


Safety and mission assurance; and


Other relevant areas as mutually determined.


Forms of Cooperation:


Use of spacecraft and space research platforms;


Use of scientific instruments onboard spacecraft and space research platforms;


Crewed spaceflight operations;


Use of sounding rockets and scientific balloons;


Use of aircraft;


Use of ground-based antennas for tracking and data acquisition;


Use of ground-based space research facilities;


Exchanges of scientists, engineers, or other experts on general or specific subjects;


Exchanges of scientific data;


Space education;


Public outreach activities; and


Other forms of cooperation as mutually determined.
