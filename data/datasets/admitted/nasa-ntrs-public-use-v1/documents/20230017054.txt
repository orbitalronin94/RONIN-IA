<p>Environmental Effects and Coatings Branch (LME)
</p>
<p>LME is engaged in R&amp;D related to environmental effects on materials durability……regardless of mission or environment
</p>
<p>Core Competencies:
• Fundamental High Temperature Materials – chemistry, thermodynamics, and kinetics thru experimental and first principles 
</p>
<p>analytical methods
• Computational Modeling – DFT, CALPHAD, Machine Learning
• Durability Testing in Extreme Environments
• Advanced Coatings – Thermal barrier coatings (TBC), Environmental barrier coatings (EBC), diffusion barriers, thermal 
</p>
<p>barriers
• Space Environments – Atomic oxygen, UV damage, Planetary atmosphere/surface
</p>

</p>

• Space Environments testing and flight projects
</p>

<p>Molten Silicate Attack and Infiltration
</p>
<p>• Particulates (i.e. sand, volcanic ash) ingested by engine 
melt into Calcium-Magnesium-Alumino-Silicate 
(CMAS) deposits above 1200°C 
</p>
<p>• Molten CMAS degrades EBCs (chemical + mechanical)
• CMAS infiltration of EBC due to lowered CMAS viscosity at elevated 
</p>
<p>temperatures → CTE mismatch
</p>
<p>• Thermochemical interactions of CMAS with EBC → spallation
</p>
<p>Eyjafjallajökull volcano 
eruption in Iceland (2010)
</p>
<p>Damage on a turbine blade 
</p>
<p>caused by CMAS &gt;1200°C
