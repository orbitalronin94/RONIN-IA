<p>Solid-state Architecture Batteries for Enhanced Rechargeability and Safety (SABERS)
Beyond Li-Ion: Technology to Enable Sustainable Electric Aviation
</p>
<p>Challenge
- Electric aviation is a revolutionary leap in 
</p>
<p>sustainable aviation
- Current battery technology does not meet the 
</p>
<p>strict aerospace performance and safety metrics
- Can we exceed 400 Wh/kg in specific energy and 
</p>
<p>operate safely at a rate of 1C and beyond?
</p>
<p>Expected Impacts
- Expand the missions served by electric aviation with a safe battery 
</p>
<p>with twice specific energy over current SOA Li-ion batteries
- Reduce waste in the environment through recyclability: recovering 
</p>
<p>solid lithium and sulfur components, and reprocessing the electrolyte
- Reduce waste in the environment by using a waste byproduct of oil 
</p>
<p>refining, Sulfur - the active component in SABERS cells
</p>
<p>Solution
- Develop a solid-state bipolar battery stack based on novel Li-
</p>
<p>S/Se chemistry and a non-flammable electrolyte to produce a 
battery that enables sustainable electric aviation
</p>
<p>- Design SABERS cells to exceed 500 Wh/kg, operate up to 150 
oC, are 1 to 4C capable, and inherently non-flammable
</p>
<p>- Satisfy system level needs such as non-flammability, energy 
density, discharge rate, packaging, and scalability
</p>
<p>Results
- Improved design optimization with computational modeling of 
</p>
<p>the cathode microstructure based on experimental results
- Achieved electrochemical-level specific energy of greater than 
</p>
<p>500 Wh/kg in coin cells
- Achieved high-temperature (100 oC), high rate (up to 4C; 
</p>
<p>ultrahigh current density &gt;30 mA/cm2) all-solid-state cells
- Demonstrated successful scale-up 
- Investigated various processing techniques for pouch cellsa) Specific energy targets b) SABERS concept to meet aerospace requirements c) Systems level benefits of typical eVTOL mission 
</p>
<p>Motivation and Solution
</p>
<p>Next Steps
- Employ ultra-thin reinforced sulfide and polymer solid    
</p>
<p>electrolytes (SE)
- Improve cyclability through Li-metal anode and Li-SE interface 
</p>
<p>engineering
- Improve reproducibility of pouch cells to increase production rate
- Fabricate SABERS bipolar stack
</p>
<p>Computational Modeling
</p>
<p>a) Cathode microstructure model b) Introduction of holey graphene in model c) Computational prediction based on experimental data 
</p>
<p>Coin Cell Development
</p>
<p>a) Energy density of dry pressed cells b) Effect of carbon type on cathode microstructure c) Compositions that achieved 500 Wh/kg
</p>
