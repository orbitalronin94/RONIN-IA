Summary
• Successful designs must consider the whole system… complexity
• Need for higher fidelity models to quickly evaluate topologies
• Material needs to meet power density targets



• Inductors: Metal amorphous nanocomposites offer tunable permeability
• Transformers: 20-40 kW/kg possible in the >100kW range
• Motors: Need to develop materials for kHz switching
