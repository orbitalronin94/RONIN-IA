The NASA Human Research Program 

HRP drives advances in scientific and technological research to enable human 
space exploration. 

It is a human-focused Program dedicated to providing solutions and mitigation 
strategies beyond low-earth orbit by reducing the risks to human health & 
performance through focused translational, applied, and operational research. 

HRP primary deliverables include:
• Human health, performance, and habitability standards
• Countermeasures and other risk mitigation solutions
• Advanced habitability and medical support technologies
