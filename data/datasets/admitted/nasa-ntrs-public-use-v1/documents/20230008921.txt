Ames COTS Parts Selection Strategy


Careful COTS


MIL/Automotive/Industrial/Commercial Grades


Wide Design Margins (Temperature, Voltage, Power)


Commonly Used Parts (Huge Production Volume)


Flight Heritage


Trusted Manufacturers & Distributors


Limited screening including GIDEP search, visual inspection, counterfeit controls


Board/System/Mission-Level Testing


Risk mitigation at board/system level


Extensive testing with multiple design revisions


Test on orbits and in low-cost missions

Observations in ARC Missions


Design & Workmanship Issues >> Component Failures 


QPL Approach Needs More Resources


Less upper-level testing (Undetected bugs)


QPL (Less integrated) Often Requires More Supporting Circuits


Up-Screened Components Would Not Have Saved/Improved Most ARC Missions.


COTS Benefits ARC:


Wider selections, faster development schedule, lower costs


Few radiation test data, but significantly more use cases


Solid design/testing has better returns than solid parts
