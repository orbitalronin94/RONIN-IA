NASA AAM Research Goals



Goals



• Understand the impacts to inform stakeholders



• Improve what we fly through the development of new tools and 
technologies to improve performance / efficiency and reduce noise



• Improve how we fly with a focus on operational efficiency and 
community impact



Leverage



• Widespread transition to renewable energy, including access to green 
electricity and sustainable aviation fuels 



• Sustainable aviation policy, market-based measures including carbon 
offsets



• Improved energy storage (e.g., batteries)



• Sustainable battery manufacturing and recycling

Summary



• Advanced Air Mobility has the potential to democratize aviation by enabling operations in and 
around communities – expanding the reach and efficiency of the transportation system



• Opportunity to target sustainable technologies from the start to achieve quieter operations with 
less lifecycle emissions



Driving the transition to a cleaner, quieter, more accessible form of aviation
