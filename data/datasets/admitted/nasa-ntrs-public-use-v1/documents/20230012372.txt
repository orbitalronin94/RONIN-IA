</p>
<p>NASA AAM Research Goals
</p>
<p>Goals
</p>
<p>• Understand the impacts to inform stakeholders
</p>
<p>• Improve what we fly through the development of new tools and 
technologies to improve performance / efficiency and reduce noise
</p>
<p>• Improve how we fly with a focus on operational efficiency and 
community impact
</p>
<p>Leverage
</p>
<p>• Widespread transition to renewable energy, including access to green 
electricity and sustainable aviation fuels 
</p>
<p>• Sustainable aviation policy, market-based measures including carbon 
offsets
</p>
<p>• Improved energy storage (e.g., batteries)
</p>
<p>• Sustainable battery manufacturing and recycling</p>
<p></p>

<p>Summary
</p>
<p>• Advanced Air Mobility has the potential to democratize aviation by enabling operations in and 
around communities – expanding the reach and efficiency of the transportation system

</p>
