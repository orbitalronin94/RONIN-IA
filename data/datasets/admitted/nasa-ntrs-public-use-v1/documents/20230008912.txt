<p>A New In-Space Manufacturing Advancement Center (ISMAC)</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide1_rId3"></div>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p>Target Application Areas</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide1_rId4"></div>
<p>Application 1. In-Space Welding, Joining, and Advanced Manufacturing Simulations</p>
<p></p>
<p>Develop ICME workflows with targeted space welding problems</p>
<p>Computational Fluid Dynamics</p>
<p>Thermodynamic Materials Modeling</p>
<p>Use existing data from archival reports and flight experiments to anchor simulations</p>
<p>Goal: evaluate and reduce risks of in-space manufacturing operations</p>
<p>Product: Disseminate model and expertise to programs and stakeholders</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide1_rId5"></div>
<p>Application 2. Laser Welding and Additive Manufacturing</p>
<p></p>
<p>Realize one-shot critical in-space welds through computational models anchored to experiments</p>
<p>Microgravity, Extreme Temperature, Vacuum of Space are considered</p>
<p>Perform parabolic flight experiments with collaborators</p>
<p>Perform pre-flight and post-flight characterization for model calibration and validation</p>
<p>Physics of laser welding applicable to laser additive manufacturing</p>
<p>Widely disseminate information</p>
<p>Application 3. Lunar Infrastructure, ISM, OSAM, and construction</p>
<p></p>
<p>Surface Applications including material extraction, processing and reuse of Al, Fe, Si usable feedstock</p>
<p>Conversion of usable feedstock into system components through printing, forging, and joining</p>
<p>Material development</p>
<p>Modeling anchored to ground-based and parabolic experiments</p>
