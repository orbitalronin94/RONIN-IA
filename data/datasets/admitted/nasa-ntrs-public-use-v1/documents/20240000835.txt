<p>DARPA Robotics Challenge - Valkyrie
</p>
<p>6
</p>
<p>The primary technical goal of the 
DRC is to develop human-
</p>
<p>supervised ground robots capable 
of executing complex tasks in 
dangerous, degraded, human-
</p>
<p>engineered environments.
</p>
<p>• 15 month design-operation
• Degraded communication
• Example set of tasks to be 
</p>
<p>completed:
• Open/close valve
• Open &amp; walk through door
• Cut hole in dry wall
• Attach fire hose to socket
• Climb ladder</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Affordance Templates
</p>
<p>• Affordance Templates
• Provide task-level structure for robots to execute complex tasks
• Built upon Robot Operating System (ROS) and RViz (ROS 
</p>
<p>visualization tool) – open source robotic software tools
• Generalizable to all robot types
</p>
<p>• Affordance Template ROS package 
• Allows for quickly programming, adjusting, and executing 
</p>
<p>robotic tasks
</p>
