<p>Refueling Pause Test Result – General Observations (1)
</p>
<p>➢ Location of Peak Pressure: The highest transient delta peak 
</p>
<p>pressures were observed just upstream of the closing valve.
</p>
<p>➢ Downstream Transient Pressure: Transient pressure drop is 
</p>
<p>more aggressive when the upstream delta peak pressure is 
</p>
<p>higher. The transient pressure drop needs to be evaluated 
</p>
<p>to stay above propellant vapor pressure to avoid vapor 
</p>
<p>collapse.
</p>
<p>16</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Refueling Pause Test Result – General Observations (2)
</p>
<p>➢ Flow Rate: There were slight deviance of flow rates from 
</p>
<p>each test. The delta peak pressure results from the test 
</p>
<p>cases show that it is highly sensitive to the flow rate.
</p>
<p>➢ Location of Closing Valve: ERM IV2 &gt; HALO IV &gt; PPE. It is 
</p>
<p>observed that the transient pressure will vary depending on 
</p>
<p>the system design around the closing valve and valve 
</p>
<p>closing time.
</p>
<p>➢ ERM IV2 is a different valve with different Cv and closing time than 
</p>
<p>HALO IV and PPE IV2. 
17</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Refueling Pause Test Result – Tank Pressure
</p>
<p>➢ Tank pressure is a sensitive parameter for pressure transients.
</p>
<p>➢ Transient delta peak pressure were observed to be higher when the 
</p>
<p>operating pressures were higher at comparable flow rates.
</p>
<p>➢ Entrained gas has a larger effect at low pressure.
</p>
<p>➢ Engrained gas effect are reduced as the operating pressure increases.
</p>

<p></p>

<p>Model Validation Approach
</p>
<p>➢ Thermal Desktop with FloCAD (SINDA/FLUINT Version 6.1)
</p>
<p>➢ Flat-front modeling technique used for priming
</p>
<p>➢ Nonvolatile water (9000 series) and air as perfect gas (8000 series)
</p>
<p>➢ Simplified two-phase HFE-7100 (7000 series)
</p>
<p>➢ Includes liquid compressibility and tube wall compliance
</p>
<p>➢ Assumed temperature of 70°F
</p>
<p>➢ No heat transfer
</p>
