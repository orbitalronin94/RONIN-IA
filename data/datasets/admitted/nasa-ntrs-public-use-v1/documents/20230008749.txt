<p>§ Perfect storm for incompatibility
› SiC-based inverter with minimal gate rise/fall 
</p>
<p>time and wideband DC bus capacitance 
› DC distribution bus uses “flat” ribbon-like cable 
</p>
<p>resulting in extremely low inductance
› Battery module sensor boards are coupled to 
</p>
<p>the cells without filterer or a drain to ground
§ Resulted in loss of battery module comms 
</p>
<p>when inverters were active (even while 
power delivery was good)
</p>
<p>§ Fix is tuned to the flight hardware
› Measured noise on actual flight units
› Developed custom T-filter to absorb switching 
</p>

<p>§ Grounding design vs reality
› Battery, Inverter, Motor were intended to be 
</p>
<p>isolated from vehicle structure and other 
system grounds.
</p>
<p>› Tight clearances between case/heatsink, 
mounting structure, baffles, vibration mounts 
resulted in multiple rounds of 'surprise' 
ground paths
</p>
<p>§ Inverter/motor act as a system and must 
be solidly bonded
› EM coupling between rotor and stator 
</p>
<p>induced current to flow through bearings 
and into vehicle structure
</p>
<p>› Spare slipring channel is used to drain rotor 
currents to CMC chassis/T-filter
</p>
<p>Close quarters in the nacelle resulted in unintentional grounding
</p>

</p>
<p>Conducted Interference from Inverter/Motor
</p>
<p>Slip ring must be rugged 
and provide reliable 
rotor-stator coupling</p>

<p>§ Electric Engines have many adjacent systems
› Prop governor (actuator and speed pickup), 
</p>
<p>tachometer, blade angle sensor, motor and air duct 
temperature sensors, accelerometers, and strain 
gages are all within inches of the inverter/motor.
</p>
<p>› Remote aircraft systems also saw switching noise 
including instrumentation throughout the vehicle, 
annunciator panel, audio comms, and flap 
indicator/controller.
</p>
<p>§ Contain and absorb the noise at the nacelle
› Common-mode chokes were installed on all wiring 
</p>
<p>bundles that entered the electric engine bay
› Grounding improvements were made to stock 
</p>
<p>systems: don’t use structure for power return path, 
route return with supply and use shields with drains.
</p>
