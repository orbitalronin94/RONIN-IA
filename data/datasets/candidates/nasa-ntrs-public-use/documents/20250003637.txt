<p>Background: ISS SAW Operation
</p>
<p>• Each of the power channels on the ISS consists of 82 solar array 
strings across two blankets to generate electrical power.
</p>
<p>• When less power is needed, strings are shunted in a set order.
</p>
<p>• A shunted string contributes no electrical power to the EPS and thus 
heats up due to the solar flux (conservation of energy).

<p>Why Identify Failed Strings?
</p>
<p>• A failed solar array string on the ISS can cause a drop of 
usable electrical power by a few hundred Watts.
</p>
<p>• Knowledge of string failures is needed to make accurate 
predictions of power capability for upcoming missions.
</p>
<p>• Power margins are getting tighter due to higher ISS utilization and 
older solar arrays.
</p>
<p>• There are multiple methods to identify a failed solar array 
string. Each method has different strengths and limitations.
</p>
