<p>Concluding Remarks
</p>
<p>• eVTOL aircraft present new aero-propulsive modeling challenges 
</p>
<p>• Accurate prediction tools are needed throughout the design process
</p>
<p>• FlightStream® yields flow solutions accessible early in vehicle design
</p>
<p>• Comparison of FlightStream® predictions to static wind-tunnel data
</p>
<p>• Future work:
</p>
<p>▪ Analysis for the RAVEN SWFT powered-airframe and other eVTOL aircraft
</p>
<p>▪ Validation of dynamic derivative predictions
</p>
<p>▪ Refinement of FlightStream® flow models and grid generation</p>
