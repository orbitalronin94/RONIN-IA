• One-to-few-walled tubes with high crystallinity
• Very long, high-aspect ratio tubes
• High scale-up potential

• Highly electroactive (due to the B-N polar bond)
• Neutron radiation shielding (due to their B content)

Summary
• Highly crystalline, long, small diameter BNNT was successfully synthesized at
high pressure, high temperature environments.
• Daily operation of BNNT synthesis (about 4g) enables purification, dispersion,
BNNT mat/yarn formation, extend BNNT research to external collaborators.
• Advanced in-situ optical diagnostics: verified up to 950 psi/1000˚C with a new
NIA BNNT science chamber
• BNNT polymer composites exhibited piezoelectric and electrostrictive strain at
low electric fields, which originated from the presence of BNNTs. MD modeling
study supported the experimental results.
• BNNT polymer composites exhibited excellent neutron and beta radiation
shielding properties and both boron and nitrogen contribute to the shielding
effectiveness.
