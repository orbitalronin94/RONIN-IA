<p>Baseline Artemis with a Lunar Microgrid
</p>
<p>▪ Create a regional lunar grid with:
• Primary distribution system to enable power sharing between local microgrids
</p>
<p>• Additional power source (FSP) that can be utilized by local microgrids
</p>
<p>• NASA has conducted grid trade studies to inform grid and interface converter design
</p>

<p></p>

<p>Artemis Grid Trade Study
</p>
<p>5
</p>
<p>▪ Electrical Power System—Sizing and Analysis Tool (EPS-SAT)
• MATLAB-based tool for power system concept analysis
</p>
<p>• https://software.nasa.gov/software/LEW-20017-1
</p>
<p>▪ Transmission bus voltage, power type, and frequency allowed to vary
• Voltage: 1.2 kV to 6 kV, DC and 3-Ph AC
</p>
<p>• Frequency: 60 Hz to 1 kHz
</p>
<p>• Evaluate radial, ring, and mesh architectures for overall grid design</p>
<p></p>

<p>6 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>Trade Study Assumptions
</p>
<p>6
</p>
<p>▪ Cables
• Copper 10-14 AWG wires with ETFE insulation (~90% design efficiency at 40 kW)
</p>
<p>• Individual phase wires or bundle of parallel phase wires to be used per ampacity
</p>
<p>• Skin/proximity effect, inductance, and temperature effects modeled
</p>
<p>• Other effects (e.g. inductive, electrostatic regolith interactions) ignored
</p>
<p>▪ Converters
• 95% efficient if DC-DC (bidirectional DC-DC)
</p>
<p>• 96.5% efficient if DC-AC (bidirectional inverter)
</p>
<p>• 98% efficient if AC-AC and no AC frequency changes (a transformer)
</p>
<p>▪ Constant design efficiency for each voltage</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>7 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>Radial Architecture
</p>
<p>▪ Radial System Assumptions
• Assume high voltage bus is near habitat
</p>
<p>• Brings ISRU and FSP power to habitat, 
to serve as a backup
</p>
<p>• Excess FSP power can flow to ISRU if 
habitat power needs are satisfied first
</p>
<p>▪ Radial Advantages
• Simple (lower implementation cost)
</p>
<p>• Lightweight
</p>
<p>▪ Radial Disadvantages
• Lack protection / redundancy during 
</p>
<p>failure</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>8 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>Radial Architecture Results
</p>
<p>8
</p>
<p>▪ Total transmission mass (converter + cables) versus grid voltage
• AC options showing various frequencies
</p>
<p>• 1 kHz had lowest mass (will only present the 1 kHz going forward for AC)</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>9 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>Ring Architecture
</p>
<p>▪ Ring architecture adds a second tie 
line between FSP and ISRU
• Assume FSP to Habitat line matches 
</p>
<p>overall grid power type
</p>
<p>▪ Ring Advantages
• Adds single line tolerance for only one 
</p>
<p>more tie line
• Adds more efficient path for FSP power 
</p>
<p>to get to ISRU mining which has no 
power of its own
</p>
<p>▪ Ring Disadvantages
• ~50% heavier than radial network
• Can only lose one line and maintain 
</p>
<p>ability to transmit power between any 
two assets</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>10 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>Mesh Architecture
</p>
<p>▪ Mesh adds two additional tie lines
• FSP to ISRU Production
• Habitat to ISRU Mining
</p>
<p>▪ Mesh Advantages
• Additional lines add more efficient paths to 
</p>
<p>transmit power throughout network
• Dual line fault tolerance
</p>
<p>▪ Mesh Disadvantages
• ~100% heavier than radial network
• ~50% heavier than ring</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>11 | #ascendspace
</p>
<p>▪ DC system significantly lighter than AC for voltages above 3-4 kV, otherwise marginal
• However, DC grid practically limited to 1.2-1.5 kV, due to lack of high voltage rad-hard switches
</p>
<p>• AC easier to increase voltage and expand, as transformers can change voltage in AC-DC designs
</p>
<p>▪ Study trends highlighted
• 3 kV AC is mass optimal feasible design solution
</p>
<p>• Architectures with more tie lines (ring, mesh) show higher fault tolerance and mass
</p>
<p>• Architecture selection should be made by mass and fault tolerance requirements/constraints
</p>
<p>▪ Buildup of a lunar surface microgrid will likely start small
• Start with radial and expand over-time to achieve increased reliability (fault tolerance)
</p>
<p>▪ Desirable to explore possible grid expansions toward global exploitation
</p>

<p>Summary
</p>
<p>▪ Artemis scale grid trade studies conducted to inform grid and interface converter
design
• AC selected for ease of increasing voltage, extensibility
</p>
<p>• 3 kV AC selected as mass optimal solution
</p>
<p>▪ Follow on studies conducted exploring possible expansions of the Artemis grid
• Focus on in-space manufacturing
</p>
<p>▪ Grids become necessary for planetary surface operations as they grow to include
more assets dependent on each other for power
• NASA investing in initial R&amp;D work for these grids</p>
