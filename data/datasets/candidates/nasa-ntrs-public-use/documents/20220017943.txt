2020 TIPPING POINT CONTRACTS – ‘RIDESHARE CFM FLIGHT DEMOS’
SpaceX

On-Orbit Large-Scale Cryogenic Propellant Management and Transfer 
Demonstration

Value:  $53.2M
Overview:  Demonstration of large-scale on-orbit cryogenic 
fluid transfer (> 10 metric tons) between tanks on a Starship 
in orbit and management to provide a basis for operational 
use of in-space refueling technology. 
Mission Overview:
• Demonstration conducted as part of a Starship flight
• Active settling maneuver
• Autogenous pressurization and propellant transfer
• Settled transfer, > 10 tons, between main propellant tank 

to header tank located in the nosecone.
Projected Flight:  FY23

ULA
Cryogenic (H2/O2) Smart Propulsion Flight Demonstration

Value:  $86.2M
Overview:  Flight demonstration of 3 key CFM technologies: passive 
thermal control, tank pressure control, and tank-to-tank propellant 
transfer for both LO2 and LH2.
Benefits: 20% increase in Vulcan Centaur launch performance, 
extending Centaur mission duration, and enhanced confidence in 
cryogenic stages benefiting Government missions, including HLS 
and commercial payloads.
Mission Overview:
• Demonstration occurs after primary spacecraft is separated; 

demonstration hardware integrated with Centaur V
• Demonstration is a ~1 day on-orbit duration
Projected Flight:  FY25

2020 TIPPING POINT CONTRACTS – ‘PAYLOAD CFM FLIGHT DEMOS’
Eta Space

Liquid Oxygen Flight Demonstration (LOXSAT-1)
Value:  $27M
Overview:  Develop, launch and fly a 9-month technology 
demonstration payload designed to test over 10 different CFM 
technologies necessary for creating practical propellent depots.
Mission Overview:
• Flight demonstration based on: 

• Zero fluid loss storage in LEO environment (Sun Synch orbit)
• Control of fluid position and thermodynamic state
• Control of tank pressure using autogenous and helium 

pressurization
• Tank-to-tank fluid transfer

• LOXSAT 1 hosted on a dedicated Rocket Lab Photon satellite bus
• Minimum 9 month mission
Projected Flight:  FY24

Lockheed Martin
Cryogenic Demonstration Mission (CDM)

Value:  $89.7M
Overview:  Ground testing and flight demonstration of 
cryogenic LH2 transfer and long duration storage in space. 
CDM incorporates 15 critical technologies identified by 
NASA into a single system that demonstrates the transfer, 
storage, and pressure control of LH2 through ground 
testing and flight demonstration.
Mission Overview:
• 400 to 600 km circular, Sun Sync orbit
• Demonstrate multiple transfers of LH2 between a 

storage tank and receiver
• Up to 60-day technology demonstration (85-day total 

mission)
Projected Flight:  FY25

LARGE CFM DEMONSTRATION (LCD) – SUMMARY
Demonstrate Technologies enabling autonomous transfer and storage of cryogenic hydrogen, capable of scaling to tens of metric tons, with 
negligible losses for long duration in space and on the lunar surface.

• Initial concept studies completed, including lifecycle preliminary schedule and cost estimates
• Additional technology efforts included assessment of LCD mission operations / design requirements vs ‘critical’ 

scaling data generation to achieve TRL 6+ for CFM technologies

• Forward work includes:
• Possible Request for Information (RFI) engagement with industry to research cost / schedule estimates
• Refining Lifecycle schedule and cost estimating approaches to link both together
• Develop acquisition approaches (‘Make’ / ‘Buy’ / ‘Hybrid’) 

• Government must determine unlimited data rights approach / dissemination
• Identifying remaining CFM technology gaps (assuming successful LCD mission) and alternative gap closure 

approaches through other CFM Project Office activities

LCD current concepts and mission intended to mature critical CFM Technologies to TRL 6+, satisfying 
the Project Office’s goal to technology gaps via a system level Integrated CFM Flight Demonstration
