<p>Transit Habitat Overview</p>
<p>Hybrid inflatable-metallic habitat with dry mass (26.4 metric tons) capable of being launched commercially and supplied with logistics in cis-lunar orbit (NRHO)</p>
<p>Support 4-crew during Lunar-Mars Analog missions leading up to a 700-1110-day Mars mission</p>
<p>Dock w/ an interim propulsion bus or Gateway for first ~5 years until Mars Propulsion System (MPS) elements available</p>
<p>Extends Gateway operations beyond 60 days</p>
<p>10.2 psia (26.5% O2) atmosphere for Gateway ops</p>
<p>14.7 psia (21% O2) atmosphere for transit and analog missions</p>
<p>Contingency Airlock and EVA capability</p>
<p>Planned reuse for multiple missions over 15-year lifetime</p>
<p>Builds on ISS and commercial investment in deep space habitation</p>
<p>Mid-2030s launch with late 2030s Mars Departure</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Ground Rules &amp; Assumptions</p>
<p>Significant GRs:</p>
<p>4 crew for up to 1,200-day Mars Transit mission duration</p>
<p>26.4 mt target dry mass (including MGA and margin)</p>
<p>Autonomous operation when uncrewed</p>
<p>Max uncrewed dormancy of up to 3 years</p>
<p>Minimum of 2 axial and 1 radial docking ports</p>
<p>Performs a series of up to ~180d Mars Analog missions while docked at Gateway</p>
<p>Self-sufficient habitat once fully deployed at orbits up to 1.0 AU, capable of receiving power from Mars propulsion system beyond 1.6 AU</p>
<p>14.7 psia 21% O2 atmosphere nominal, capable of 10.2 psia 26.5% O2 during Gateway docked open-hatch ops</p>
<p>Safe Haven and Solar Proton Event (SPE) Shelter</p>
<p>15-year life with multiple missions of increasing duration</p>
<p>TH Sparing and Maintenance - Manifested to achieve 99% system availability</p>
<p>10</p>
<p>Significant Assumptions:</p>
<p>Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit (NRHO) via Commercial Launch Vehicle(s)(CLV). Options for SLS cargo delivery are possible but should feed cost assessments.</p>
<p>Early 2030’s launch with Mars mission in late 2030’s</p>
<p>Replenishable Reaction Control System (RCS) through docking or Gateway interface between mission phases (analogs, Mars Propulsion System (MPS) shakedown, Mars transit)</p>
<p>Contingency EVA airlock</p>
<p>Trash/waste removal (11.6 kg/day avg)</p>
<p>Accommodate 1000 kg of science and utilization payloads</p>

<p>Continued concept refinement alongside M2M architecture</p>
<p>Further CONOPS definition within each operational phase</p>
<p>Phase 1: TH in cis-lunar NRHO with Gateway and MPS integration</p>
