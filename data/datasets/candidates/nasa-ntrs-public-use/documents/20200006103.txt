• Higher the a-stabilizing element (e.g. Al) content, higher the b transus temperature.
• Al contents of both samples are significantly lower than those of their corresponding
base plates. This is equivalent to significant Al losses during EBF3.
• For DSC analysis, Al loss appears to be nearly alike in both samples at the
measurement location (≈ 30 mm in +z-axis direction)
• Hysteresis in b transus for heating versus cooling, is likely a measure of
heterogeneity of a + b microstructures, and b grain size.

Conclusions

Comparison of the microstructural characteristics between the openloop (uncontrolled) and closed-loop control samples shows distinct
differences due to overall thermal input.
Preferential aluminum vaporization is significant in both deposited
samples and likely contributes to the variation.
The open loop sample, with higher thermal input and reduced thermal
gradient, exhibited significant variations in through-thickness
microstructure.
The closed-loop control sample, where beam power was reduced in
order to maintain consistent molten pool size, contained less
microstructural variation.
