Numerics

FCT = Flux Corrected Transport

⦁ First monotone scheme for fluid equations
⦁ Invented at NRL by Boris for 1D hydrodynamics
⦁ Elaborated and extended by Book, Zalesak, et al.
⦁ Generalized to multi-dimensional MHD by CRD



Numerics

FCT = Flux Corrected Transport

⦁ FCT emphasizes positivity and monotonicity
⦁ FCT de-emphasizes theoretical orders of errors
⦁ Typical schemes are 2nd/4th order in time/space
⦁ Adept at handling shock waves and current sheets



Numerics

PARAMESH = PARAllel Adaptive MESH Toolkit

⦁ Developed at GSFC by MacNeice, Olson, et al.
⦁ Supported by HPCC-ESS software development
⦁ PARAMESH is the backbone for ARMS, FLASH, etc.
⦁ Parallelizes and manages block-adaptive grid data



Numerics

ARMS = Adaptively Refined MHD Solver

⦁ Merged fixed-grid 3D FCT MHD w/ PARAMESH
⦁ Supported by DOD CHSSI software development
⦁ Developed at NRL, transitioned later to GSFC
⦁ Project conceived, led, and named by Antiochos

A Simulated Coronal-Hole Jet

⦁ Excess magnetic energy builds up slowly 
below the null point of the source region
⦁ Onset of ideal convulsion of the twisted field 
drives it into and through the null point
⦁ Reconnection between twisted internal and 
untwisted external fields launches the jet



A Simulated Coronal-Hole Jet

⦁ Nonlinear Alfvén waves lead the outflow and 
propagate away into the inner heliosphere
⦁ The outflow takes the form of a switch-on 
MHD shock wave with a mild density jump but 
strong transverse magnetic fields and flows
⦁ Its properties are consistent w/ PSP S-bends



Prospects – Science

⦁ Improved understanding of plasma response 
in SEEs via comprehensive treatment of coronal 
thermodynamics – in progress
⦁ Higher resolution, higher Lundquist-number 
numerical studies, especially in 3D
⦁ Coupled kinetic/MHD model (K-ARMS?) to
calculate energetic-particle acceleration in SEEs
