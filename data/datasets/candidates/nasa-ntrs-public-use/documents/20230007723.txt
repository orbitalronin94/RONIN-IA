<div class="slide-content"><p>SUPPORTING STATION</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide3_rId3"></div>
<p>Celebrating 22+ years of human presence on the International Space Station:</p>
<p>International partners: NASA, Roscosmos, CSA, ESA, and JAXA</p>
<p>250+ individuals from 19 countries have visited the ISS</p>
<p>Astronauts have conducted more than 255 spacewalks since 1998</p>
<p>5 Cargo vehicles: Northrup Grumman's Cygnus, SpaceX’s Dragon, JAXA’s HTV, Russia’s Progress, ESA’s ATV</p>
<p>3 Crew transportation vehicles: SpaceX’s Crew Dragon, Boeing’s Starliner, Russia’s Soyuz</p>
<p>1 Crew and Cargo transportation:  Space Shuttle</p>
<p>ISS has hosted over 3,000 research investigations from researchers in over 100 countries and counting</p>
<p>https://spotthestation.nasa.gov/</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<div class="slide-notes"><p> *ISS photo taken by Crew-2 astronauts during the first ISS fly-around of a commercially made vehicle. </p>
<p></p>
<p>Orbits the Earth every 90 minutes, approximately 260 miles above the planet’s surface </p>
<p>International partnership between NASA, Roscosmos, the European Space Agency (ESA), Canadian Space Agency (CSA), and Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA)</p>
<p>Inhabited continuously since November 2000 and hosted over 253 individuals from 19 countries</p>
<p>Orbiting outpost and National Laboratory advancing science and technology and inspiring the world </p>

<p>CREW-1</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide34_rId5"></div>
<p>Crew-1 Mission: November 15, 2020 - May 2, 2021 </p>
<p>Crew:  Shannon Walker, Victor Glover, Mike Hopkins, Soichi Noguchi</p>
<p>Falcon 9 Block 5 launched from KSC, LC-39A</p>
<p>Docked to ISS - Resilience (C207): 167 days docked/168 days in orbit </p>
<p>Port Relocate on ISS: April 5, 2021 from Forward to Zenith</p>
<p>Crew recovery by GO Navigator, Gulf of Mexico (off coast of Panama City, FL) </p>
<p>First nighttime splashdown since Apollo 8</p>
<p>Crew-1 broke the record for the longest spaceflight by a U.S. crewed vehicle, surpassing the 84-day mark set by an Apollo capsule on the final flight to the Skylab </p>
