Where This Comes From – An Systems Engineer’s View

• Exploration Medical Capability (ExMC) Systems Engineering (SE) Tasked with Developing 
the Level of Care IV Short Duration Lunar Orbit Medical System Foundation
– This Medical System is a Precursor Specification not Tied to a Specific Mission 
– ExMC SE Chose to Develop using a Model Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) Approach 
– MBSE Model in MagicDraw using Object Management Group (OMG) Systems Modeling Language (SysML)

• Issues:
– Not All Stakeholders Have Access to the Modeling Tool (limited licenses)
– Not All Stakeholders Have Access to the Model (Network Access)
– Not All Stakeholders Know the Language
– Stakeholders Use Information from the Model Differently than it is Built (Model Organization)

• Goals
– Easy Accessibility for All Stakeholders
– Present Information in the Manner that the Stakeholders Need

**Same information Presented Different Ways
– Continue to Examine and Improve Usability

Questions engineers ask…
• What do you need to bring? 
• How much mass/power/volume/data?

event?

system?

Questions clinicians ask…
• How do we do medicine in Exploration 

Missions?
• What is likely to happen and how often?
• What resources do we need and why? 
• How do we communicate to engineers? 



How We Involved and Designed to Users

Goal is to capture and visualize the information 
provided by subject matter experts (e.g., 
Clinicians) and other sources to meet the 
needs of our users.

Clinicians:
• Identify the medical conditions 
• Map the treatment of conditions to the 

medical capabilities
• Identify medical resources needed by those 

capabilities to treat the conditions

Systems Engineers:
• Import information into a model
• Show relation maps that visualize the traces 
• Derive and trace requirements

Review Process

Review Process:
1. Create Special Diagrams with Links to Model Elements

2. Generate HTML Report and Make it Generally Available to Community

3. Sanity Check to Confirm Links

4. For Groups that Have not Reviewed Report, Give Short, Introduction to Model and Report Rules

5. Perform Review

6. Gather Comments

7. Disposition

8. Update Model

Reviews Performed by:
• Internal Review by Modeling Team
• ExMC Systems Engineers (ConOps, Modeling, CST, Requirements)
• ExMC SE Management
• Greater ExMC including people not on the project team
• ExMC Executive Management and Center Leads
• Road Shows to Control Boards for Human Research Program, Medical Operations Group, Space 

Medical Operations 16



Review Overview and Findings Summary

The CR comments generally fell into the following categories:
– Spelling/grammatical changes
– Addition of clarification text to the HTML and User Guide
– Suggestions to make the HTML more navigable
– Suggestions regarding content changes
– Questions regarding Magic Draw-specific symbols/text included in the 

HTML
– Problems with links not opening

Rejected comments:
• Duplicates
• Comments with suggestions that were not in the scope of the 

Foundation work
• Things not possible in MagicDraw

Future Looks

Lessons Learned

• Sharing with non-modelers requires building views 
specifically for the type of user

• People from different disciplines can do the modeling work

• Model can be used for multiple purposes and can supply 
information to different projects, but dependencies must be 
managed (and planned for)

• A Model centric approach and workflow requires training

• Expect to reorganize (refactor) the model

• Have an architecture that supports future uses

• Need good supporting processes (CM, CCM, reviews)

• Create and use a model management plan
