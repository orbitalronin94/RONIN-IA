<p>Introduction – NASA RVLT Project 
</p>
<p>Impact Dynamics / Crash Safety Task
</p>
<p>• Task Objective:  “To improve the crashworthiness and impact safety of Urban Air Mobility (UAM) vehicle and provide 
</p>
<p>data to simplify the certification process. Efforts will include development of validated computational models of these 
</p>
<p>vehicles, as well as other impacting bodies such as birds and drones. Efforts will also focus on developing and evaluating 
</p>
<p>energy absorbing and crush properties of emerging and non-traditional composite materials and processes. Finally, 
</p>
<p>occupant protection will be addressed using computational models and physical assets as it pertains to all rotorcraft 
</p>
<p>environments.”
</p>
<p>• Problem Statement: “There currently is a lack of data for requirements regarding the crashworthy performance of 
</p>
<p>UAM vehicles and impact loads generated by a bird strike. To address this technology gap, NASA will develop test 
</p>
<p>guidelines, adopt modeling methodologies demonstrating capability for ‘certification by analysis’, acquire vehicle and 
</p>
<p>occupant data on full-scale representative vehicles, and provide data/guidance to consensus standards organizations and 
</p>
<p>the UAM community.” 
</p>
<p>• 4 Main focus points
</p>
<p>– The investigation of occupant injury using physical and computational assets
</p>
<p>– The development of energy absorbing technology
</p>
<p>– The generation of data from sub- and full-scale crash test data
</p>
<p>– The execution of advanced finite element modelling techniques 2</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Lift + Cruise Full-Scale Crash Test Objectives
</p>
<p>1. Generate data on generic electric Vertical Take-off and Landing (eVTOL)
</p>
<p>vehicle design under crash conditions to inform regulation development
</p>
<p>2. Generate data for the use in validation of computer modelling efforts
</p>
<p>3. Generate data from onboard Anthropomorphic Test Devices (ATDs, a.k.a.
</p>
<p>crash test dummies) for the evaluation of injury
</p>
<p>4. Generate data on experimental energy absorbing component designs
</p>

</p>
<p>• Test article was primarily designed through the use of computational 
</p>
<p>simulations

<p>– Sizing
</p>
