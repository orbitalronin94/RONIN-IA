<p>Technology Type Description Impact Area(s)
Damage-Arresting 
Composites (DAC)
</p>
<p>Structures Stitched structure with damage arrestment capabilities that 
prevent critical damage due to damage propagation, reduce 
airframe weight, and easier manufacturing processes
</p>
<p>Weight 
Reduction
</p>
<p>Active Load 
Alleviation
</p>
<p>Flight 
Systems
</p>
<p>Active maneuver load and gust load alleviation systems allow 
for coordinated control deflections in response to maneuver 
and gust loads that reduce the wing bending moment
</p>
<p>Weight 
Reduction
</p>
<p>Riblets Aerodynamics V-Shaped grooves aligned with the direction of the flow that 
reduce the skin friction drag of turbulent boundary layers 
</p>
<p>Drag 
Reduction
</p>
<p>Natural Laminar 
Flow
</p>
<p>Aerodynamics Facilitates extended runs of laminar flow over an aircraft 
surface by passively stabilizing the boundary layer to produce 
significant drag reductions 
</p>
<p>Drag 
Reduction
</p>
<p>Excrescence 
Reduction
</p>
<p>Aerodynamics Decreases parasite drag by imposing stricter tolerances in 
design and manufacturing 
</p>

Reduction
</p>

Turboprop Engine 
Cycle &amp; Materials
</p>
<p>Propulsion Expected evolutionary improvements in engine components 
and engine cycle design allowing for improved OPR, turbine 
temperatures and component efficiencies

<p>• Baseline models established for the B1900D, ATR 42-600, Saab 340B, 
and DHC-7-100
• Serve as benchmarks for future EAP technology sensitivity studies for EPFD
</p>
<p>• Demonstration of Systems Analysis capabilities of coupled uncertainty 
propagation and M&amp;S analysis
• Parametric reference models and methodology will allow for robust analysis 
</p>
<p>of EPFD concepts with EAP
</p>
<p>• Future studies will work on performance assessment of hybrid and 
fully-electrified aircraft concepts
• Systems Analysis tools such as GASP have been modified to conduct analysis 
</p>
<p>of EAP-enabled configurations
