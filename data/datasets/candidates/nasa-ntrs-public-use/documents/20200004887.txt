General Background
•
•

The models considered here take the form of convection-diffusion
equation(s) with source terms
The equations are discretized in a manner similar to the mean flow in
both space and time, except:
– First-order convective terms (therefore nearest neighbor stencil)
– Source terms use Green-Gauss gradients

•

Solved in a loosely-coupled fashion with the mean flow equations at
each time step
– Tend to use CFL numbers roughly one order of magnitude smaller than
mean flow

•

Primary difference from the mean flow implementation is the special
care taken to ensure positive eddy viscosity during the solution
process
– Follows directly from the approach outlined in Spalart and Allmaras (1992)
and Baldwin and Barth (1991)

•

The outline shown here is the current approach; this is a very active
area of research

An M-type matrix is a matrix that is diagonally-dominant with positive

property of an M-type matrix is that its inverse contains only non-negative
elements and is therefore a positive operator.
