<p>Introduction
</p>
<p>3
</p>
<p>• The FAA Part 25 Appendix O was issued in 2015 to define a 
</p>
<p>representative icing environment for supercooled large drops 
</p>
<p>(SLD) including
</p>
<p>▪ freezing drizzle (FZDZ) and freezing rain (FZRA) conditions  
</p>
<p>• The NASA Icing Research Tunnel (IRT) is a sea level icing test 
</p>
<p>facility that operates* in
</p>
<p>▪ Appendix C conditions
</p>
<p>▪ Limited SLD conditions in FZDZ
</p>
<p>• The SLD cloud development &amp; calibration in the IRT need to be 
</p>
<p>expanded to consider   
</p>
<p>▪ Velocity deficit (or slip velocity) of large drops
</p>

</p>

<p>Conclusions
</p>
<p>• A 10-day test entry in the IRT was performed in September 12-23, 
</p>
<p>2022, to evaluate the planned operation of the PTV probe to obtain 
</p>
<p>the supercooled larger drop velocity characteristics
</p>
<p>1. The probe did obtain valid drop size, drop shape and drop velocity data from 
</p>
<p>the SLD icing spray conditions tested in the IRT with measured drop sizes up 
</p>
<p>to 700 µm
</p>
<p>2. The drop velocity distribution at a given air speed in the IRT test section is not 
</p>
<p>affected by different spray bar pressure settings. So, it is desirable to have 
</p>
<p>more shorter spray repeats than fewer longer sprays 
</p>
<p>3. The larger drops (e.g., D &gt; 500 µm)  generated in the IRT clearly experience 
</p>
<p>significant velocity deficit in comparison with the tunnel airspeed. For an 
</p>
<p>airspeed of 250 kt in the IRT, the velocity of the largest drop measured at the 
</p>
<p>Station 1 was about 40 knots slower 
</p>
<p>4. The probe also produces a small fraction of faulty velocity measurements for 
</p>
<p>smaller size drops, and these could be easily filtered out 
</p>
<p>5. The PTV probe head influences the smaller drop velocity measurements, but 
</p>
<p>larger drops (D ≥ 300 µm) won’t be affected due to large inertia 
</p>
<p>22</p>
<p></p>
</div>

</p>
<p>Recommendations
</p>
<p>• Additional test is required to develop the procedure for traversing 
</p>
<p>the PTV probe in the IRT test section to fully characterize the large 
</p>
<p>drop size velocity distribution.    
</p>
<p>• Attention should also be given to understand what the large drop 
</p>
<p>velocity deficit may do to SLD ice shapes via numerical ice 
</p>
<p>accretion simulation studies 
</p>
<p>• The SLD cloud development &amp; calibration in the IRT needs to be 
</p>
<p>expanded also to consider 
</p>
<p>▪ supercooling deficit of large drops 
</p>
<p>• New methodology to obtain the supercooling deficit of SLD clouds 
</p>
<p>generated in the IRT test section is needed
</p>
