Surface Power</p>

<p>POWER
The key commodity needed to utilize the Lunar surface
</p>
<p>Illumination
The scarce resource needed to produce power
</p>
<p>Polar Illumination Limits
• Intermittent with up to 3-day periods of continuous darkness
• Highly dependent on location/elevation
</p>
<p>Equatorial Illumination Limits
• Cyclical  periods of 14 days illuminated, 14 days (340 hours) dark
• Consistent</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Lyndon B. Johnson Space Center Rice: Cis-Lunar Infrastructure November 4, 2023 6
</p>
<p>At 10 m above grade, installations along the South 
Pole “Shackleton Connecting Ridge” area can 
require energy storage to operate for periods as 
long as 3 days during the Winter Months.
</p>
<p>Installations in certain Connecting Ridge locations 
can operate across consecutive Summer months 
with no draw on stored energy.
</p>

</p>
<p>by Earth’s magnetic field.

</p>
<p>in Low Earth Orbit
