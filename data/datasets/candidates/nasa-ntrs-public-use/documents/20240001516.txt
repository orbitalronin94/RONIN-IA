</p>

</p>
<p>Microbiology Laboratory.

</p>

</p>
<p>Environmental Lab.
</p>
<p>• JPL - Microbial Tracking 1 and 2.

</p>
<p>locations.</p>

<p>5
</p>
<p>Microbial Isolate Database 
</p>
<p>Data will allow analysis of microbial 
</p>
<p>community trends of the ISS environment.
</p>
<p>• Acquire and organize ISS microbial 
</p>
<p>isolate data since 2001
• ISS Air
</p>
<p>• ISS Surface
</p>
<p>• Veggie, APH, XROOTS
</p>
<p>• Ground Surfaces including vehicles and 
</p>
<p>payload hardware to be launched
</p>
<p>• Developed a series of searchable fields
</p>
<p>• Developing a tutorial for use
</p>
<p>• Over 3200 entries of cultured isolates</p>
<p></p>
</div>

</p>

</p>
<p>• ISS location
</p>
<p>• ISS mission/increment
</p>

</p>

</p>
<p>• Sample type (i.e. Root, Leaves, Substrate)
</p>
<p>• Plant type (if applicable)
</p>
<p>• Microbial category  
</p>
<p>• Genus
</p>
<p>• Species
</p>
<p>• Method (of identification)
</p>
<p>• Lab (source)
</p>
<p>• APC
</p>
<p>• Fungal CFU
</p>
<p>• LSDA submission
