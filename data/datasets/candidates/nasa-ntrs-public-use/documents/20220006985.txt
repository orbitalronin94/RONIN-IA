• Key Observations
• Tailoring the aggregation orbit significantly improves architecture feasibility and “optimality”
• Launch vehicles are a significant constraint in the NTP architecture

• Commercial launch vehicle fairing volumes limit LH2 tank volumes
• Limited SLS launch and production rates prevent taking full advantage of its capabilities

• Opportunities for Improvement
• Incorporate cost analysis insights to capture opportunities for significant cost savings
• Leverage excess performance on Cargo missions to pre-position return propellant at Mars

• MTAS has developed a highly capable NTP architecture providing multiple approaches 
to enabling a human Mars mission in the 2030s

• Modular vehicle design provides "right sizing" flexibility for many different mission profiles and 
objectives

• NTP performance delivers shorter roundtrip times, reducing cumulative crew radiation dose
