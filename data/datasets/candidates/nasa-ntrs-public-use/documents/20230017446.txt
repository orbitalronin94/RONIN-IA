<p>Use NASA Earthdata in the Cloud
</p>
<p>Why did NASA pick 
the cloud?
</p>
<p>1. Scalability 
The size and use of the Earthdata archive can expand easily and rapidly as needed.
</p>
<p>2. Flexibility
Mission needs can dictate options for selecting operating systems, programming 
languages, databases, and other criteria to enable the best use of mission data. 
</p>
<p>Cloud flexibility allows users to choose which NASA Earthdata service works best 
for their use case, like Giovanni, CMR or Harmony.
</p>
<p>3. Reduced Redundancy
The use of a common infrastructure with cloud native services will reduce redundant tools 
and services, enable sharing, and enforce the use of community standards as well as 
uniform policies and processes.
</p>
<p>Why should you 
consider the cloud?
</p>
<p>1. Equal Access to High Performance 
Computing Adjacent to the Data
</p>
<p>2. Rapid Deployment
• Scale and orchestrate analysis with ECS (Elastic Container Service), EKS (Elastic 
</p>
<p>Kubernetes Service) and Fargate
• Stand up analysis pipelines with CloudFormation and Code
</p>
<p>• Maintain databases without DBAs (Database Administrator(s))
• Develop and deploy machine learning processes with Sagemaker
</p>
<p>• Reproduce your science with Jupyter Notebooks
</p>
<p>3. Cost Effectiveness
• Rent large amounts of compute for short periods of time
</p>
<p>• Economy of scale: Oracle RDS (Relational Database Service) is generally cheaper 
than bringing your own Oracle license to your compute
</p>
<p>• Underserved communities can benefit from the cloud
</p>
<p>Where can you get 
started? 
</p>

cloud resources such as NASA 
</p>

</p>

below!
</p>

<p>Giovanni in the 
Cloud
</p>
<p>Common Metadata 
Repository (CMR) Harmony
</p>
<p>The evolution of data in NASA's EOSDIS
(Earth Observing System Data and 
</p>
<p>Information System) collection is expected 
to lead to a new paradigm in how earth 
</p>
<p>data are used. Rather than individual data 
users downloading data from multiple 
</p>
<p>sources for analysis on their local 
machines, Big Data will be most efficiently 
</p>
<p>used by working close to the data and 
conducting analyses in the cloud, then 
downloading only the results. This also 
</p>
<p>will facilitate more communal use of data 
in the cloud by teams of researchers.
</p>
