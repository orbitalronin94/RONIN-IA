Compact Total Irradiance Monitor-Flight Demo (CTIM-FD)

• Built by LASP
</p>
<p>CTIM: 11kg CubeSat 
• Built by LASP
</p>
<p>TSIS-1: 363kg 
• Built by LASP
• Mounted to the ISS
</p>
<p>SORCE: 290kg 
• On an Orbital 
</p>
<p>LEOStar-2 bus
</p>
<p>Measures solar spectral irradiance (SSI), and monitoring Total Solar Irradiance (TSI) to explore how 
solar variability impacts the Earth’s climate, contributing to long-term continuity measurements from 
TSIS SIM/TIM and SORCE SIM/TIM</p>
