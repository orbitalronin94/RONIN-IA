<div class="slide-content"><p>Planetary Entry</p>
<p>4</p>
<p>Thermal Protection System (TPS) protects spacecraft from harsh heating </p>
<p>TPS materials are typically thermally robust, low-density insulators that are inherently fragile</p>
<p>For the human-rated Orion vehicle, some crucial parts of the TPS need to be extremely strong as they are part of the vehicle structure</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide4_rId2"></div>
<div class="embedded" id="slide4_rId3"></div>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Why is 3D-MAT Needed for Orion?</p>
<p>5</p>
<p>Compression Pads Have Severe Requirements</p>
<p>Must be extremely strong due to high mechanical loads</p>
<p>Must survive an explosive bolt pyroshock </p>
<p>Must survive extremely hot re-entry temperatures (~3300 °F) </p>
<p>3D-MAT was invented to enable Orion Artemis missions</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide5_rId3"></div>
<div class="embedded" id="slide5_rId4"></div>
<p>Compression Pad Locations </p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide5_rId5"></div>

<p>Module</p>
<p>European Service</p>
<p>Module</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide5_rId6"></div>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<div class="slide-notes"><p>Supports / carry  entire weight / load of CM.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Compression pads are interface between Crew Module &amp; Service Module and are required to withstand:</p>
<p>Launch &amp; ascent structural loads, pyro-shock, Earth re-entry</p>
<p></p>
<p>5</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Prior State of the Art Insufficient for Artemis</p>
<p>6</p>
<p>Orion’s Entry Flight Test-1 (Earth orbit) in 2014 verified that the best structural ablator we had would not work for future missions beyond low Earth orbit</p>
<p></p>
<p>Significantly higher loads for Artemis lunar missions meant that no known material would work</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p></p>

<p></p>

<p>6</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>What is 3D-MAT?</p>
<p>7</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide7_rId2"></div>
<div class="embedded" id="slide7_rId3"></div>
<p>Quartz Yarn</p>
<p>3D Woven Preform</p>
<p>3D Weaving</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide7_rId4"></div>
<p>Resin Infusion</p>
<p> </p>
<p>3D-MAT is a composite material</p>
<p>Made from 3D weaving quartz yarns &amp; infusing with a cyanate ester resin</p>
<p>3D orthogonal weave with high fiber volume maximizes the material strength</p>
<p>Resin infusion to fully densify the material (no pores) locks in the strength &amp; robustness</p>
<p></p>
<p>13 x 12 x 3 inches</p>
<p>3D Composite Billet</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide7_rId6"></div>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<p></p>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Automated Weaving Breakthrough</p>
<p>8</p>
<p>Developed new Jaquard loom harness design to individually control 5000 yarns</p>
<p></p>
<p>First achievement of continuous, automated 3D orthogonal weaving at large scale (12” x 3”) &amp; high fiber volume (60%)</p>
<p></p>
<p>4 times larger cross section than prior state   of the art was a major disruptive breakthrough</p>
<p></p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide8_rId3"></div>
<p>Bally Ribbon Mills</p>
<div class="embedded" id="slide8_rId4"></div>
<p>3D-MAT developed hardware</p>

<div class="slide-notes"><p>5000? Individual warp yarns individually controlled</p>
<p>~50 layers built up from top to bottom prior to advancing one linear step</p>
<p>Large infrastructure of fibers on the creel etc.</p>

</div>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Game Changing Innovation in Resin Infusion</p>
<p>10</p>
<p>Full resin densification of large preforms established for the first time (&lt;1% porosity) </p>
<p>Maximized the strength of the 3D-MAT composite</p>
<p>Factor of 12 times thicker than prior state of the art for full densification</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>3D-MAT: Breakthrough Composite</p>
<p>11</p>
<p>The combination of materials and architecture result in an extremely high-performance composite unlike any other</p>
<p></p>
<p>Compared to 2D carbon phenolic used on Orion EFT-1, 3D-MAT is:</p>
<p>9 times stronger in tensile strength (z)</p>
<p>3 times stronger in shear strength</p>
<p>25% lower in thermal conductivity</p>
<p></p>
