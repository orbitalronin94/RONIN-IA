<p>Regenerative Fuel Cell Technology
</p>
<p>4
</p>
<p>Technology Product Capability:  Develop RFC energy storage system technology that can provide sustained 
and reliable electrical power for lunar surface and near-surface missions where photovoltaics/battery or 
nuclear options may not be feasible; advance integrated RFC from TRL3 to at least TRL5 for lunar surface 
applications.
</p>
<p>What is an RFC?
An energy storage system that utilizes hydrogen and 
oxygen gases to store energy.
</p>
<p>Why? 
Higher specific energy (W·hr/kg) for high energy 
applications where fully packaged battery systems 
become too massive.</p>
<p></p>

<p>Known Technical Gaps
</p>
<p>18
</p>
<p>SPACE
</p>
<p>1. Availability:
</p>
<p>o New technologies not yet flight qualified for 
microgravity applications
</p>
<p>o No flight-qualified fuel cell since the end of the Space 
Shuttle Program
</p>
<p>o Domestic Industrial supply chain compromised
</p>
<p>2. Operational Life:
</p>
<p>o Pure oxygen reactants provide challenging 
operational environment
</p>
<p>o Pure water introduces long-term failure mechanisms
</p>
<p>o Space Missions have limited maintenance options
</p>
<p>o Long dormancy periods with large thermal variations
</p>
<p>3. System Integration:
</p>
<p>o Advantageously leveraging different systems to 
reduce overall vehicle mass 
</p>
<p>o Putting it all together in a low-mass cost-effective 
package
</p>
<p>o Demonstrating component and system reliability 
</p>
<p>4. Specific Energy: 
</p>
<p>o Increase system-level specific energy to increase 
vehicle payload capacity
</p>
<p>AERONAUTICS
</p>
<p>1. Thermal Management:
</p>
<p>o High Power applications = large thermal loads 
</p>
<p>o Fundamental electrochemical technologies limited 
by thermal management 
</p>
<p>o Electric aircraft have multiple distributed thermal 
loads
</p>
<p>o Advanced Hydrogen combustion technologies have 
localized thermal loads
</p>
<p>2. Manufacturing Scale:
</p>
<p>o Domestic supply chain capabilities limit component 
size / scale
</p>
<p>3. Power Management and Distribution:
</p>
<p>o High Electrical Current
</p>
<p>o High Power / High Voltage Conversion
</p>
<p>o Wiring mass
</p>
<p>4. On-board Hydrogen Management:
</p>
<p>o Cryogenic Storage
</p>
<p>o Hydrogen Monitoring 
</p>
<p>o Hydrogen Materials
</p>
<p>5. System Integration:
</p>
<p>o Putting it all together in a cost-effective 
package for commercial applications</p>
