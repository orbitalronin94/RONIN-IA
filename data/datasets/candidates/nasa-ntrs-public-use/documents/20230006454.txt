<p>Introduction (1)
</p>
<p>• Stirling convertors use a linear alternator to 
extract power from the piston motion; this 
requires a coil and a magnet (Faraday’s 
law)
</p>
<p>• The coil introduces unwanted phase shift 
to degrade power output as well as 
introduce instability; i.e., low power factor
</p>
<p>• Tuning capacitor is added to increase 
power output and stability; widely accepted 
that tuning capacitor value will affect both 
the power output and stability</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Introduction (2)
• It is widely accepted that tuning capacitor 
</p>
<p>value affects both the power output and 
stability: “The Conventional Theory”
</p>
<p>• Also, according to this theory, the tuning 
capacitor value for the maximum power 
output is different from that for the highest 
stability.
</p>
<p>• As a result, two separate tuning capacitor 
values were suggested during launch and 
mission.
</p>
<p>• However, recent modeling analysis 
suggested that this may not be correct.
</p>

<p>Effect on Power Output
• This test was conducted in 2022 to understand the 
</p>
<p>effect of tuning capacitor value on the power output.
</p>
<p>• Same convertor used in the vibration test was tested.
</p>
<p>• Test Conditions
- Hot-end ~ 760°C / Cold-end ~ 40°C
- Pressure ~ 490psig
</p>
<p>- Piston amp ~ 4.3mm
</p>
<p>- Operating frequency ~102Hz
</p>
<p>- Tuning cap value: 840𝜇𝐹, 1040𝜇𝐹, 1240𝜇𝐹
</p>
<p>• Test results show that the effect of the tuning 
capacitor value on the power output is negligible.
</p>
<p>• Power output results also in agreement with the 
simulation results but NOT with the conventional 
theory.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>What Does This Mean?
• Previously, two separate tuning capacitor 
</p>
<p>values were suggested during launch and 
mission.
</p>
<p>• However, according to this study, this is not 
necessary; we can use a single tuning 
capacitor value that maximizes the 
stability. 
</p>
<p>• Therefore, no need for additional 
communication and control.
</p>

<p>Effect on Start-Up Behavior
</p>
<p>• During the power output testing, we observed that 
the rate of the change of the piston amplitude over 
hot-end temperature changed with the tuning 
capacitor value.
</p>
<p>• Assuming the rate of the change of the piston 
amplitude over hot-end temperature is constant, 
the rate is inversely proportional to the tuning 
capacitor value. 
</p>
<p>• We believe this is related to the impedance of the 
tuning capacitor. Further study needed.
</p>
<p>*Note that a sharp change in piston happened when the 
</p>
<p>amplitude of the AC bus power supply was increased.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Conclusion
• According to the conventional theory, different tuning capacitor values are 
</p>
<p>required during launch (requiring higher stability) and mission (requiring higher 
power output); this complicates communication and control system.
</p>
<p>• According to the new theory presented in this paper, however, the effect of the 
tuning capacitor value on the power output is negligible; therefore, no need for 
additional communication and control. 
</p>
<p>• Also, tuning capacitor value affects the start-up behavior of the Stirling 
convertor; this may require a different controller design. Further study needed.</p>
