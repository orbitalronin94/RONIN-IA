<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Supporting Spacecraft and Launch 
Vehicle Development 
</p>
<p>• The Natural Environments (NE) Branch is within the MSFC Engineering Directorate’s 
Spacecraft and Vehicle System (S&amp;VS) Department
</p>
<p>• S&amp;VS plans, performs and directs the technical Design, Analysis, Test, Evaluation, Verification, 
Integration, and Applied Research and Development of future Spacecraft and Launch Vehicle 
Systems
</p>
<p>• Current programs we support - Space Launch System, Human Landing System, Mars Ascent 
Vehicle, Orion, Gateway   
</p>
<p>• NE maintains expertise in terrestrial, space, and planetary natural environments 
definition, characterization, and analysis 
</p>
<p>• Responsibility to disseminate this expertise to Agency programs and projects in support of 
environment definition, requirements development, vehicle development, operations, and 
sustaining engineering through program’s life cycle
</p>
<p>• Branch has two teams; Terrestrial and Planetary Environments (T&amp;PE) and Space Environments 
(SE) 
</p>
<p>• T&amp;PE has extensive expertise characterizing terrestrial surface and atmospheric 
(aloft) environments
</p>
<p>• Winds, temperature, humidity, pressure, lightning, density, precipitation, radiant energy, 
aerosols, sea state, sea surface temperature 
</p>
<p>• Maintains climatological databases for engineering analyses 3</p>
<p></p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Space Launch System (SLS)
</p>
<p>• The four Natural Environments owned Launch Commit Criteria (LCC) (two 
temperature, one precipitation, and one wind) were updated and baselined for 
Artemis II.
</p>
<p>• There were no changes from Artemis I to the temperature constraints for tanking or 
launch.
</p>
<p>• The wind and precipitation LCCs were updated to only be monitored from L-3 hours to 
internal power instead of from L-7 hours to internal power.
</p>
<p>• The wind placard was updated from an omnidirectional 24kt at 60ft placard to a 24kt to 
34.4kt at 60ft placard (graphic on next slide).
</p>
<p>• Worked with KSC Weather office on updates to the Program Requirements 
Document for the Eastern Range.
</p>
<p>• Added need for solar radiation instrumentation.
• Included High-Resolution (HR) balloon need and differentiated between Low-Resolution 
</p>
<p>(LR) and HR by including expected effective vertical resolutions.
4</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Space Launch System (SLS)
</p>
<p>• Directional Wind Placard for Artemis II liftoff peak winds
</p>
<p>Artemis II and beyond peak ground wind limits for launch, at the 
18.3 m (60 ft) level. Radial values are in units of meters per 
second.
</p>
<p>Artemis I peak ground wind limits for launch, at the 18.3 m (60 
ft) level. Radial values are in units of meters per second. 5</p>

<p>Orion/MPCV
</p>
<p>• Analyzed landing availability impacts due to sea conditions due to reducing landing 
range along specified azimuths to mitigate the risk of heat shield damage on 
Artemis II. 
</p>
<p>• Analysis revealed that using the nominal Design Specification for Natural Environments 
(DSNE) limits would be too constraining for landing availability, and that different zero-
margin constraints were used by different vehicle subsystems. 
</p>
<p>• Determined and analyzed an additional constraint set along two entry azimuths at 
bounding entry interface stations with Orion Vehicle Integration Office, Crew Module 
Uprighting System (CMUS), and recovery personnel. 
</p>

<p>DOLILU Support
</p>
<p>• Updating Profile Envision and Splicing Tool (PrESTo) and Weather 
Analysis Display Tool (WAND) ahead of Artemis II support.
</p>
<p>• Updating user interface to improve human factors. 
• Adding capabilities within PrESTo to splice balloons together that end at differing 
</p>
<p>heights, using the most up-to-date cycle balloons, and allowing a splicing of TDRWP 
straight into the Earth-GRAM monthly mean if no high balloon is available. 
</p>
<p>• Training new team members to support DOLILU operations. 
</p>
<p>• Working to implement new Orion DOLILU assessments into the process.
• DOLILU documentation will be updated to capture the new processes.
• Natural Environments will be creating an additional design profile for the Orion team 
</p>
<p>during DOLILU operations.
</p>
<p>• Received funding to support two DOLILU Joint Integration Sims with live-
balloon data in FY2024.
</p>
<p>• Program schedule of November 2024 launch date.

<p>On-Going Work
</p>
<p>• AMPS replacement
• Working on estimating the Effective Vertical Resolution of the new Vaisala system 
</p>
<p>based on simultaneous releases from Vandenberg Space Force Base.
• MW41 conditional certification - Validation of records on MW41 needs to be done if

<p>measurements from system are used for any analyses.

</p>

</p>
<p>environments. 
• Concern exists that air temperature thresholds for propellent freezing could occur 
</p>
<p>during transport from the landing site back to San Diego leading to hardware damage.

</p>

</p>
<p>exceeding abort sea conditions after including the vehicle’s probability of
