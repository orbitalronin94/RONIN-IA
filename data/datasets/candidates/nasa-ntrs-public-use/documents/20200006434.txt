Take home message
• The Super Resolution Altimetry technique provides improved
CALIPSO surface elevation estimates with a precision of better
than 1.4 m.
• Canopy penetration differences at 532 and 1064 nm indicate a
moderately strong relationship for estimating forest canopy
heights.
• Results indicate that CALIOP-derived canopy heights are well
correlated with the ICESat derived canopy heights.

CALIOP 30º off-nadir measurement for ocean
subsurface studies
Why pointing CALIOP 30 degree off-nadir:
avoid ocean surface backscatter

1. Direct demonstration of CALIOP ocean subsurface signals in both co-polarization and
cross-polarization to convince the community that CALIOP can measure ocean
subsurface particulate backscatter
2. Direct measurements of particulate depolarization ratio in water to improve CALIOP
estimate of phytoplankton backscatter and biomass estimate.
Lu, X, et.al. Optics
Express, 2016

CALIOP ocean subsurface signals on July 17, 2014

measurements. Observations suggest that ocean surface backscatter at 532 nm is about 30%
less than ocean surface backscatter at 1064 nm.

Take home message
• CALIOP 532nm perpendicular channel can provide information
about the water diffuse attenuation coefficient (Kd), particulate
backscattering coefficient bbp (/m), and ocean subsurface
backscatter. Results are in good agreement with MODIS results.
• CALIOP 532nm perpendicular channel and 1064nm channel
can provide bathymetry study. Preliminary results indicate that
CALIOP-derived water depth are well correlated with the
British oceanographic water depth.
• CALIOP provides unique high latitude winter measurements
for studying bloom cycle, and ocean par.

Summary and Future work
Operational Achievements:
• Long term measurements: CALIOP collected more than 12 years of measurements so far;
• Observations during day/night and for all seasons
• Data publicly available
• Other applications:

Altimetry; (Hu et. al., 2007; Lu et.al,2014)
Surface type distinguish, Arctic sea ice trend; (Lu et.al, 2016, 2017)
Ocean subsurface; (Behrenfeld et. al., 2013, 2016; Lu et.al, 2014, 2016)
Ocean surface-wind speed (Hu et.al, 2007)

Future work:
• Global diffuse attenuation coefficient study, especially in polar region,
where MODIS has limited results. Day and night difference.
• Bathymetry of shallow water, Pond depths study over Greenland,
melting water mass, and its relation with Greenland glacier loss
• Snow grain size retrieval from CALIOP surface reflectance at 532nm
and 1064nm
