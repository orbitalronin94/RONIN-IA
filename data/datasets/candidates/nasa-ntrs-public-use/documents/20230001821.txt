<p>What was the goal of the test effort?
</p>
<p>Commissioning a new or upgraded facility is needed to verify the vibration test facility, including its test personnel and test procedures, 
</p>
<p>meet requirements and can perform testing in a safe and efficient manner. Commissioning activities are even more important for 
</p>
<p>complex facilities such as the Mechanical Vibration Facility. 
</p>
<p>MVF Commissioning – 2021 
</p>
<p>• Facility commissioning needed to prepare for the Dream Chaser spaceplane sine vibration test campaign scheduled to begin August 2023. The Dream 
</p>
<p>Chaser spaceplane is a product of Sierra Space and is the first-ever winged commercial spaceplane. Dream Chaser will support NASA resupply missions 
</p>
<p>for the International Space Station. 
</p>
<p>• The Dream Chaser spaceplane will be the first ever flight hardware tested at the facility. 
</p>
<p>• A head expander (HE) for the MVF table that provides a continuous flat mounting surface with a maximum diameter of 16.25-ft was installed on the 
</p>
<p>MVF table for the first time April 2021. The HE will be used for the Dream Chaser sine vibration test campaign.
</p>
<p>• The Dream Chaser lateral axis test specification starts at 2 Hz and response limiting will be needed below 5 Hz. 
</p>
<p>• Due to lack of resources, need, and personnel availability, MVF largely remained idle from 2016 to 2021. Therefore, commissioning activities began 
</p>
<p>early 2021 with the simplest test configuration possible – a bare vibration table. </p>

<p>2021 MVF Commissioning Verification Test Article – MPCV Orion 
</p>
<p>Crew Module Launch Abort System (CM-LAS) Mass Simulator
</p>
<p>• Originally, the CM-LAS mass simulator consisted of a blue painted tubular steel 
</p>
<p>structure and a white painted wood constructed heat shield simulator. The mass 
</p>
<p>simulator interfaced with the primary E-STA structure with 4 base attachment 
</p>
<p>points.
</p>
<p>• The CM-LAS was fixed-base modal tested and its FEM was correlated to test 
</p>
<p>results up to 60 Hz. 
</p>
<p>• Following the completion of the E-STA sine vibration test campaign, CM-LAS 
</p>
<p>remained in storage at SEC.
</p>
<p>• The CM-LAS was chosen as the 2021-2022 VTA for multiple purposes: (1) 
</p>
<p>Commission the MVF head expander with a test article (2) Demonstrate MVF 
</p>
<p>sine vibration response limiting capabilities  (3) Demonstrate MVF sine vibration 
</p>
<p>response limiting capabilities below 5 Hz.
</p>
<p>• For 2021-2022 facility commissioning and customer demonstration, the CM-
</p>
<p>LAS was modified by removing its wood constructed heat shield simulator 
</p>
<p>component and increasing the base attachment points from 4 to 6. The yellow 
</p>
<p>pedestals used for the 6 attachment points were also new.
</p>
<p>• Prior to commissioning and demonstration activities, the new CM-LAS 
</p>
<p>configuration was not modal tested so model correlation activities were not 
</p>
<p>possible. This meant that response limits for the test article were defined using an 
</p>
<p>uncorrelated model. 
</p>

<p>What did we learn?
</p>
<p>Figure 10: CM-LAS Hard Mount X-axis Control &amp; Response Plot
</p>
<p>The Structural Analyst on this project defined acceleration response limits to eliminate the risk of damage to the test article incurred by resonance.
</p>
<p>‘ST1X’ Response limit
</p>
<p>‘DL1R’ Response limit
</p>
<p>All instrumentation is given a code 
</p>
<p>(up to 5 digits) for the facility data 
</p>
<p>acquisition system. This code is 
</p>
<p>generally shorthand that indicates 
</p>
<p>where the instrumentation is located 
</p>
<p>on the test article. 
</p>
<p>‘ST1X’ – Vertical accelerometer on 
</p>
<p>the stub of the CM-LAS, East 
</p>
<p>quadrant 
</p>
<p>‘DL1R’ – Radial accelerometer on the 
</p>
<p>lower diagonal of the CM-LAS, East 
</p>
<p>quadrant 
</p>
<p>The plot shows that the wide notch of concern is due to engagement of the 
</p>
<p>‘ST1X’ and ‘DL1R’ response limits. The response limits engage when an 
</p>
<p>accelerometer designated in the control software as a response limit crosses 
</p>
<p>the set acceleration threshold and disengages when the response is below that 
</p>
<p>set threshold. 
</p>
<p>The modal test results helped further explain the vibration test results.   </p>

<p>Figure 11: ‘DL1R’ and ‘ST1X’ Modal Test Results

<p>Multiple Input Hammer Test – 2 reference channels per response channel
</p>
<p>Modal test results indicated that the width of the 
</p>
<p>response limited region from 56-80 Hz was due to 
</p>
<p>closely spaced modes which all had similar levels 
</p>
<p>of response. 
</p>
<p>• During commissioning, these closely spaced 
</p>
<p>modes made it difficult to commission the 
</p>
<p>vibration control software
</p>
<p>The damping values of the CM-LAS modes were 
</p>
<p>all well below the damping of a well-designed test 
</p>
<p>article. 
</p>
<p>• Explains the need for deep notching to keep the 
</p>
<p>response level of limit channels below the abort 
</p>
<p>level set in the system
</p>
<p>With better understanding of the CM-LAS response 
</p>
<p>limits defined by the Structural Analyst and a better 
</p>
<p>understanding of the CM-LAS dynamics, the test 
</p>
<p>team was able to focus on commissioning the 
</p>
<p>vibration control software.

<p>Verification Test Article Design Considerations
</p>
<p>• Mass and strength properties 
</p>
<p>• Does the VTA have mass properties that encompass the heaviest/largest anticipated test article?
</p>
<p>• Does the VTA have sufficient strength to withstand the inertial loading produced by the anticipated 
</p>
<p>highest level test spectra?
</p>
<p>• Modal properties
</p>
<p>• Does the VTA have repeated or very closely spaced modes?
</p>
<p>• Should the VTA be relatively stiff driving its fundamental high effective mass modal frequencies up or 
</p>
<p>should it be soft to lower them?
</p>
<p>• Is the VTA damping approximately the same as the anticipated test article?
</p>
<p>• Response limits
</p>
<p>• If acceleration measurements are to be used for response limiting, was the VTA FEM used to define the 
</p>
<p>response limits correlated to modal test data?
</p>
<p>A well-designed verification test article is essential for facility commissioning success.</p>
