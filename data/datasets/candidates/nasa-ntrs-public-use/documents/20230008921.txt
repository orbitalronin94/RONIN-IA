<div class="slide-content"><p>Traditional QML/QPL Challenges for ARC</p>
<p>Cost</p>
<p>Prohibited MOQ (Unit Cost Not Main Blocker)</p>
<p>Schedule</p>
<p>Lengthy Procurement Process &amp; Delivery (6~12 months not uncommon)</p>
<p>Tight Design &amp; Prototype Schedule</p>
<p>Inflexibility</p>
<p>Design Changes  Cost &amp; Schedule Loop</p>
<p>No Alternatives/Replacements</p>
<p>Size, Weight &amp; Power</p>
<p>Performance &amp; Functionality</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Ames COTS Parts Selection Strategy</p>
<p>Careful COTS</p>
<p>MIL/Automotive/Industrial/Commercial Grades</p>
<p>Wide Design Margins (Temperature, Voltage, Power)</p>
<p>Commonly Used Parts (Huge Production Volume)</p>
<p>Flight Heritage</p>
<p>Trusted Manufacturers &amp; Distributors</p>
<p>Limited screening including GIDEP search, visual inspection, counterfeit controls</p>
<p>Board/System/Mission-Level Testing</p>
<p>Risk mitigation at board/system level</p>
<p>Extensive testing with multiple design revisions</p>
<p>Test on orbits and in low-cost missions</p>

<div class="embedded" id="slide6_rId3"></div>

<p>6</p>
</div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>Observations in ARC Missions</p>
<p>Design &amp; Workmanship Issues &gt;&gt; Component Failures </p>
<p>QPL Approach Needs More Resources</p>
<p>Less upper-level testing (Undetected bugs)</p>
<p>QPL (Less integrated) Often Requires More Supporting Circuits</p>
<p>Up-Screened Components Would Not Have Saved/Improved Most ARC Missions.</p>
<p>COTS Benefits ARC:</p>
<p>Wider selections, faster development schedule, lower costs</p>
<p>Few radiation test data, but significantly more use cases</p>
<p>Solid design/testing has better returns than solid parts</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Radiation Risks for COTS Approach</p>
<p>Lack of Radiation Test Data on COTS</p>
<p>Mitigations</p>
<p>Software (TMR), EDAC</p>
<p>Over-current detection &amp; protection.</p>
<p>Redundant Design</p>
<p>Watchdog Timers (H/W &amp; SW)</p>
<p>Selective Radiation Tolerant Components</p>
<p>QPL Engineering Unit, COTS Equivalent RT-Parts (same die)</p>
<p>COTS with radiation test data</p>
<p>FRAM, MRAM, GaN, SiC</p>

<p>COTS has better SWaP &amp; performance</p>
<p>Component failures are very rare among ARC missions</p>
<p>Higher grade parts would not have saved most missions.</p>
<p>Radiation test data on COTS is typically not available</p>
<p>Risk Awareness</p>
<p>Risk Mitigations (Proper S/W &amp; H/W Design, Careful Parts Selection)</p>
<p>Risk Taking (Stakeholder buy-ins)</p>
<p>Parts availability, performance, cost &amp; schedule remain key drivers for ARC to pursue COTS solutions for high risk &amp; high reward missions</p>
<p>ARC has 30+ successful projects with COTS (past 17 years)</p>
<p>COTS approach (potentially) good for higher classes of missions too</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Additional COTS Benefits</p>
<p>Benefits</p>
<p>Wide Part Selections &amp; Design Flexibility</p>
<p>Size, Weight &amp; Power</p>
<p>Fewer Components =&gt; Improved Reliability</p>
<p>More time available for thorough testing of designs</p>
<p>Far Lower Cost &amp; Shorter Schedule to Accomplish Missions</p>
<p>COTS may not have radiation test data; but has millions of samples over multitude of applications to establish quality &amp; reliability</p>
<p>Any parts can fail, a solid design beats a solid part</p>
<p>Proven Successes Over 30+ Spaceflight Missions (17 years)</p>
