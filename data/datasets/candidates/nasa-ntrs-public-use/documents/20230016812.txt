<div class="slide-content"><p>Introduction</p>
<p>Motivation: Fuel injector design, leading to efficient fuel-air mixing, combustion, and flameholding, remains one of the key challenges for scramjet propulsion systems.</p>
<p>Reynolds-averaged simulations (RAS), “calibrated” with experiments, continue to be the primary research tool for scramjets.</p>
<p>RAS turbulence models and their parameters, like the turbulent Schmidt number (Sct), are usually selected based on expert experience and available experimental data.</p>
<p>In very rare cases, performance metrics of practical interest may not be (very) sensitive to modeling choices. More often their sensitivities are significant, especially in reacting flows.</p>
<p>In absence of experiments or in conjunction with experiments, scale resolving simulations, such as large-eddy simulations (LES) can be leveraged to inform RAS modeling choices.</p>
<p>Goal: Compare the mixing efficiency obtained using RAS, LES, and experiments and investigate the applicability of LES to serve as a surrogate or augment for the experimental data</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>16</p>
<p>Summary and Conclusions</p>
<p>Mixing efficiency was obtained for a strut fuel injector at hypervelocity flow conditions using RAS, LES, and experiments.</p>
<p>Current work demonstrated two ways in which LES can be leveraged to improve RAS predictions:</p>
<p>Direct tuning of the Sct in RAS (can match LES but potentially for the wrong reasons) </p>
<p>Use of least-squares “best-fit” approach to determine the most appropriate value of Sct , allowing for a selection of a turbulence model</p>
<p>Grid convergence index (GCI) was also used in an unconventional way to estimate where a further refined LES solution may reside.</p>
<p>Albeit both RAS and LES underpredicted the experimental data, LES appears to predict a region of suppressed mixing identified in the experiments. Additional experimental data are needed to verify this.</p>
<p>LES was successfully leveraged to improve RAS predictions, and identified Wilcox-2006-NP turbulence model with Sct of 0.7 as a good candidate for this problem.</p>
