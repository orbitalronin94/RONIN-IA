The High Spectral Resolution Lidar (HSRL)
technique adds a second detector channel with a
high precision filter that can separate the
backscattered returns from particles from the
frequency-broadened molecular signal. This
second channel means there are two equations
for two unknowns, making the lidar equation
fully determined, unlike a single-channel system.

True backscatter coefficient
• Direct measure of backscatter (geophysical quantity) rather than attenuated backscatter (integrated quantity)
• Accurate at all altitudes; errors do not accumulate with range
• Can be averaged more than attenuated backscatter (if necessary) since there is no dependence on overlying layers
Attenuated backscatter is the quantity measured directly by a
backscatter lidar (e.g. CALIPSO). It is the true aerosol backscatter
coefficient times an exponential attenuation factor, which
represents light that is lost along the optical path.

Constrained retrieval (attenuated
backscatter from HSRL instrument+ AOD
constraint) produces errors in extinction
due to inhomogeneity in lidar ratio. Note,
error bars increase with decreasing altitude.

• Constrained extinction builds up
greater levels of uncertainty as it
penetrates deeper into the
atmosphere.
• Constrained extinction has
systematic uncertainty when the
aerosol column is heterogeneous
due to assumption of constant
lidar ratio.
• Constrained extinction retrieval
is sensitive to differences in field
of view, particular in
heterogeneous atmosphere.
• Passive AOT retrieval has more
errors near clouds, and over
bright or heterogeneous surfaces.
• Passive AOT availability is poor in
certain scenarios: the night side,
polar regions, and broken cloud.
The ability to measure extinction
without constraint means
• More retrievals in difficult
scenarios (i.e. broken clouds,
heterogeneous aerosol) —
particularly relevant for aerosol
indirect effect
• More accurate retrievals in the
boundary layer — relevant for
aerosol indirect effect and air
quality

Aerosol-Cloud Interactions

Houston TX, September 11, 2013

• Better accuracy low in the atmosphere and in heterogeneous situations means more retrievals in proximity to clouds.
• Extinction and microphysical retrievals provide better proxies for vertically resolved CCN.
• High sensitivity to vertically resolved tenuous aerosol and cloud layers gives input (quantity, type) on ice nucleii (IN)
in proximity to cirrus.

• Lidar ratio gives the most information of all intensive parameters about aerosol composition for
aerosols other than dust.
• Particulate depolarization is also more accurate in HSRL system, since separating particulate and
molecular parts requires accurate backscatter.
• Extinction angstrom exponent is more directly related to particle size than backscatter angstrom
exponent.
• Backscatter angstrom exponent from HSRL is more accurate and precise than from single
channel backscatter instrument.

What about simple
backscatter lidar + AOT
constraint?

• No need for inferred/modeled lidar ratio or external constraint.
• No accumulation of error from bootstrapping down into the atmosphere.
• Molecular channel provides direct measure of AOT at higher resolution than passive, without cloud
adjacency or surface artifacts.
• No need for layer detection. Extinction can be retrieved (with error bars) even if it is near zero.

are used to infer aerosol

• Aerosol typing is useful for model assessment/assimilation since it gives information on which species
in the model need to adjust to match observations, instead of changing all species proportionately.
• Relevant to direct radiative forcing because it gives a proxy for anthropogenic fraction.
• Relevant to indirect radiative forcing because it informs on particle type and activation potential.

• Because HSRL backscatter and extinction don’t suffer from increased
calibration error at low altitudes, HSRL allows accurate retrievals in the
boundary layer where aerosol is relevant to air quality and human health.
• Vertically resolved extinction gives a better proxy for PM2.5 than AOD
divided by boundary layer height
• Microphysical retrievals from HSRL alone or HSRL + passive provide number
concentration and size information which give still better proxies for PM2.5
