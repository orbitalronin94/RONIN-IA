<div class="slide-content"><p>What’s my job?</p>
<p>I’m a Materials Scientist / Engineer</p>
<p>Materials scientists study stuff/matter/materials</p>
<p>Materials engineers design materials with specific properties for an application</p>
<p>I work on Thermal Protection Systems</p>
<p>Which protect a spacecraft from the extreme heat generated when it enters the atmosphere of a planet or moon</p>
<p>A big part of my job is using computers to simulate heat shield materials</p>
