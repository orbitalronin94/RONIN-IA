<p>Modeling
</p>
<p>• Liquid modeled as a lumped point mass 
translating within an ellipsoidal constraint 
surface
</p>
<p>– Two particles, one particle per constraint surface
</p>
<p>– Particles have infinitesimal volume
</p>
<p>– (6 RB spacecraft DOF) + 2×(3 point-mass DOF) = 
12 DOF
</p>
<p>– Assumptions:
• Liquid remains cohesive
</p>
<p>• Cylindrical tank with hemispherical end caps
</p>
<p>• Bare tank (i.e., no baffles)
</p>
<p>• Features include:
</p>
<p>– Wall interaction dynamics
</p>
<p>– Variable-size constraint surface as a function of 
</p>
<p>fill ratio
</p>
<p>• Increasing volume decreases range of motion of 
</p>
<p>point masses
</p>

spacecraft

<p>Apollo Era Heritage
</p>
<p>Residual propellant + RCS thruster profile 
= anomalous retrograde motion [1].
</p>
<p>Conclusion from Ref. [2] using a contained particle model.
</p>
<p>Particle model was used to replicate anomalous motion, and then to confirm that the 
corrected thruster profile would eliminate the possibility of retrograde motion [1,2].
</p>
<p>[1] “MSC-03466, Apollo 11 Mission Anomaly Report No. 3, Service Module 
Entry,” NASA Apollo 11 Technical Crew Briefing, vol. 2, Nov. 1970.
[2] Merchant, Gates, and Murray, “Prediction of Apollo Service Module Motion 
after Jettison,” Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets, vol. 8, pp. 587-592, 1971.</p>

<p>Docked Control
</p>
<p>• Flying the tank using 6-DoF LEO trajectory inputs
</p>
<p>• No settling thrust
</p>
<p>• Control inputs applied to maintain constant vehicle 
</p>
<p>orientation (Sun pointing)
</p>
<p>Key Observations:
</p>
<p>• Baffles can keep the fluid aft somewhat, 
</p>
<p>but can introduce some unexpected effects
</p>
<p>• Tendency is for fluid to wet as much of the 
</p>
<p>wall as possible and to rotate around the 
</p>
<p>tank long axis
• Not ideal for transfer 
</p>
<p>• Significant more boiloff
</p>
<p>• Detrimental for vehicle control
</p>
<p>• Use of tangential thrusters tends to unsettle 
</p>
<p>the propellant
</p>
<p>Credit: John Mishic, GRC
</p>
<p>Generic tank configuration
</p>
<p>Possible Solution: use axial thrusters for 
</p>
<p>simultaneous settling and vehicle control </p>

<p>residual propellant before gas is 
</p>
<p>ingested (VPT)

</p>
<p>high rate to low rate.
