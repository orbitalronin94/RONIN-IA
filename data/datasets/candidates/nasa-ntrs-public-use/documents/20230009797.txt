<div class="slide-content"><p>Motivation of this work</p>
<p>Terra and Aqua are drifting. Their mean local overpass time is not maintained anymore. </p>
<p>When the drift exceeds 15 min, Terra and Aqua observations are switched to NOAA 20 observations. </p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="slide-master-content"></div>
<div class="slide-content"><p>CERES observations and surface irradiance computations</p>
<p>Top-of-atmosphere radiative fluxes are derived from observations by CERES instruments.</p>
<p>CERES instruments are on Terra and Aqua and have been observing radiation budget since March 2000.</p>
<p>NOAA20 observations have been used since April 2022.</p>
<p>Surface radiative fluxes are computed using imager derived cloud and aerosol properties and reanalysis temperature and humidity profiles. </p>
<p>Terra + Aqua captures cloud diurnal cycle but using only afternoon orbit (NOAA20) is not sufficient to capture the cloud diurnal cycle. </p>
</div>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Edition 4.2 algorithm change from Edition 4.1</p>
<p>Only MODIS and VIIRS retrieved cloud properties are used. (i.e. No cloud properties derived from geostationary satellites is used.)</p>
<p>MERRA-2 provides temperature and humidity profiles and skin temperature. However, MODIS and VIIRS cloud properties are derived using GEOS-5.4.1. </p>
<p>Climatological adjustment is applied to Terra only (March 2000 through June 2002) and NOAA20 only (April 2022 onward) periods.</p>
<p>Climatologically adjusted flux  for Terra only and NOAA20 only periods is the Terra+Aqua climatological mean flux + anomalies derived from Terra or NOAA20. </p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Summary</p>
<p>Cloud properties derived from one orbit (i.e. Terra only or NOAA20 only) are sufficient to derive surface irradiance anomalies. </p>
<p>Surface irradiances for Edition 4.2 EBAF are computed using only MODIS and VIIRS derived cloud properties (i.e. no geostationary satellite derived clouds).</p>
<p>Uncertainty in Edition 4.2 surface irradiance anomalies is significantly smaller than the uncertainty in Edition 4.1 surface irradiance anomalies. </p>
