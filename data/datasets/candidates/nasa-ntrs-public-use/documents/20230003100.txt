<p>3/8/2023 11
</p>
<p>• Electron screening results in a more 
transparent Coulomb Barrier, shifting the 
Gamow Factor, as if deuterons were at far 
higher energies.
</p>
<p>• This exponentially increases fusion rates.
</p>
<p>• Laboratory astrophysics using accelerated 
deuteron beams across the Periodic Table 
show lattice and plasma screening provide up 
to 3+ keV screening.
</p>
<p>• The PRC Theory Paper indicates a higher 
probability of large angle scattering of 
screened charged particles on screened 
deuterons.
</p>
<p>However, screening is only
effective below 10 keV.
</p>
