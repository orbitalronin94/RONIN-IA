<p>Overview
</p>
<p>Successful Cyber-Physical-Human (CPH) teaming relies on robust autonomy.
• Autonomous actions need to be predictable
</p>
<p>• Uncertainty needs to be understood and managed
</p>
<p>Present the reference problem of agents servicing objectives in an uncertain 
environment.
</p>
<p>• Common problem formulation to every domain
</p>
<p>• Can be simplified for ease of analysis
</p>
<p>Walk through our proposed Autonomous Persistent Intelligent Swarm (APIS) 
approach to robust collaborative autonomy.
</p>
<p>• Demonstrate performance under a variety of conditions and parameters
</p>
<p>• Compare APIS performance to a baseline collaborative approach
2</p>

<p>Definitions
</p>
<p>Agent: A system operating off the common tasking algorithm (in context, either APIS or Auction)
</p>
<p>Objective: A location where an agent may complete a task
</p>
<p>Priority: A measure of the urgency for an agent to complete an objective’s task
</p>
<p>Bid: A measure of the fitness of a particular agent to complete a particular task
</p>
<p>Algorithm Effectiveness: Inverse of the mean priority of all objectives in the area
</p>
<p>Algorithm Robustness: Inverse of the standard deviation of each trial’s mean priority
3</p>

</p>
<p>Balanced parameters not optimal effectiveness;

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Conclusions
</p>
<p>APIS demonstrates promising solution to swarm coordination in uncertain 
environments.
</p>
<p>• More efficient (lower mean objective priority)
</p>
<p>• More reliable (lower trial performance standard deviation)
</p>
<p>• Advantage in efficiency and reliability contingent on uncertain environment
</p>
<p>APIS behavior can be meaningfully altered by varying parameters.
• Higher harvesting chance: more efficient in ideal environment
</p>
<p>• Higher scout/dance chance: more reliable in uncertain environment
</p>
<p>• Key takeaway: increased communication alleviates impact of uncertain conditions
</p>
<p>Abnormally high-performance scout-only APIS variation indicates further role 
integration required.
</p>

<p>Future Work
</p>
<p>Increase design reference mission fidelity – eliminate “simplified” traits.
• Continuous state space
</p>
<p>• More realistic communication modeling
</p>
<p>• More sources of uncertainty in objective
</p>
<p>Compare APIS to more specialized state-of-the-art task assignment strategies.
• Apply state-of-the-art solutions to design reference mission
</p>
<p>• Simulate against APIS to identify strengths and weaknesses
</p>
<p>Test APIS applicability in both air and space domain missions to test algorithm 
applicability and identify more use cases.

teams can reduce trust, and harm

</p>
<p>• Informational errors
</p>
<p>• Communication degradation

<p>Sources of task uncertainty:
• Diagnostic uncertainty

<p>• Servicing uncertainty
