</p>
<p>Conclusions and Future Work
</p>
<p>• For this application and set of assumptions
</p>
<p>1. Mechanically geared is shown to be lightest but benefit decays as torque is reduced
</p>
<p>2. Direct drive is shown to be the most efficient due to low motor electrical frequencies
</p>
<p>3. Magnetic gears require design improvement or reduced margin relative to nominal power to be 
</p>
<p>competitive
</p>
<p>• Future Work:
</p>
<p>1. Incorporate reliability models in the design optimization 
</p>
<p>2. Integration of explicit control response requirements to eliminate torque margin assumptions
</p>
<p>3. Expand studies to different applications, technologies, and power levels
</p>
<p>4. Explore other possible methods of improving magnetic gear performance beyond just reducing 
</p>
<p>airgap</p>
