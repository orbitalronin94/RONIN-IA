Update on SUSAN Concept Vehicle Power and 
Propulsion System



Subsonic Single Aft eNgine (SUSAN) Electrofan:

 180 Passenger regional aircraft concept

 Distributed propulsion system 

 Hybrid electric configuration

 16 electrically driven wing propulsors

 Single aft mounted geared turbofan

 High efficiency electrical system with advanced 
thermal management system

2

Introduction

 Single engine produces power for mission and distributed design
 Increases efficiency of thrust production, reliability

 Reduces maintenance cost, thrust requirements

 Backup power (in case of failure) provided by onboard batteries



3

SUSAN P&P system

• Single geared turbofan engine
• 4 x 5 MW generators
• High efficiency electrical 

distribution system
• Rechargeable battery for 

controls support and electrical 
system stabilization

• Non-rechargeable battery for 
backup in case of system 
failure

• 2 x 0.8 MW motors to run each 
of the 16 wing propulsors



4

Geared Turbofan

Dual spool geared turbofan

 Requirements derived from Boeing 737,  2 x LEAP-1B thrust

 Single engine Technologies :

 High OPR design

 System BPR over 20

 Boundary layer ingestion

 Advanced materials

Thrust split (wing vs. engine) 
tuned based on : 

 weight analysis

Single fault analysis

Advanced materials

 Low fan pressure ratios

 Variable area exhaust nozzles



5

Advanced Engine Control (TEEM)

Turbine electric energy management (TEEM) 

 Make use of power extraction and injection on the high and low power shafts to reduce 
engine transients.

 Safer operation : increased surge margin and more control over map placement



6

Electrical system

 High efficiency megawatt motors (HEMM): goal of 16 kW/kg with 99 efficiency

 Self-cooled superconducting rotor with a semi-slotless stator

 Multi-level switching converters : goal of 20 kW/kg with 99.5% efficiency

 Interleaving filters  with harmonic suppression controller

 Battery DC-DC converters added to stabilize battery voltage over the mission.

Advanced low weight high efficiency electrical system



7

Thermal Management System (TMS)

Two major cooling loops : single-phase

 Electrical system : 60 ˚C, outer mold line cooling with oscillating heat pipes + surface heat 
exchanger

 MIL-PRF-87252E PAO , dielectric coolant for direct cooling methods (immersion)

 Battery : 40 ˚C , using outer mold line cooling with oscillating heat pipes

 propylene glycol/water mixture, non-dielectric coolant for indirect cooling methods

8

Summary and Conclusions

Over the past year the SUSAN concept P&P has been updated

 Engine
 Cruise Assist (distributed propulsion system) : Power extraction at cruise to enable high efficiency distributed 

propulsion concepts

 Take off Assist : Power insertion at high power points to increase engine efficiency

 TEEM control system

 Electrical System

 High efficiency motors, converters, and DC-DC converters added to battery

 Thermal Management system
 Exploration of outer mold line cooling and heat exchanger configurations

 Development of coolant choices for dielectric and non-dielectric for immersion and conventional cooling systems
