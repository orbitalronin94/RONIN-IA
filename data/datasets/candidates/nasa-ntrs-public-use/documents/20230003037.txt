<p>Need for Oxygen Control

<p>• Surface tension of molten metals is affected by even a 
</p>
<p>small amount of adsorption of oxygen

</p>
<p>tension measurements1. 
</p>
<p>• Typically causes a decrease in surface tension

<p>• Oxidation can occur at very low pO2

<p>‒ Has been observed in the MSFC ESL as low as ~10-25

<p>Electrostatic Levitation
</p>
<p>• Electrostatic levitation (ESL) 
</p>
<p>‒ uses Coulomb forces acting on an electrically charged sample
</p>
<p>• Electrostatic field
</p>
<p>‒ generated by six electrodes
</p>
<p>‒ positioned in pairs along three mutually orthogonal lines.
</p>
<p>• Three of the six electrodes are grounded
</p>
<p>‒ other three electrodes are each connected to individual high 
voltage amplifiers. 
</p>
<p>• Provides an unobstructed view of the levitated sample
</p>
<p>6</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Electrostatic Levitation
</p>
<p>• An electrostatic field does not have a three-
</p>
<p>dimensional potential minimum
</p>
<p>‒ Active control is required
</p>
<p>• Sample position measured 
</p>
<p>‒ two dual-axis position sensitive detectors (PSD)
</p>
<p>‒ two collimated light sources, e.g. LED lights.
</p>
<p>• Heating by laser
</p>
<p>‒ decouples the heating from the position control.
