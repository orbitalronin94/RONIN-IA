Conclusions and Forward Work

• Sublimation losses at PSR conditions within 
0.25 wt% requirement:

• 𝑇𝑖 reduced to 77 K 

• 𝑃𝑎𝑚𝑏 reduced to 2.67E-10 Pa

• Forward work:

• Losses at 𝑇𝑖 = 176 K (worst-case regolith 
warming from TRIDENT drill)

• Input sensitivity study

• Collect additional data for model validation

• LN2-chilled sublimation testing

• Darcy’s Constant test stand at high-
vacuum pressures

• MATLAB integration

• Link regolith thermal properties script with 
COMSOL model directly

• Update material properties as water 
sublimates out

• Inlet hoppers for water processing plants
