analysis tool enabling the specification/design of space or 
ground-based classical communication systems:

• Orbital propagator
• Line-of-sight calculations
• Optical power transfer

• Near-field/far-field beam propagation
• Tx/Rx pointing error

• Atmospheric channels
• Attenuation models
• Optical turbulence models

• Noise and background radiance

based quantum communications systems including

• Single-photon sources and entangled pair sources
• Near-field optimized power transfer
• Synchronization and atmospheric photon timing fluctuations
• Photon counting detection including non-linearities (blocking loss, deadtime, 

paralysis) and photon number resolution
• Coincidence rates for simultaneous quantum communication links
• Photonic entanglement distribution links with linear projective Bell state
