<div class="slide-content"><p>Introduction</p>
<p>The Artemis program is currently focusing on exploration sites at the Lunar South Pole (LSP)</p>
<p>Rugged, mountainous, and cratered terrain</p>
<p>Low Sun and Earth angles</p>
<p>Lighting conditions will be challenging</p>
<p>Apollo missions focused on equatorial regions</p>
<p></p>
<p>Digital terrain</p>
<p>Based on 5m per pixel Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data</p>
<p>Enhanced to 20cm per pixel with artificial craters and rocks</p>
<p>Process outlined in Digital Lunar Exploration Sites (DLES)</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Human Eye Response</p>
<p>Human Eye Characteristics:</p>
<p>Uses a combination of mechanisms to adapt</p>
<p>Light to Dark Adaptation</p>
<p>Pupil: few minutes</p>
<p>Cones: 5-10 minutes</p>
<p>Rods: 20-30 minutes</p>
<p>Dark to Light Adaptation  - much faster</p>
<p></p>
<p>Simulated Eye Response:</p>
<p>Auto exposure algorithm with a center weighted pixel sampling</p>
<p>Weighting based on the cosine of the angle off center</p>
<p>Decreased adaptation time vs real world</p>
<p>Light to Dark: 10-20 seconds</p>
<p>Dark to Light: 1-2 seconds</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
