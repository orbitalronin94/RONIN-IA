<p>Water on the Moon
</p>
<p>• 1994 : Clementine indicated the presence 
of water ice at the poles based on the 
response of radar interactions with the 
surface
</p>
<p>• 1998: Lunar Prospector detected hydrogen 
using a neutron spectrometer and 
estimated the content of water ice to be 
300 million metric tons 
</p>
<p>• 2008: Chandrayaan-1 puts the estimate at 
600 million metric tons (240,000 Olympic 
swimming pools)
</p>
<p>• 2009: LRO/LCROSS provided definitive 
proof of water ice in Cabeus cratercrater

<p>Water Electrolysis
</p>
<p>Pros
• Flight heritage (ISS)
• Efficient gas compression
• High operating pressures
• Relatively low operating 
</p>
<p>temperature
</p>
<p>Cons
• Liquid water must be kept above 0o C during lunar night
• Feedwater must be deionized
• Radiator is required to reject waste heat
• Oxygen dryer is required
</p>
<p>Proton Exchange Membrane
Pros
• Does not require deionized feedwater
• Oxygen is dry and pure
• Flight heritage (MOXIE)
• Should be able to survive lunar night with minimal power
• Waste heat can be used to keep stack at operating 
</p>
<p>temperature
</p>
<p>Cons
• Low operating pressure (&lt; 30 psia)
• High operating temperature (~800 o C)
• Output gases are hot and may require more liquefaction power
• Water will need to be vaporized twice, once for extraction and again for 
</p>

3) It will much easier to liquefy and store methane than hydrogen.
