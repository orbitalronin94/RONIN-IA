<p>Fission Surface Power (FSP)
</p>
<p>24
</p>
<p>40 kW power system on lunar rover User I/F Control electronics
Power system
</p>
<p>• NASA and DOE are collaborating on the development of a 40 kWe fission surface power system for a 
</p>
<p>demonstration on the moon by late 2020s with extensibility to Mars missions
</p>
<p>– DOE has designated Idaho National Laboratory to manage development contracts. Los Alamos National 
</p>
<p>Laboratory will provide subject matter expertise for reactor design
</p>
<p>• Develop the system for a 10-year life, support sustainable lunar operations
</p>
<p>• Government reference design technical specs
</p>
<p>– 10,000 kg mass estimate, 250-270 kg/kw
</p>
<p>– System is separated into 3 packages: power system, controllers, and load converter
</p>
<p>– Power conversion: Four - 6 kWe Stirling pairs, sodium heat pipes 
</p>
<p>– 1-3 km power transmission to users. Elevated voltage (2 kV+) proposed. Assessing risks.
</p>
<p>– Nominal thermal power: 250 kWth
</p>
<p>– Heat transfer: sodium vaper heat pipe
</p>

</p>

<p>Our objective is to develop a tether-based power transmission system to provide power 
over several kilometers to serve remote loads. The end-to-end tether power system will 
deliver 100 W – 10 kW of power at above 90 % efficiency and provide communications to:
</p>
<p>• Enable high-power transmission capabilities for nuclear or solar power systems
• Enable rover access to extreme terrain, like lunar craters, pits, caves, and lava tubes
</p>
<p>Applications for the TYMPO system include a number of end-users for the lunar surface and 
other planetary bodies throughout the solar system, such as Mars and Enceladus. Some 
specific end users include:
</p>
<p>• Fission Surface Power and Surface Solar Power • 1 km • 10 kW • Fiber comms
• Moon Diver (Tethered Lunar Pit Descender) • 300 m • 100 W • Copper comms
• EELS (Enceladus Vent Crawler) • 3 km • 200 W • Fiber Comms
• PRIME (Europa Melt Probe) • 50 m • 200 W • Fiber coms
• FARSIDE (tethered telescope array for the Moon) • 12 km, 70 W, Fiber coms
</p>
<p>Develop a stand-alone tether power subsystem that can be integrated into landers, rovers, 
and power transmission systems for numerous lunar applications. The system elements 
below will be developed and raised to TRL 6 over 3 years:
• GaN-Based Modular multilevel converter (MMC) DC-DC converter modules for step up 
</p>
<p>and step down conversion at 1 kW+, 95% efficient from 100 V to 1.5 kV
• A high voltage (500 V - 1.5 kV) tether capable of 100 m – 10 km
• Dual communications system supporting both fiber optic and data over power lines 
</p>
<p>uplink (8 kbps) and downlink (8 mbps) communications for various mission types
</p>
