<p>Static vs Dynamic Assurance
</p>
<p>• Traditional assurance cases are static
</p>
<p>⁃ Design time assurance
</p>
<p>⁃ Establish a level of confidence to approve a 
</p>
<p>system for service
</p>
<p>⁃ Arguments should not be frequently updated
</p>
<p>‣ Argument update after operation → Argument was 
</p>
<p>invalid to begin
</p>
<p>• Dynamic assurance
</p>
<p>⁃ Confirmation in operation that safety / 
</p>
<p>assurance baseline is maintained (or improved)
</p>
<p>⁃ Need a computable notion of assurance
</p>
<p>‣ Safety Architectures
</p>
<p>• (Compositions of) event-chain / barrier models connected to 
</p>
<p>safety targets a.k.a. target level of safety (TLOS)
</p>
<p>• Dynamic Assurance
</p>
<p>‣ Safety Architectures
</p>
<p>• Model for risk assessment
</p>
<p>⁃ The goal is insight, not numbers
</p>
<p>• Linking design to operations
</p>
<p>‣ Runtime risk assessment models
</p>
<p>‣ Safety performance metrics and indicators
</p>
<p>• Relate to event and barrier risk levels
</p>
<p>• Relate to other assurance artifacts
</p>
<p>⁃ Need to define what is updated, when, and how 
</p>
<p>frequently
</p>
<p>‣ During missions (inner loop)
</p>
<p>‣ Between missions (outer loop)
</p>

<p>Dynamic Update to Risk Assessment
</p>
<p>• Pr(c) = p : Allocated event probability 
</p>
<p>⁃ p is an expected upper bound 
</p>
<p>⁃ Equivalent to m observations of event C in N
</p>
<p>missions or simulation runs 
</p>
<p>⁃ Observations of C could be direct or inferred 
</p>
<p>via some metric M(C)
</p>
<p>‣ M(C) = m
</p>
<p>‣ Pr(c) = p = m/N
</p>
<p>• Let metric threshold T = t
</p>
<p>⁃ Number of additional observations of the  
</p>
<p>state of event C to be made in k subsequent 
</p>
<p>missions / simulation runs
</p>
<p>• p ideally constant across all missions
</p>
<p>⁃ m/N = (m + t)/(N + k) → t = k(m/N) →
</p>
<p>t = k p
</p>
<p>• Update rules for events 
</p>
<p>⁃ No updates necessary when observed M(C) ≤ 
</p>
<p>Nearest integer(t)  
</p>
<p>⁃ Otherwise
</p>
<p>‣ Updated Pr(c) = p’ = (m + t)/(N + k)
</p>
<p>‣ Updated T for k’ subsequent runs = t’ = k’ p’
</p>
<p>• Similarly for barriers Bi
</p>
<p>• Estimate residual risk for Pr(e) based on 
</p>
<p>updates to Pr(c) and Pr(bi)
</p>
<p>May 11, 2023 Software Certification Consortium Meeting 21 22</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Risk-based Decision Support
</p>
<p>• Pr(c) and Pr(bi) unchanged or decrease 
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) unchanged or decreases
</p>
<p>‣ Validates risk assessment
</p>
<p>‣ Design may be more conservative than necessary
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases but &lt; TLOS
</p>
<p>‣ Design is incomplete
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increase &gt; TLOS 
</p>
<p>‣ Design is unsafe, cease operation
</p>
<p>• Pr(c) and Pr(bi) both increase
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases but &lt; TLOS 
</p>
<p>‣ Either improve Pr(bi) to reduce Pr(e) to previous level
</p>
<p>‣ Extent of improvement determined from re-allocation of safety target 
</p>
<p>‣ Or Accept risk
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases &gt; TLOS
</p>
<p>‣ Design likely unsafe, cease operation
</p>
<p>‣ Reassess risk and introduce new mitigations / re-design
</p>
<p>• Pr(c) unchanged, Pr(bi) changes
</p>
<p>⁃ Some barriers underperform, some perform as expected, 
</p>
<p>others perform better than expected
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases but &lt; TLOS
</p>
<p>‣ Improve underperforming barriers 
</p>
<p>‣ Re-allocate safety targets fixing integrities of performant barriers
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases &gt; TLOS
</p>
<p>‣ Design likely unsafe; Reassess risk and introduce new mitigations / 
</p>
<p>re-design
</p>
<p>‣ Cease operation 
</p>
<p>• Pr(c) increases, Pr(bi) unchanged
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases but &lt; TLOS
</p>
<p>‣ Either improve Pr(bi) to reduce Pr(e) to previous level, or accept risk
</p>
<p>‣ Extent of improvement determined from re-allocation of safety target
</p>
<p>⁃ Pr(e) increases &gt; TLOS
</p>
<p>‣ Design likely unsafe; Reassess risk and introduce new mitigations / 
</p>
<p>re-design
</p>
