<p>Introduction
</p>
<p>• ISRU for water capture on the moon is 
important for sustained lunar presence
</p>
<p>• Storage of water as a solid provides an 
efficient method of transfer
</p>
<p>• Understanding of low-pressure ice 
formation is critical for efficient storage 
and transport of the ice
</p>
<p>• Generating a thermal model allows for 
optimization of pressure and 
temperature conditions without 
extensive testing
</p>

<p>Conclusions and Future Work
</p>
<p>• Experiments show possibility of an open-gap concept for lunar water 
capture
</p>
<p>• While the current experimental setup is not quite an analog for 
expected lunar conditions, the results will inform the decisions of 
future testing campaigns
</p>
<p>• Another experiment campaign is investigating the influence of the 
regolith pan depth
</p>
