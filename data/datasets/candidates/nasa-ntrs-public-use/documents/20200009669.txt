1. While the SAF correlates with AA, its seasonal maximum in
summer does not energetically line up with maximum surface
warming in winter, therefore cannot directly drive AA
1. While all of the incident summertime radiation is stored in the
ocean, this radiation is not completely cooled in fall/winter as
surface turbulent fluxes are controlled by the atmospheric
conditions and the difference Ts-Ta

We find that the limitations on winter
surface cooling in ice-retreat regions is
responsible for the model spread in AA

• Increased HFLUX corresponds to more LWCS
• Offsetting behavior between HSTOR and HFLUX- in regions of
small ΔTs, HFLUX cools all heat stored in the ocean. In regions
with greater ΔTs, cooling of the surface by S+L cannot keep up
with the increased HSTOR
The largest warming and model spread occurs in HSTOR and HFLUX
in the Barents Sea; processes operating in this region that control
HSTOR and HFLUX are prime candidates to constrain AA
