<div class="slide-content"><p>1. Concept Documentation (1/2)</p>
<p>17</p>
<p>Artifact(s):</p>
<p>20230712 AIF - MPH-MCR Concept Study Team Outbrief</p>
<p>Draft MPH-CONOP-001 MPH Concept of Operations</p>
<p>Draft MPH Baseball Card (BBC) - Submitted to inform LAT SAC23 Trades on 03/20/2023 </p>
<p>A joint NASA and ASI/TASI MCR/MDR Reference Concept Definition Team successfully conducted a 6wk study that evaluated and identified a technically feasible solution to meet mission/project needs</p>
<p>NASA’s draft MPH-CONOP-001, MPH Concept of Operations, and MPH BBC reflect the outputs of the joint study and describes the overall high-level concept of how the system will be used to meet stakeholder expectations</p>
<p></p>
<p>Per NASA Systems Engineering Handbook - </p>
<p></p>
<p>Concept of Operations (ConOps) (Concept Documentation): Developed early in Pre-Phase A, the ConOps describes the overall high-level concept of how the system will be used to meet stakeholder expectations, usually in a time-sequenced manner. It describes the system from an operational perspective and helps facilitate an understanding of the system goals. It stimulates the development of the requirements and architecture related to the user elements of the system. It serves as the basis for subsequent definition documents and provides the foundation for the long-range operational planning activities.</p>
