<p>ABSTRACT
</p>
<p>Electroluminescence (EL) imaging can be
</p>
<p>used as a quantitative characterization method
</p>
<p>for solar cell performance when combined with
</p>
<p>image processing, allowing for the impact of
</p>
<p>dust grain size on electroluminescence
</p>
<p>imaging characteristics to be investigated.
</p>
<p>Simulating these results via modelling can help
</p>
<p>to predict what dust grain sizes, if any, will
</p>
<p>have a greater impact on performance.
</p>
