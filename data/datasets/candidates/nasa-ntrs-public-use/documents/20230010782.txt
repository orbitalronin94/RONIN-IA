<p>SAGE III Contamination Monitoring
</p>
<p>• Why does SAGE III monitor contamination?
</p>
<p>• The instrument optics are susceptible and sensitive to 
contamination.
</p>
<p>• The goal of the Contamination Monitoring Packages (CMPs) is to aid 
in efforts to minimize contamination impacts to the payload and by 
extension the science data product.
</p>
<p>• The instrument contamination window can be closed to protect the 
telescope.
</p>
<p>• The flight computer has the ability to automatically close the 
contamination window if necessary.
</p>
<p>• The window can be manually commanded to close.
</p>
<p>• CMP measurements aid the SAGE III team in determining where to stow 
the contamination window for minimal exposure to contamination 
sources.</p>

<p>Summary
• SAGE datasets are notable for their inherent stability derived from the occultation 
</p>
<p>technique.
</p>
<p>• SAGE uniquely provides geophysical reference standards for vertically resolved 
stratospheric concentrations of ozone, water vapor, nitrogen dioxide, and multi-
wavelength aerosol extinction coefficient.
</p>
<p>• SAGE III/ISS is in an excellent position to extend the uniquely valuable science 
records throughout this decade, improve their quality and bridge to future 
missions.
</p>
<p>• SAGE III/ISS is manifested on ELC4 through 2030
</p>
<p>• Over 60 published papers using SAGE III/ISS science data products
</p>
<p>• Continued monitoring and sharing of the ISS external contamination data to the 
ISS community</p>
