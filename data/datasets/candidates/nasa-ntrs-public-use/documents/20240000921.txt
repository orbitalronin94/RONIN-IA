<p>Sensor-based Prognostics to Avoid Runaway Reactions &amp; Catastrophic 
Ignition (SPARRCI)
Ensuring Safe, Reliable Power for Electric Aircraft
Challenge
- Electric aircraft concepts require high power and high 
</p>
<p>energy storage, but they must also be SAFE!
- State-of-the-art batteries suffer from catastrophic 
</p>
<p>failures, resulting in over-engineered, heavy failure 
solutions to provide safer energy storage 
</p>
<p>- Existing solutions aim to contain or prevent thermal 
runaway propagation at a battery level, but single-cell 
events cannot be predicted
</p>
<p>Turning a chemistry into a cell, packaged 
battery, and safe battery adds layers of mass
</p>
<p>Embedded sensors provide higher quality data 
to improve fidelity of off-nominal models 
</p>
<p>Expected Impacts
- With early detection of failure, we ensure 
catastrophic failures are eliminated and the result is a 
safe, higher energy battery to enable next-generation   
electric aircraft concepts
- Next-generation battery chemistry development 
</p>
<p>occurs on a long timeline and may offer higher 
specific energy but not additional safety
</p>
<p>- SPARRCI offers a shorter timeline to implement new 
chemistries with a better understanding of safety
</p>
<p>Partners and/or  Participants
- NASA Glenn Research Center, batteries/sensors 
- NASA Langley Research Center, NDE
- NASA Ames Research Center, modeling &amp; 
</p>
<p>prognostics
- Cornerstone Research Group, small business 
</p>
<p>partner
</p>
<p>Solutions
- SPARRCI offers a multi-disciplinary approach combining 
</p>
<p>battery failure analysis, nondestructive evaluation (NDE), 
sensor development, and modeling &amp; prognostics for early 
failure detection
</p>
<p>- Using embedded sensor and NDE data fed into advanced 
off-nominal models, failures can be predicted based on 
changes in cell function or material morphology
</p>
<p>- Enabling for higher energy, safer aircraft batteries for 
existing chemistries and shortening the timeline for future 
development
</p>
<p>Results
- Using shadow mask and photolithography, sensors are     
printed onto battery materials &amp; provide valuable internal data   
when embedded in pouch cells
- NDE techniques such as digital radiography and ultrasound, 
and laser doppler vibrometry can detect material defects
- Advances to battery performance models predict off-nominal 
behavior
</p>
<p>Battery failure analysis pairs with UT NDE 
techniques, showing sensitivity to defects
</p>
<p>SPARRCI offers decreased weight, increased 
safety, and improved aircraft performance
</p>
<p>Next Steps
-Demonstrate benefit of data from combined sensor + NDE    
techniques during nominal and off-nominal cell behavior
- Integrate concepts into battery pack-level NASA projects
- Address optimization of sensor connections, data 
</p>
<p>collection, and modeling/prognostics fidelity
</p>
