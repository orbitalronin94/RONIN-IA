<p>Conclusions
• Spectroscopy can be used to first order constrain age of surface features
</p>
<p>• However, it cannot be used for exact ages
• Surface of Venus should react quickly with the atmosphere
</p>
<p>• Iron oxides and Sulfates
• Consistent with surface coloration – Pieters et al. (1986)
• High S content of Vega 2 analysis
</p>
<p>• Lava flows at Idunn Mons, and possibly other volcanoes are likely young
• Years to decades old (oxidation only exp)
• Decades to thousands of years (CO2 only exp)
• Possibly much younger (Experiments with S)
</p>
<p>• Future missions should investigate young volcanic rises for changes
• D’Incecco et al. (2021 SSR)</p>
