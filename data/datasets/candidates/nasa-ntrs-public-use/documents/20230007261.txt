<div class="slide-content"><p>Introduction</p>
<p>2</p>
<p>The Space Launch System (SLS) Program computes rollout loads using measured base drive interface accelerations as the forcing function</p>
<p>Measured interface accelerations between Crawler Transporter (CT) and Mobile Launcher (ML) used as an enforced acceleration in the analysis of the SLS ML and vehicle model</p>
<p>This data is from the Shuttle program, Ares program, and early ML only rollout tests</p>
<p>CT speed placard of  instituted to minimize loads</p>
<p>Rollout set the design load on the Multi-Purpose Crew Vehicle (MPCV) for some primary structure loads (section loads) and for the Vehicle Stabilizer (VS) tangent load</p>
<p>The Dynamic Rollout Test (DRT) was the first rollout with the SLS vehicle</p>
<p>New configuration not represented in base drive data used for design analysis</p>
<p>Significantly more weight on the CT than existing data</p>
<p>The item being carried could have impact on characteristics of base drive accelerations</p>
<p>Real-Time Monitoring (RTM) was recommended for the DRT to verify that loads remained below design values during rollout</p>
<p>Concern with possibility of high loads exceeding design limits</p>
<p>Tuned nature of loads and high sensitivity of loads to CT speed</p>
<p>Uncertainty in rollout forcing functions</p>
<p>Lack of experience in performance of Vehicle Stabilizer (VS) </p>
<p>Verify adequacy of CT speed placard given model and forcing function uncertainty</p>
<p>Opportunity, sensors associated with Integrated Modal Test (IMT) made RTM a feasible option</p>
<p>This presentation provides an overview of the RTM process</p>
<p>MSFC-EV31-SRH-20180213</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Measurement Requirements</p>
<p>9</p>
<p>The PEQ load was estimated using acceleration and anemometer data obtained real-time during rollout</p>
<p>1 tri-axial accelerometer at station </p>
<p>2 bi-axial accelerometers at station </p>
<p>3 tri-axial accelerometers at station </p>
<p>Anemometer measurement at top of ML</p>
<p>Additional recoveries included VS tangent strut loads and CT-ML interface accelerations</p>
<p>These are direct measurements, no indirect estimations are required</p>
<p>Some processing was applied</p>
<p>Filtering to remove noise or to focus on lower frequencies</p>
<p>Rotations or sign changes to account for as-installed orientation</p>
<p>MATLAB function created to return PEQ estimate from measured acceleration data combined with measured winds</p>
<p>Includes multiple redundancies for accelerometer locations</p>
<p>Includes error checking and compensation for missing channels</p>
<p>Primary transducer for wind load is anemometer data but tower strain gauge data included as a backup to anemometer</p>
<p>Load estimate can compensate for missing / dead channels</p>
<p>Flag used to adjust load estimate for missing channels</p>
<p>Redundant accelerometers at sensor locations averaged or omitted depending on availability</p>

<div class="slide-content"><p>Summary</p>
<p>15</p>
<p>An indirect real time metric was used to estimate MPCV loads</p>
<p>Acceleration at LAS shows good correlation with MPCV PEQ loads</p>
<p>Signal processing used to combine accelerations from multiple locations to estimate CG acceleration and lower load projection sensitivity to model errors.</p>
<p></p>
<p>VS tangent loads were measured directly</p>
<p></p>
<p>Metrics were based on peak load</p>
<p>Monitored to ensure PEQ and VS loads stay below enveloped design loads</p>
<p>Did not directly take into account fatigue/fracture concerns, but was consistent with provided time histories for fatigue</p>
<p></p>
<p>While there were some minor data acquisition and software challenges, the effort was largely successful</p>
<p>The ability to monitor vehicle response during rollout in real-time was established</p>
<p>Processes for modifying rollout speed or even halting the CT can be implemented if future needs dictate</p>
<p>Load estimates were largely in the expected range with minor exceedances</p>
<p></p>
<p>Post rollout reconstruction </p>
<p>Simulations are accurate to within range of design uncertainty values</p>
<p>Observed loads can be better recreated in simulations with minor modeling updates.</p>
<p>VS stiffness increased and VS damping reduced for low amplitude responses</p>
<p>Post-IMT ML model update implemented</p>
