<p>Cislunar Autonomous Positioning System Technology Operations and Navigation Experiment</p>
<p>CAPSTONE Spacecraft</p>
<p>Image Credit: NASA</p>
<p>Objectives: Demonstrate how to enter and function in a near rectilinear halo orbit around the Moon and demonstrate spacecraft-to-spacecraft navigation.</p>
<p>Timeline: </p>
<p>Launched: June 28, 2022</p>
<p>Arrived at Lunar Orbit: November 13, 2022</p>
<p>First demonstration of two-way ranging with LRO: October 14, 2023</p>
<p><a href="https://eyes.nasa.gov/apps/orrery/#/sc_capstone?time=2023-12-05T06:42:42.933+00:00">CAPSTONE in NASA’s Eyes Demonstration</a></p>
<p>Spacecraft and Lunar Orbit: </p>
<p>Size: 12-unit CubeSat platform</p>
<p>Dimensions and Weight:</p>
<p>Lunar Orbit: Within 1000 miles of one lunar pole on its near pass and 43,500 miles from the other pole at its peak, every seven days</p>
