<p>18
</p>
<p>Conclusions
</p>
<p>• Each design is different and should be validated 
</p>
<p>• Silicone gel can be an effective potting material for 
</p>
<p>fragile MANCs under DO-160G vibrations
</p>
<p>• Softer mounts can shift vibrations to lower 
</p>
<p>frequency in neighboring components
</p>
<p>• Design Tradeoffs: 
</p>
<p>– high/low thermal and electrical conductivity
</p>
<p>– Soft/rigid mounting
