<p>Flow Modeling and Radiation
</p>
<p>• LAURA hypersonic CFD code 
used for all simulations 
</p>
<p>• HARA radiation transport code 
is used to compute spectral 
radiance 
</p>
<p>• Vaporization of the meteoroid 
modeled using steady-state 
equilibrium assumption 
</p>
<p>• Melting not modeled 
• Details on the computational 
</p>
<p>approach can be found in 
Johnston et al., Icarus, 2018</p>

<p>Benesov Validation Study
</p>
<p>‣ Computational approach produces detailed 
spectra over an arbitrary wavelength range

</p>
<p>‣ The Benesov bolide, which occurred over the 
Czech Republic on May 7, 1991, is one of the few 
meter class events for which detailed spectra 
were captured

➡ Provides an invaluable source of validation data for 
</p>
<p>entry models being applied to impact assessments
</p>
<p>Borovicka &amp; Spurny, 1996
</p>
<p>‣ Detailed computations were performed for a 
0.8m sphere

➡ Velocity = 20 km/s

➡ Altitude = 47 and 57 km

➡ LL chondrite elemental composition</p>

</p>

<p>‣ Simulated spectra able to capture

features from the observation

<p>11
</p>
<p>State-of-the-Art
</p>
<p>‣Flow Modeling and Radiation (“The Good”)  
➡ “Tipping point” achieved in our predictive capability for radiation and luminosity

➡ Modeling approach can now be applied to improving our reduced order models for meteoroid luminous 
</p>
<p>efficiency 

➡ Can also be deployed in the design of future bolide observing campaigns

</p>
<p>‣Ablation Modeling and Experiments (“The Promising”)  
➡ Significant progress has been made on high-fidelity, first principles ablation models

➡ Preliminary validation studies have been performed using novel high-enthalpy experiments

➡ On-going effort to synthesize this work into reduced-order models for impact risk assessments

</p>
<p>‣Fragmentation and Break-up (“…and the Ugly”) 
➡ Remains the most challenging aspect of the asteroid entry problem

➡ Tuned observational models, and nascent physics-based approaches do not converge

➡ Current effort underway to utilize free-flight hypersonic wind tunnel experiments to aid in model development 
</p>
<p>(The good, the promising, and the ugly)</p>
