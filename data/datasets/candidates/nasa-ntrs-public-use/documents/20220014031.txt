National Aeronautics and Space Administration 26

Closing Comments 
Foundational to ICME and thus material innovation is the linkage of real and virtual data in which materials processing, 
microstructure, properties, and performance are coupled to enable application-driven design and optimization of materials 
and structures!

Herein we have Demonstrated
• A robust information management schema – based on the ANSYS GRANTA MI system - that can be utilized as an ICME 

Platform
• Enables the capture, analysis, dissemination and maintenance of various types of data for all materials.
• New additions – Microstructure, Model Pedigree Tables (physics-based or data driven), Software Tools Table and Application Table
• Facilitates the characterization and uncertainty quantifications of model input parameters, verification and validation of model output, and 

certification of toolsets at multiple length scales
• Accepted and being implemented/tested by MDMC members

• GRANTA MI can handle the demands of integrating both virtual and experimental data 
• Achieved linkage of virtual and experimental data at two length scales (micro and macroscale)

• Seamless integration of in-house simulation toolsets (e.g., MAC/GMC) and workflow engines (e.g., Matlab) with GRANTA 
MI via newly created Python GUI and API functions as well as third party tools

Moving forward, NASA GRC is working on
• Establishment of the final link -- an Application Table 

• Enables linkage of structural application (performance) requirements with the “design of material” problem
• Provides location for BOM and summary information for application requirements
• Enables unique location to link CAD/PLM/SLM information to material information

• Integration of optimization algorithm(s) to provide final linkage of processing to performance criteria; thus, making 
ICME a reality.
