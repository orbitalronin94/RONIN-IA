<p>ML for Automatic Flaw Detection in AFP
Goals:
</p>
<p>• Automated highlighting of areas where 
further inspection is required. 
</p>
<p>Challenges
</p>
<p>• Other image analysis method performed 
poorly (e.g. edge detection, clustering).
</p>
<p>• Thermal discontinuities are not uniform in 
size, shape, or any characteristic.
</p>
<p>• Thermography is, by nature a diffusive 
process, meaning discontinuities lack 
defined edges.
</p>
<p>• “Clean” data with clear flaws that can be 
used for training are difficult to come by. 
</p>
<p>ML Approach:
</p>
<p>• Manually segmented images to make 
a training dataset. 
</p>
<p>• Trained a UNet to segment thermal 
images for manufacturing flaws. 
</p>
