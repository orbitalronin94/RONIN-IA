<p>Thermal Protection System (TPS)
</p>
<p>• The TPS insulates vehicles from the extreme heat of entry
</p>
<p>• Two main classes of TPS materials:
</p>
<p>• Ablative – materials that ablate and decompose during entry to 
dissipate energy
• Wide range of densities ( 0.2 to 1.8 g/cc) but can handle much higher heat 
</p>
<p>fluxes and heat loads 
</p>
<p>• Examples – PICA, Avcoat, HEEET, 3MDCP
</p>
<p>• Reusable – materials that do not degrade or change during 
aerothermal heating 
• Low density ceramic fiber-based materials with low thermal conductivity
</p>
<p>• Examples – Shuttle tile (HRSI), AFRSI blanket, TUFROC
</p>

<p>Base Heat Shield
</p>
<p>• Consists of borosilicate glass (B2O3/SiO2), 
silicon boride (SiBx), and molybdenum 
disilicide (MoSi2) 
</p>
<p>• Produces a stronger, tougher silica tile but at 
the cost of increased mass
</p>
<p>• Standard TUFI tiles were used on the Shuttle 
Orbiter's underside. White variants with 
higher impact resistance and conductivity 
were used on the upper body.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Coatings
</p>
<p>• RCG coatings sit on top of the 
substrate &amp; partially seal surface
</p>
<p>• TUFI surface treatments 
penetrate the substrate (~ 0.1”) 
&amp; add toughness
</p>
<p>• Coatings &amp; surface treatments 
share some goals:
</p>
<p>- high temp. stability
</p>
<p>- high emissivity (≥0.9)
</p>
<p>- low catalycity (exothermic atom 
recombination)
</p>
<p>- mechanically stable as part of 
the system (e.g., no thermal 
expansion mismatch)
</p>

<p>Current Efforts 
</p>
<p>• Coated AETB tile is made with heritage raw materials using processes 
and equipment that have not been updated since invention and are 
no longer available for purchase 
</p>
<p>• Current efforts focus on modernization of legacy processes using 
heritage materials to make contemporary versions of AETB tile with 
TUFI and RCG coating
</p>

<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>TUFROC
</p>
<p>• “Toughened Uni-piece Fibrous Reinforced 
Oxidation-Resistant Composite”
</p>
<p>• A multi-component tiled TPS system that is the state-of-
the-art reusable material system used on the leading edges 
of X-37B 
</p>
<p>• Features (vs. C/C or C/SiC)
• Low cost (10x cheaper than C/C)
</p>
<p>• Light weight (~0.3 g/cm3)
</p>
<p>• Insulative
</p>
<p>• Reusable temperature 2900oF (≥3, 5min exposures)
</p>
<p>• Single-use temperature exceeding 3100oF
</p>
