<p>Conclusion
• Research motor drive developed for high power fixed-wing electrified 
</p>
<p>aircraft
• 250 kW, 1 kV, 9.8 kW/kg, 99.3%* efficiency, 1.4%* THD (*simulated)
</p>
<p>• Altitude capable up to 40 kft
• Compatible with NEAT testbed
• Explores advanced topological choices – multilevel and interleaving
</p>
<p>• Successful demonstration
• Full power testing in coming months
</p>
<p>• Future work in improving stator current quality
• Resonant inverter
• High switching frequency allows for low mass filtering
• Enabling for 1.4 MW HEMM (cryocooled rotor)</p>
