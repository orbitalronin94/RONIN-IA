<p>What are aerogels?</p>
<p>Made by removing solvent from wet gels without collapsing the structure</p>
<p>Highly porous solids with extremely small pore size </p>
<p>Low density </p>
<p>High surface area</p>
<p>Low thermal conductivity</p>
<p>reduces heat transfer (convection, conduction, and radiation)</p>

<p>Summary</p>
<p></p>
<p>Aluminosilicate aerogels</p>
<p>•Aluminosilicate aerogels can be used up to 1100oC.  </p>
<p>•Their composites are not flakey and can be flexible.  </p>
<p>•The aerogel and their composites can be modified to be hydrophobic.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Yttria and/or Ytterbia stabilized Zirconia aerogels</p>
<p>•The surface areas of the as dried aerogels depend on the total dopant level and water ratio, but no relationship with Y to Yb ratio.</p>
<p>•With increase dopant level, the pore size increases up to 20% dopant, and surface area decreases with the increased dopant.</p>
<p>•Dopant level and the choice of dopant can affect the phase change and crystalline size growth</p>
<p>•Increasing dopant above 20% can avoid the phase transformation and suppress the crystalline growth at 1100oC</p>
<p>•SiO2  coating can effectively suppress the crystalline growth and phase change.</p>
