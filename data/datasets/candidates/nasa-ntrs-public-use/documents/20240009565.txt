<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>• Parametric modeling approach was developed to evaluate impact of MW-class EAP systems on 
vehicle-level performance for the TPH C-130H concept:
</p>
<p>• Engineering-level models and mission profiles were created along with integrated approach to battery 
sizing and power management
</p>
<p>• Based on payload, 2030-2050 EAP technology levels &amp; cruise mode of operation:
</p>
<p>• Motor @ NRP: 200—800 NM &amp; 40-60% fuel savings [Maximize Fuel Savings]
</p>
<p>• Turbine @ NRP: 600–1,000 NM &amp; 25-35% fuel savings [Maximize Range]
</p>
<p>• Key design constraints identified were battery and electric motor sizing:
</p>
<p>• Trade-off between range performance and fuel-savings; increasing electric power draw results in larger 
battery size, off-loading payload allows for increased fuel/battery capacity
</p>
<p>• Battery specific energy density improvements lead to simultaneous range/fuel savings improvements 
compared to baseline C-130H
</p>
<p>• Insufficient motor power affects climb requirements and runway length (≥1.82 MW for TPH C-130H 
configuation) and insufficient battery capacity constrains range and cruise altitude (min. ~3.25 MWh)
</p>

revolutionary technology strategy to meet aggressive 
sustainability targets
</p>
<p>• Through the Electrified Powertrain Flight Demonstration 
(EPFD) Project, NASA collaborates with U.S. industry 
partners to:
</p>

<p>Further Background

<p>Vehicle-Level Impacts of 
</p>
<p>Aircraft Electrification
</p>
<p>Advantages:
</p>
<p>• Reduced fuel consumption → decreased CO2 &amp; 
</p>
<p>GHG emissions for short-haul missions
</p>
<p>• EAP conversion efficiency (91.3%) higher than 
</p>
<p>conventional gas turbine with Jet Fuel A (30%)
</p>
<p>Disadvantages:
</p>
<p>• Current pack-level battery specific energy 
</p>
<p>density (~250 Wh/kg) 48x lower than Jet Fuel A 
</p>
<p>(12,000 Wh/kg) → reduced payload and range 
</p>
<p>capabilities
</p>
<p>• EAP concept feasibility limited by current 
</p>
<p>electrical system technology levels in terms of 
</p>
<p>power density</p>
<p></p>
