<p>Reflectivity Control Devices (RCDs)
</p>
<p>• Reflectivity Control Devices are thin film membranes that can change 
reflective properties when a voltage is applied to them
</p>
<p>• They essentially consist of sail material layers with a Liquid Crystal 
Diode (LCD) layer in between
</p>
<p>• They also include a layer to enable application of the voltage
</p>
<p>• They are intended to be used for control of solar sails
</p>
<p>• RCDs flew successfully on the JAXA IKAROS in 2010
</p>
<p>2</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>RCD Control of a Solar Sail
</p>
<p>• The RCD can be placed near the tip of a solar sail boom for optimal 
moment arm
</p>
<p>• For solar cruiser, we intend to use them for Roll control (axis normal 
to sail) only, thus they are canted wrt to the sail surface
</p>
<p>• Initially the top and bottom layers of the sail were intended to be 
clear, but analysis showed a reflective bottom layer was more 
efficient at providing Roll torque
</p>
<p>3</p>
<p></p>
</div>

<p>Trades for Solar Cruiser RCDs
</p>
<p>• The following parameters were primary drivers in the trade space:
- Area of RCDs
</p>
<p>- Tent angle (angle wrt to sail normal surface)
</p>
<p>- Voltage applied to RCDs
</p>
<p>- Thickness of material (i.e., mass of RCDs)
</p>
<p>• RCDs also designed with 125% margin
</p>

<p>RCDs Trade Well vs. Other Actuators
</p>
<p>• Magnetic Torque rods do not work in deep space
</p>
<p>• Moving masses require moving parts and weigh more
</p>
<p>• Power requirements for RCDs are relatively modest compared to 
other actuators
</p>
<p>• RCDs have much lower mass than other actuators
</p>
<p>8</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>Conclusions and Future Work
</p>
<p>• We have made great progress in developing RCDs for use on Solar 
Cruiser and other future solar sail missions
</p>
<p>• Work remains to further develop RCDs to survive in the harsh space 
environment, but progress has been made in this area as well
</p>
<p>• For Solar Cruiser and future solar sail missions, RCDs offer great 
promise as low mass and reasonable power requirements trade well 
compared to other Attitude Control System (ACS) solutions, 
particularly for Roll control
</p>
