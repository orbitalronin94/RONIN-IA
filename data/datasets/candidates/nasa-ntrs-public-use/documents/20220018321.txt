HRP Science Highlights

o rHEALTH TECH DEMO PERFORMED ON ISS MAY 13 AND 16 – SAMPLES RETURNED SPACEX-25.

o Human Exploration Research Analog (HERA) INTERNATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
o ROI hosted representatives from Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Center (MBRSC) during pre-mission 

training and BDC activities for HERA C6M4 who shadowed ROI personnel.  MBRSC personnel will 
return for in-mission observations in 2023.

o ROI is assessing MBRSC and ESA science proposals for inclusion in HERA C7. Additionally, MBRSC 
will provide crewmembers for C7 and participate in C7 integration activities to help inform the 
development of their own isolation and confinement analog.

rHEALTH (or reusable Handheld Electrolyte and Lab 
Technology for Humans) is a miniature flow cytometer for 
biomarker identification to assess biological changes in 
space and a medical analyzer designed for diagnosing and 
monitoring spaceflight medical conditions such as blood 
clots, kidney stones, radiation exposure, and other illnesses 
and injuries.

HRP Science Highlights

o HSIA Usability Scale – Crew feedback used to improve usability 
scale for evaluating interfaces. Required by Gateway and HLS 
programs to aid provider development testing. (Holden)

o Lighting for Spaceflight  – Updated NASA Standards document to 
reflect expert lighting recommendation to support circadian health 
and well-being (Brown et al., 2022)

o Extended Mission Duration in HERA – Publication highlights results from either, 
45-day HERA Missions with 32 subjects. Mission duration had significant effect 
on spaceflight operational performance. Accuracy peaks mid-late quartiles, 
then degrades to baseline; however, workload decreased linearly, consistent 
with task learning over a campaign. (Duda, Draper)) 

o Artemis Prep: Microgravity Validation of Dry Saliva, Dry Blood 
Collection Protocols (Immune Biomarkers Study, PI: Crucian) – New 
methodology validated in parabolic flight

o Kraken - Navy motion simulator to explore and mitigate HLS landing 
risks and test sensorimotor countermeasures for landing, egress and 
early EVA’s.
