Technology Drives Exploration 11

Innovation: Leveraging Stochastic Reduced Order Models (SROMs)

SROMs can be viewed as a smart Monte Carlo simulation method

• Monte Carlo – thousands or millions of random, equally-likely samples to quantify effects of uncertainty

Structural 
Simulation

• SROMs – a small number of manually selected/weighted samples

Preprocessing 

Optimization
Structural 
Simulation

➢ Efficient – Fewer model evaluations required; computational speedup ≈ 𝑁/𝑚
➢ Practical - Non-intrusive; easily combined with existing codes; get optimal solution for your resources
➢ Accurate - Good agreement with MCS; proven asymptotic convergence



Technology Drives Exploration 12

IRAD: Safe and Reliable Design Under Uncertainty for AM

➢ Goal: Deliver a high-performance, uncertainty-aware design tool for creating reliable, 
next-gen structural concepts 

➢ Approach: Integrate state-of-the-art uncertainty quantification (UQ) algorithms from 
NASA1 into massively-parallel design optimization software from Sandia2

1. Stochastic reduced order models (https://github.com/nasa/srompy) ;  
2. Plato – Sandia optimization-based design (https://www.sandia.gov/plato3d/index.html)  

➢ Two pronged development strategy:

1) Fundamental research collaboration 2) Software development partnership

• Formulation of a novel method for efficient and 
accurate optimization under uncertainty

• Goal: Publish new methodology in peer-reviewed 
journals; Present findings at relevant conferences

• Migration of prototype research codes into 
production software for large scale problems 

• Goal: Provide access to design under uncertainty 
capabilities to both NASA & Sandia

Problem and Impact to NASA

Future aeronautics paradigms demand new 
lightweight structural concepts that cannot be 
fabricated with conventional manufacturing

➢ On-demand mobility (ODM) vehicles

➢ Supersonic & civil aviation transport

Additive manufacturing (AM) promises to fill this 
void, but advanced design software is needed 
to to produce reliable & safe components

Existing design tools ignore uncertainties, 
resulting in AM components that are at risk of 
failure when margins are narrow

Innovation
Integrating state-of-the-art uncertainty quantification

(UQ) algorithms from NASA into massively-parallel

design optimization software from Sandia

➢ Stochastic reduced order models (SROMs)1 for fast &

accurate UQ throughout the design process

➢ GPU-accelerated topology optimization2 for near real-

time design optimization of large scale problems

Goal/Objective
• Goal: to deliver a high-performance,

uncertainty-aware computational design tool
for creating reliable, next-gen structural
concepts

• Allow for uncertainty to be controlled & 
reduced throughout the design process 
rather than quantified afterwards or ignored 
altogether
