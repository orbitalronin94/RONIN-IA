<div class="slide-content"><p>Introduction
</p>
<p>4 July 2023</p>
<p>3</p>
<p>ISRU technology is critical for NASA’s future human spaceflight efforts [1,2].</p>
<p>NASA is developing technology to produce cryogenic propellants on the Lunar and Martian surfaces. </p>
<p>ISRU (in-situ resources utilization) techniques collect resources in gaseous form, and they must be purified and condensed to be used as propellant.</p>
<p>Liquefaction is a combination of convection and condensation processes. Both processes must be modeled in detail. </p>
<p>Objectives:</p>
<p>1) to develop a model for the liquefaction process of gaseous propellant inside a cryogenically refrigerated elliptical tank;</p>
<p>2) to enhance capabilities of engineers for design, analysis, and evaluation of technology advancements for ISRU propellant and liquefaction processes.</p>
<p></p>

<p>Cylindrical tank with ellipsoidal top and bottom sections. </p>
<p>Assumptions: </p>
<p>Constant mass inflow rate of pure GN2 at constant pressure and temperature.</p>
<p>Ullage gas is pure GN2.</p>
<p>Tank walls are refrigerated uniformly at a constant temperature below saturation of the ullage gas.</p>
<p>Condensed liquid is collected at the bottom of tank.</p>
<p>Earth gravity environment.</p>
<p>Specified ellipsoidal caps for top and bottom segments.</p>
<p>Filmwise condensation is the only mode of condensation on the tank surfaces.</p>
<p>Condensation is modeled using commonly-applied method of considering liquid and gas side thermal resistances as described in Ghiaasiaan[3]</p>

<p>The model predictions were compared with the data from LN2 CryoFILL test data for low, mid, and high fill level cases.</p>
<p>Liquefaction model increases in accuracy with higher fill levels. </p>
<p>This model can be highly useful scoping calculations and predicting liquefaction rates for a variety of tank geometries and operating conditions.</p>
