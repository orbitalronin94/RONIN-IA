<p>Motivation
</p>
<p>3
</p>
<p>The NASA CERES project provides over 22 years of observed TOA fluxes and a suite
of datasets that include surface fluxes and cloud properties on different spatial and
temporal scales.
</p>
<p>The Ed4 FluxByCldTyp data product is a new dataset that provides radiative fluxes as a
function of cloud pressure layer and optical depth bin (ISCCP-like) cloud types for
climate studies
</p>
<p>As part of the effort to continue to improve accuracy and consistency of the
FluxByCldTyp fluxes, we used machine learning technique to calculate CERES sub-
footprint fluxes from MODIS narrowband radiances within mixed scene satellite
footprints
</p>
<p>I will present preliminary results which show improvement over the current Ed4
algorithm that will be eventually become part of Ed5</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="page"><p></p>
<p>The CERES Project
</p>
<p>4
</p>
<p>I. The Clouds and the Earth Radiant Energy System (CERES) instruments were
first aboard TRMM satellite launched in December 1997 and currently on
Terra, Aqua, NPP and NOAA20 polar orbiters.
</p>
<p>II. CERES produces flux data products based on radiances measured in three
bands.
</p>
<p>• SW (0.3-5μm)
• IR Window (8-12μm)
• Total channel (0.3-200μm)
</p>
<p>LW = Total Channel – SW
</p>
<p>III. Cloud properties are retrieved from the onboard imagers (MODIS or VIIRS)
measurements on the same satellite to assist CERES radiance to flux
conversion
</p>
<p>IV. CERES produces a suite of datasets of different spatial and temporal scales
(https://ceres.larc.nasa.gov/)</p>

<p>Summary and Future work
</p>
<p>• The DNN_rad/ADM and DNN_flux are developed to improve fluxes 
in FluxByCldTyp product. They both show improvement over Ed4.1
</p>
<p>• The two methods give about the same results. DNN_flux is 
slightly better. 
</p>
<p>• The DNN_flux method does not require the CERES ADM, thus 
greatly reduce code complexity.
</p>
<p>• DNN will be incorporated in to FluxByCldTyp Ed5.
• Further improvement involves including more MODIS NB channels. </p>
