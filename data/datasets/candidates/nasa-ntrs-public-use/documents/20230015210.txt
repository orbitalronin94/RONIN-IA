<p>eVTOL Aircraft Modeling Overview
</p>
<p>• Conventional methods (e.g., one-factor-at-a-time) fail to efficiently capture 
</p>
<p>the complexity and interactions present in eVTOL aircraft designs
</p>
<p>• Several new techniques have been developed for rapid, accurate, 
</p>
<p>statistically-rigorous full-envelope eVTOL aircraft aero-propulsive modeling
</p>
<p>• Response surface representations of complex aerodynamics
</p>
<p>• Applicable throughout the design process enabling rapid aircraft realization
</p>

<p>Concluding Remarks
</p>
<p>• eVTOL aircraft present new aero-propulsive modeling challenges 
</p>
<p>• Multiple efficient experiment design and aero-propulsive modeling 
strategies for eVTOL aircraft have been successfully demonstrated
</p>
<p>• Significant progress in modeling approaches for complex aircraft
</p>
<p>• Active research is further refining eVTOL vehicle modeling techniques
</p>
<p>• Techniques can be applied for many current and future eVTOL vehicles
</p>
<p>• Strong emphasis on public release of eVTOL aircraft data and technology
</p>
